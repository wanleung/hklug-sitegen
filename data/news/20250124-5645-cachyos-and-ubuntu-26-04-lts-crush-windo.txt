Title: CachyOS and Ubuntu 26.04 LTS Crush Windows 11 Performance on Budget Wildcat Lake Hardware
Content:
<p>Most operating system benchmarks focus on flagship hardware. A new Phoronix comparison takes the opposite approach, pitting Windows 11, Ubuntu 26.04 LTS, and the performance-tuned CachyOS against each other on an entry-level CHUWI laptop priced at $449. According to the report, this device pairs an Intel Core 3 Wildcat Lake processor with 8GB of RAM — a configuration that reveals how dramatically OS choice can impact the real-world experience on cost-constrained systems.</p>
<p>The test environment represents a common deployment scenario: budget hardware for office, education, or kiosk use where resources are scarce. On such machines, the inherent overhead of the operating system directly competes with applications for limited CPU and RAM, making efficiency critical.</p>
<p>The results, as reported by Phoronix, are striking. The performance-optimized CachyOS consistently led in raw throughput and responsiveness across a variety of workloads. Ubuntu 26.04 LTS, Canonical's upcoming long-term support release, also performed on par with or often better than Windows 11. The default Windows 11 installation was frequently the slowest of the three on this low-spec configuration.</p>
<p><em>Editor's note: The source article references Ubuntu 26.04 LTS, which is not scheduled for official release until April 2026. The benchmarks may have used a development or pre-release build. Details regarding the specific CHUWI laptop model could not be independently verified at time of publication.</em></p>
<p>These findings present a clear strategic consideration for IT planners. When the primary constraints are budget and performance — not feature parity — the OS itself becomes a key performance lever.</p>
<p>For IT teams seeking maximum responsiveness from existing budget fleets, a performance-focused distribution like CachyOS offers a compelling advantage. The trade-off is potentially greater administrative overhead and a faster release cycle compared to a stable enterprise option.</p>
<p>Ubuntu 26.04 LTS, in contrast, embodies the stability-focused choice. Its promised five-year standard support cycle provides a predictable, manageable platform for large-scale deployments. Crucially, the benchmarks suggest this stability is achieved without a significant performance penalty on entry-level Wildcat Lake hardware.</p>
<p>While Windows 11's ecosystem of enterprise management tools and application compatibility keeps it as a default for many, this data forces a cost-benefit analysis. On this class of hardware, sticking with Windows may mean accepting a tangible performance deficit that impacts daily productivity.</p>
<p>The study's value lies in filling a common blind spot. Procurement for educational labs, thin clients, or office fleets often depends on performance at the lower end of the spectrum, not the top. The performance delta between operating systems is amplified here, making the OS choice more consequential than it is on a high-end workstation.</p>
<p>As organizations seek to optimize spending and extend the useful life of their hardware, a more efficient operating system is a powerful tool. This Phoronix comparison provides a starting point for IT teams to investigate piloting these Linux distributions, ultimately selecting the platform that best balances raw speed, long-term support, and specific application needs.</p>

<p>大多數操作系統基準測試都聚焦於旗艦硬件。Phoronix 一項新的比較測試採取了相反的方法，在一部售價 449 美元的 CHUWI 入門級筆記本電腦上，對比 Windows 11、Ubuntu 26.04 LTS 以及經過性能調校的 CachyOS。據報告，該設備配備了 Intel Core 3 Wildcat Lake 處理器及 8GB 記憶體——這種配置揭示了操作系統的選擇如何能顯著影響資源受限系統的實際使用體驗。</p>
<p>測試環境代表了一種常見的部署場景：用於辦公室、教育或自助終端機的低階硬件，資源十分有限。在此類機器上，操作系統本身的固有開銷會直接與應用程式爭奪有限的 CPU 和記憶體資源，因此效率至關重要。</p>
<p>結果如 Phoronix 報導所述，相當引人注目。注重性能調校的 CachyOS 在原始吞吐量和響應速度上，於多種工作負載中均持續領先。Ubuntu 26.04 LTS 作為 Canonical 即將推出的長期支持版本，其表現亦與 Windows 11 持平，甚至通常更佳。在這種低規格配置下，預設安裝的 Windows 11 往往是三者中最慢的。</p>
<p><em>編者按：原文引用了 Ubuntu 26.04 LTS，該版本預計於 2026 年 4 月才正式發布。基準測試可能使用了開發版或預發布版本。關於具體 CHUWI 筆記本型號的細節，在發佈時未能獨立核實。</em></p>
<p>這些發現對 IT 規劃人員提出了一個明確的戰略考量。當主要的限制因素是預算與性能——而非功能對等時——操作系統本身便成為一個關鍵的性能調節槓桿。</p>
<p>對於希望從現有低階設備中獲得最佳響應速度的 IT 團隊而言，像 CachyOS 這樣注重性能的發行版提供了顯著的優勢。但其代價可能是相比穩定的企業級選項，需要承擔更重的管理負擔和更快的發行週期。</p>
<p>相比之下，Ubuntu 26.04 LTS 體現了注重穩定性的選擇。其承諾的五年標準支持週期為大規模部署提供了可預測且易於管理的平台。至關重要的是，基準測試表明，這種穩定性並未在入門級 Wildcat Lake 硬件上造成顯著的性能損失。</p>
<p>儘管 Windows 11 的企業管理工具生態系統和應用程式兼容性使其仍是許多情況下的默認選擇，但這些數據迫使我們進行成本效益分析。在此類硬件上堅持使用 Windows，可能意味著要接受一個實實在在的性能差距，從而影響日常生產力。</p>
<p>這項研究的價值在於填補了一個常見的盲點。教育實驗室、瘦客戶端或辦公室設備的採購，往往取決於光譜低階而非高階的性能表現。操作系統之間的性能差距在此被放大，使得操作系統的選擇比在高階工作站上更為關鍵。</p>
<p>隨著組織力求優化支出並延長硬件的使用壽命，一個更高效的操作系統是一個強大的工具。這項 Phoronix 的比較測試為 IT 團隊調查試用這些 Linux 發行版提供了起點，最終選擇在原始速度、長期支持及特定應用程式需求之間取得最佳平衡的平台。</p>
