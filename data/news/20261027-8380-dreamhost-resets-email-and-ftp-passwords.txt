<h3>DreamHost Resets Email and FTP Passwords, Quietly Breaking Stored Logins</h3>
<p>DreamHost has forced password resets for email and FTP/SFTP users in response to an ongoing incident that also disrupted connections to its File Manager interface, according to BleepingComputer.</p>
<p>The web hosting provider applied the resets unilaterally — users did not initiate them — leaving customers locked out of automated services that rely on stored credentials. DreamHost has not publicly stated what triggered the resets as of this writing.</p>
<p>Industry practice suggests a provider-initiated mass credential reset is rarely routine maintenance. Hosting companies typically take this disruptive step only when they can no longer vouch for the password material they hold on customers' behalf, or when they need to proactively invalidate credentials that may have been compromised elsewhere. Either way, breaking every stored password at once signals that the provider considers the risk of inaction greater than the disruption of forcing a reset.</p>
<h4>What breaks when credentials are rotated remotely</h4>
<p>For individual users, the immediate impact is an inability to send or receive mail and to connect over FTP/SFTP until new passwords are in place. The less visible consequences tend to matter more:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Mail clients stop syncing.</strong> Desktop and mobile clients configured with saved passwords will fail authentication silently, often surfacing only as delayed or missing messages.</li>
<li><strong>Scheduled jobs fail without alerting.</strong> Cron jobs, deployment scripts, and CI/CD pipelines that push to a server over SFTP will begin erroring — or exiting quietly — the moment stored credentials stop working.</li>
<li><strong>Backups may stop running.</strong> Any off-site or remote backup routine authenticated with an old password can lapse unnoticed for days.</li>
<li><strong>Phishing attacks gain an opening.</strong> Credential-reset waves give attackers a ready pretext for convincing-looking recovery emails. Legitimate resets rarely require clicking through unsolicited links.</li>
</ul>
<p>The automation failures are the point most easily missed. An administrator may notice their mail client prompting for a password within minutes, but a nightly deployment or backup job that fails at 3 a.m. can go undetected until something downstream breaks.</p>
<h4>What to do now</h4>
<p>If your hosting account was affected, the following checklist covers the actions worth taking in order:</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Change passwords through the official control panel only.</strong> Navigate to DreamHost's site directly rather than following any reset link received by email.</li>
<li><strong>Update every stored credential, not just the one that failed.</strong> Check mail client configurations, FTP/SFTP clients, and any application that stores server credentials.</li>
<li><strong>Audit automation.</strong> Review cron entries, CI/CD secrets, deployment scripts, and backup configurations for hard-coded or vault-stored passwords that need rotation.</li>
<li><strong>Test scheduled jobs manually.</strong> Run a backup or deployment by hand to confirm it authenticates successfully rather than assuming silence means success.</li>
<li><strong>Enable or verify two-factor authentication</strong> on the hosting control panel and associated mail accounts while credentials are already being changed.</li>
<li><strong>Treat unsolicited reset emails with suspicion.</strong> Verify any message against the provider's official status channels before acting on it.</li>
</ol>
<h4>A developing story</h4>
<p>The reporting describes an incident still in progress, and DreamHost has not publicly stated what triggered the resets or whether any customer data was exposed as of this writing. Organisations relying on shared hosting for mail, file transfers, or automated deployments should watch the provider's official status and announcement channels for a root-cause statement before drawing conclusions about the scope of the event.</p>
<p>For Hong Kong businesses running mail and deployments on overseas shared hosting, the failure mode is identical regardless of where the provider is based.</p>
<p>For IT teams, the broader lesson is one worth applying regardless of outcome: stored credentials are single points of failure. Any service that authenticates silently — mail sync, scheduled jobs, CI pipelines — deserves periodic verification that it is still working, because the first sign of a broken password is often not an error message, but an absence.</p>

<h3>DreamHost 強制重設電郵及 FTP 密碼，令已儲存的登入悄然失效</h3>
<p>據 BleepingComputer 報道，DreamHost 為應對一宗持續中的事故，已強制要求電郵及 FTP/SFTP 用戶重設密碼，該事故同時亦影響其 File Manager 介面的連線。</p>
<p>這家網頁寄存服務商單方面執行重設——用戶並未主動申請——導致依賴已儲存憑證的自動化服務全部失效，客戶無法登入。截至本文撰稿時，DreamHost 尚未公開說明是次重設的觸發原因。</p>
<p>按業界慣例，由服務商主動發起的大規模憑證重設極少屬例行維護。寄存公司通常只有在無法再為代客戶保管的密碼資料作保，或需要主動作廢可能已在其他地方外洩的憑證時，才會採取這種具破壞性的步驟。無論哪種情況，一次性令所有已儲存密碼失效，意味著服務商認為不作為的風險大於強制重設所帶來的中斷。</p>
<h4>遠端輪換憑證會令甚麼失效</h4>
<p>對個別用戶而言，即時影響是在新密碼設定前無法收發郵件及以 FTP/SFTP 連線。較不明顯的後果往往更為關鍵：</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>郵件客戶端停止同步。</strong> 已設定儲存密碼的桌面及流動郵件客戶端會無聲驗證失敗，通常只會以郵件延遲或遺失的形式浮現。</li>
<li><strong>排程任務失敗而沒有任何警示。</strong> cron jobs、部署腳本，以及透過 SFTP 將變更推送至伺服器的 CI/CD pipelines，一旦儲存的憑證失效便會開始出錯——或者靜默退出。</li>
<li><strong>備份可能停止執行。</strong> 任何以舊密碼驗證的異地或遠端備份程序，可能連續數日失效而無人察覺。</li>
<li><strong>釣魚攻擊有機可乘。</strong> 密碼重設潮為攻擊者提供了現成藉口，發送貌似可信的復原電郵。真正的密碼重設極少要求用戶點擊不請自來的連結。</li>
</ul>
<p>自動化失效是最容易被忽略的一環。管理員可能在數分鐘內便留意到郵件客戶端要求輸入密碼，但一個在凌晨三時執行失敗的深夜部署或備份任務，可能要等下游環節出事才會被發現。</p>
<h4>現在應採取的行動</h4>
<p>如果你的寄存帳戶受到影響，以下清單按先後次序列出值得採取的行動：</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>只透過官方控制面板更改密碼。</strong> 直接瀏覽 DreamHost 網站，切勿跟隨任何經電郵收到的重設連結。</li>
<li><strong>更新所有已儲存的憑證，而不只是失效的那一項。</strong> 檢查郵件客戶端設定、FTP/SFTP 客戶端，以及任何儲存伺服器憑證的應用程式。</li>
<li><strong>審核自動化設定。</strong> 檢視 cron entries、CI/CD secrets、部署腳本及備份設定，找出需要輪換的硬編碼或 vault 儲存密碼。</li>
<li><strong>手動測試排程任務。</strong> 親自執行一次備份或部署，確認其能成功驗證，而非假設沒有訊息便代表正常。</li>
<li><strong>在寄存控制面板及相關郵件帳戶啟用或核實雙重認證（two-factor authentication）</strong>，趁憑證正在更改之際一併處理。</li>
<li><strong>對不請自來的重設電郵保持警惕。</strong> 在採取任何行動前，先對照服務商的官方狀態頻道核實訊息真偽。</li>
</ol>
<h4>事件仍在發展中</h4>
<p>報道描述的是一宗仍在進行的事故，截至本文撰稿時，DreamHost 尚未公開說明重設的觸發原因，亦未交代是否有客戶資料外洩。依賴共享寄存（shared hosting）處理郵件、檔案傳輸或自動化部署的機構，應密切留意服務商的官方狀態及公告頻道，等待根本原因聲明，才就事件影響範圍作出結論。</p>
<p>對於在海外共享寄存平台營運郵件及部署的香港企業來說，無論服務商位於何地，故障模式均完全相同。</p>
<p>對 IT 團隊而言，無論最終結果如何，都有一項值得汲取的教訓：已儲存的憑證是單一故障點（single point of failure）。任何靜默驗證的服務——郵件同步、排程任務、CI pipelines——都值得定期核實其仍在正常運作，因為密碼失效的第一個徵兆往往不是錯誤訊息，而是什麼也沒有。</p>
