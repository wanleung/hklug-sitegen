Title: Hack Week 26 Opens Next Week as openSUSE Project Board Fills With Networking, Security and Storage Ideas
Content:
<p>openSUSE published a preview of Hack Week 26 on November 25, ahead of the event starting next week, with openSUSE and SUSE developers filling the project board with entries at every stage — from early ideas to nearly finished code.</p>
<p>The newest list spans networking, security, storage, operations and a bit of retro gaming. Further-along entries include NetworkManager Wi-Fi WDS support from contributor sbrabec, and rlv, a terminal tool inspired by k9s and Freelens that turns a Rancher support bundle into a navigable, queryable virtual cluster with automatic problem detection. Still early are ai-guard, which is looking for a hacker to build it — the proposed tool would use mitmproxy to decrypt and inspect outbound HTTPS traffic destined for Gemini, OpenAI, Claude and DeepSeek — and a frozen btrfs snapshot project with one hacker and one follower listed. The gaming side features Geeko McFly, an NES project built with llvm-mos, neslib and nesdoug for a 32 KB NROM cartridge. openSUSE notes that Hack Week began in 2007 and has since produced tools such as Weblate and Aeon Desktop, while ownCloud and Nextcloud both trace their origins to a Hack Week project.</p>
<p>Read as an informal roadmap, the board points in two directions: entries close to completion suggest changes likely to surface in Tumbleweed, Leap or upstream projects in the near term, while the earliest entries flag areas developers still consider under-served.</p>
<p>Hack Week is open to everyone, from individual contributors to large project teams, who can claim an existing idea or propose a new one. The full preview is on the openSUSE news site, and board registration is open.</p>
<hr>
<p>openSUSE 於 11 月 25 日刊出 Hack Week 26 預覽，活動將於下週展開，openSUSE 與 SUSE 開發者正把專案看板填上各個階段的條目——由早期的點子到幾近完成的程式碼兼有。</p>
<p>最新一批清單橫跨網絡、保安、儲存、系統維護，以及少量復古遊戲題材。進度較前的條目包括：由貢獻者 sbrabec 提出的 NetworkManager Wi-Fi WDS 支援；以及受 k9s 與 Freelens 啟發的終端工具 rlv，能把 Rancher 支援資料包轉成可導航、可查詢的虛擬叢集，並自動偵測問題。仍處於早期階段的有兩項：一是 ai-guard，正物色開發者動手實現——該工具構思以 mitmproxy 解密及檢視發往 Gemini、OpenAI、Claude 與 DeepSeek 的對外 HTTPS 流量；二是 btrfs 凍結式快照專案，目前僅列出一名開發者與一名追隨者。遊戲方面則有 Geeko McFly，一個以 llvm-mos、neslib 與 nesdoug 製作、面向 32 KB NROM 卡匣的 NES 專案。openSUSE 指出，Hack Week 於 2007 年首辦，此後產出過 Weblate、Aeon Desktop 等工具與桌面發行版；ownCloud 與 Nextcloud 亦同樣源自一個 Hack Week 專案。</p>
<p>若把看板視為一份非正式路線圖，可見兩個方向：接近完成的條目，預示 Tumbleweed、Leap 或上游專案短期內可能浮現的變動；最早的條目，則標示開發者認為仍欠支援的範疇。</p>
<p>Hack Week 向所有人開放，無論個人貢獻者還是大型專案團隊，均可認領既有構想或提出新想法。完整預覽已刊於 openSUSE 新聞站，看板登記同步開放中。</p>
