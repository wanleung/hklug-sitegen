Title: Internet Society Honours Dave Täht With Jonathan B. Postel Service Award

<h3>Internet Society Honours Dave Täht With Jonathan B. Postel Service Award</h3>
<p>The Internet Society has named engineer and open-source contributor Dave Täht as the latest recipient of the Jonathan B. Postel Service Award, recognising decades of work on the unglamorous plumbing that keeps internet connections responsive. LWN.net reported the announcement on 25 November.</p>
<p>The Society's announcement, written in the past tense and describing what "Täht's legacy can be found in," indicates the honour has been awarded posthumously. In its accompanying coverage, LWN adds an editorial note that Täht "is still deeply missed by those of us who knew him."</p>
<p>The award itself is one of the more consequential honours in networking circles. Named after Postel, the long-time editor of the RFC series who died in 1998, it has been presented annually since 1999 to individuals whose sustained service has benefited the internet as a whole. Previous recipients include figures behind core protocols and infrastructure rather than product launches.</p>
<p>Täht's best-known contribution is the fight against bufferbloat — a problem that produces the familiar symptom of a connection that tests at full speed yet stutters on a video call, chokes when someone else starts a download, or adds a full second of lag to a game.</p>
<h4>The problem, in plain terms</h4>
<p>Bufferbloat is not a capacity problem. It is an architecture problem. When routers and home gateway devices queue packets in oversized buffers instead of dropping or scheduling them intelligently, traffic sits in line. Throughput looks fine on a speed test; latency balloons under load. Users respond by buying a faster plan, which does nothing, because the delay is caused by the queue, not the pipe.</p>
<p>The fix Täht championed was queue management that keeps buffers short and fair. His FQ-CoDel (Fair Queueing with Controlled Delay) algorithm, co-developed with Kathleen Nichols, was merged into the Linux kernel in version 3.5 and has shipped in essentially every mainstream distribution and router firmware built on Linux since. The later CAKE (Common Applications Kept Enhanced) shaper, designed for easier deployment on consumer-grade hardware, entered the kernel in version 5.4. Neither requires new hardware or more bandwidth — only firmware that applies sane defaults.</p>
<h4>Why it matters</h4>
<p>According to the <a href="https://www.internetsociety.org/blog/2026/11/postel-awardee-dave-taht-made-every-millisecond-count/">Internet Society's announcement</a>, Täht's legacy can be found in community hubs in Nicaragua, where he worked to expand connectivity and first discovered the urgency of solving bufferbloat, and in every lag-free video call that has since become ordinary. The Society also credits him with helping Starlink work through latency challenges on its network.</p>
<p>The practical lesson for network operators and home users alike: an underloaded router with a bloated queue will still ruin a call. The remedy is shipping bufferbloat-aware queueing — often called Smart Queue Management, or SQM — through open firmware projects such as OpenWrt, and pressing equipment vendors to enable it by default rather than leaving it buried.</p>
<p>For anyone running a gigabit connection through a stock ISP-supplied gateway, the takeaway is the same: raw speed is rarely the bottleneck once buffers are left unchecked, and SQM-capable firmware remains the cheapest performance upgrade available.</p>
<p>The award recognises work that made every millisecond count — and ratifies what the kernel community has accepted for more than a decade: that milliseconds, not megabits, are the metric that decides whether a network feels fast.</p>

<h3>互聯網協會頒發 Jonathan B. Postel 服務獎予 Dave Täht</h3>
<p>互聯網協會（Internet Society）宣布，工程師及開放源碼貢獻者 Dave Täht 獲選為最新一屆 Jonathan B. Postel 服務獎得主，表揚他數十年來投身於網絡連線背後毫不起眼的「管道工程」，令互聯網連接維持回應速度。LWN.net 於 11 月 25 日報道有關消息。</p>
<p>協會的公告以過去時態行文，並描述「Täht 的遺澤可見於……」，顯示今次表揚為身後追頒。在相關報道中，LWN 另加按語，指 Täht「在我們這些認識他的人心中，依然深受懷念」。</p>
<p>該獎項本身是網絡技術界最具份量的榮譽之一。獎項以 RFC 系列長期編輯、1998 年逝世的 Postel 命名，自 1999 年起每年頒予持續服務、惠及整個互聯網的個人。過往得獎者多為核心協議與基礎架構背後的人物，而非產品發布的推手。</p>
<p>Täht 最為人熟知的貢獻，是對付緩衝區膨脹（bufferbloat）的持續努力——這問題造成的症狀大家都很熟悉：連線測試明明跑出全速，視像通話卻卡頓不已；別人一開始下載就窒礙不通；玩遊戲平白多出整整一秒延遲。</p>
<h4>問題所在，用白話來說</h4>
<p>緩衝區膨脹並非頻寬容量問題，而是架構問題。當路由器及家用閘道裝置把數據包排進過大的緩衝區，而非明智地丟棄或調度時，流量便滯留在佇列之中。速度測試顯示吞吐量一切正常；一旦負載上升，延遲便急劇膨脹。用戶的反應是升級更快的上網計劃，但於事無補，因為延遲源自佇列，而非管道本身。</p>
<p>Täht 力推的解決方案，是把緩衝區維持在短而公平的佇列管理方式。他與 Kathleen Nichols 共同開發的 FQ-CoDel（Fair Queueing with Controlled Delay，公平佇列排程搭配受控延遲）演算法，已於 Linux kernel 3.5 版本合併，此後基本上所有主流 Linux 發行版及基於 Linux 的路由器韌體均有搭載。其後為方便消費級硬件部署而設計的 CAKE（Common Applications Kept Enhanced）整形器，則於 5.4 版本進入 kernel。兩者皆無需新硬件或更多頻寬——只需韌體套用合理的預設值。</p>
<h4>為何重要</h4>
<p>根據<a href="https://www.internetsociety.org/blog/2026/11/postel-awardee-dave-taht-made-every-millisecond-count/">互聯網協會的公告</a>，Täht 的遺澤可見於尼加拉瓜的社區網絡樞紐——他在當地致力擴展網絡覆蓋，並首次體會到解決緩衝區膨脹的迫切性；亦可見於其後成為日常、再無延遲之憂的每一場視像通話之中。協會亦歸功於他協助 Starlink 解決其網絡上的延遲挑戰。</p>
<p>對網絡營運商及家用用戶同樣適用的實務教訓是：一台佇列膨脹的路由器，即使負載不重，一樣會毀掉通話質素。補救方法是透過 OpenWrt 等開放韌體項目，推出支援緩衝區膨脹意識的佇列管理——通常稱為智慧佇列管理（Smart Queue Management，SQM）——並推動設備供應商預設啟用此功能，而非將其埋藏在深處。</p>
<p>對任何透過 ISP 提供的原裝閘道裝置使用千兆連線的用戶來說，結論一樣：一旦緩衝區無人看管，原始速度很少是真正的瓶頸；而支援 SQM 的韌體，仍然是目前成本最低的性能升級方案。</p>
<p>今次獎項表揚的是讓每一毫秒都發揮作用的工作——並實質上追認了 kernel 社羣十多年來的共識：決定網絡「感覺快不快」的量度標準，是毫秒（milliseconds），而非 megabits。</p>
