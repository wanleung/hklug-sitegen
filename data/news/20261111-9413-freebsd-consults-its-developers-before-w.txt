<h3>FreeBSD Consults Its Developers Before Writing Rules for AI-Generated Code</h3>
<p>The FreeBSD Project is moving toward a formal position on AI-generated contributions, with work on a draft policy now underway after the project polled its developers — a step that places one of the oldest open-source operating systems among the projects quietly turning informal practice into written rules.</p>
<p>The effort is listed alongside the quarter's other development updates in FreeBSD's Q3 2026 status report, published Tuesday, according to Phoronix, which covered the report's release. The document covers a range of ongoing initiatives across toolchain work, ports, and documentation; the AI policy work stands out less for its technical scope than for what it signals about how mature projects are choosing to govern machine-assisted contributions.</p>
<h4>From unwritten norms to written policy</h4>
<p>FreeBSD's approach is notable for its sequencing: rather than issuing a top-down decree, the project canvassed its own developers first. That consultative method reflects a broader reality in open-source governance — many projects have spent the past two years improvising norms around AI-authored patches, generated documentation, and LLM-assisted bug triage, often with nothing more than moderator judgement and a handful of mailing-list threads to guide them.</p>
<p>The result across the ecosystem has been uneven. Some large software foundations have leaned toward enablement, publishing guidance that encourages responsible use of AI tooling while placing the onus on contributors to verify and own what they submit. Other projects have drawn harder lines, restricting or scrutinising AI-generated contributions more tightly on the grounds that unverified output burdens reviewers and can introduce subtle defects. FreeBSD's poll-based process suggests it is trying to derive its policy from the people who will have to enforce it, rather than importing a template.</p>
<p>That distinction matters for the wider community. AI contribution policies are effectively accountability documents: they determine who answers when a generated patch breaks something, how much review scrutiny is expected, and whether the volume of a contribution is itself a virtue. A project that publishes a clear, developer-informed policy gives downstream users and packagers something concrete to assess.</p>
<h4>Verification still required</h4>
<p>The report is a status document rather than a finished policy, and the drafting process appears to be in progress rather than complete. Organisations that need to cite FreeBSD's formal position on AI-generated contributions should consult the project's primary sources — the Q3 2026 status report and FreeBSD's own documentation channels — directly before relying on it for compliance or procurement purposes. Terms of any policy, including scope and enforcement, are not settled until the project says so.</p>
<p>An open question remains even after a policy lands: how FreeBSD will verify compliance with it, given that distinguishing human-written from model-assisted code is difficult and disclosure norms are hard to enforce. For IT professionals running FreeBSD-derived systems — including firewall and networking appliances — the project's choices in this area may eventually be felt whether or not they follow upstream project politics.</p>

<h3>FreeBSD 諮詢開發者後才制定 AI 生成程式碼規則</h3>
<p>FreeBSD Project 正就 AI 生成貢獻擬定正式立場，目前在向項目開發者進行調查後，已開始草擬政策文件——此舉令這個歷史最悠久的開放源碼作業系統之一，加入了悄然將非正式慣例轉化為成文規則的項目行列。</p>
<p>據 Phoronix 報道，該報告於本周二發布，此項工作與報告中其他開發進展一併列出。文件涵蓋 toolchain 工作、ports 及文件編製等多項持續進行的計劃；AI 政策工作之所以突出，與其技術範圍無關，而在於它反映成熟項目如何選擇管治機器輔助貢獻。</p>
<h4>由不成文慣例到成文政策</h4>
<p>FreeBSD 的做法值得注意之處在於其順序：項目沒有頒布自上而下的指令，而是先向自身開發者徵詢意見。這種諮詢方式反映開放源碼治理的一個更廣泛現實——過去兩年間，許多項目一直在就 AI 撰寫的 patch、自動生成的文件及 LLM 輔助的 bug 分類摸索不成文規範，往往僅憑管理員判斷及若干郵件列表討論作為指引。</p>
<p>整個生態系統的結果參差不齊。部分大型軟件基金會傾向支持 AI 工具的使用，發布指引鼓勵負責任地使用 AI 工具，同時要求貢獻者自行核實及承擔所提交內容的責任。其他項目則劃下更嚴格界線，以未經核實的產出會加重審核員負擔、並可能引入細微缺陷為由，對 AI 生成貢獻作出更嚴密的限制或審查。FreeBSD 以調查為基礎的流程顯示，它試圖從將來需要執行政策的人身上擬定政策，而非直接套用現成範本。</p>
<p>這項區別對整個社群意義重大。AI 貢獻政策本質上是問責文件：它們決定當一個生成的 patch 弄壞東西時由誰負責、預期需要多少審核力度，以及貢獻數量本身是否算是一項優點。一個公布清晰、經開發者意見形成的政策的項目，能為下游用戶及打包者提供具體的評估依據。</p>
<h4>仍須核實</h4>
<p>該報告是一份狀態文件而非完成的政策，草擬過程看來仍在進行而非已告完成。需要引用 FreeBSD 就 AI 生成貢獻的正式立場的機構，應在將其用於合規或採購用途前，直接查閱項目的一手資料——2026 年第三季狀態報告及 FreeBSD 自身的文件渠道。政策的任何條款，包括範圍及執行方式，須待項目正式公布方算落實。</p>
<p>即使政策出台，仍有一個懸而未決的問題：FreeBSD 將如何核實是否遵守政策——畢竟區分人手編寫與模型輔助生成的程式碼十分困難，而披露慣例亦難以強制執行。對於運行 FreeBSD 衍生系統的 IT 專業人士——包括防火牆及網絡設備——項目在這方面的選擇，無論他們是否跟隨上游項目動向，日後都可能感受得到。</p>
