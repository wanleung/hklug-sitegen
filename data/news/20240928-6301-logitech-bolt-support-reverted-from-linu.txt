Title: Logitech Bolt Support Reverted from Linux 7.3 Kernel to Protect Stability
Content:
<p>A planned feature for the upcoming Linux 7.3 kernel has been pulled back to safeguard system stability. A patch enabling Logitech Bolt wireless receiver support via the HID++ protocol was initially merged, but a wave of user-reported bugs has led maintainers to revert the change.</p>
<p>The functionality was added early in the 7.3 development cycle to the <code>hid-logitech-dj</code> driver, aiming to bring compatibility to newer Logitech peripherals. However, as testers began using early kernel builds, numerous problems surfaced, including erratic mouse movement and connection instability with Bolt-equipped devices. The regressions were widespread enough to trigger the revert.</p>
<p>This action exemplifies a core tenet of the Linux kernel's development philosophy: mainline stability takes precedence over unproven features. By reverting the patch, developers ensure the final release remains reliable for all users, rather than shipping a feature that could break existing hardware.</p>
<p>The process highlights the essential role of the community in testing and hardening open-source code. While the setback delays official Bolt support, it provides a stable foundation for developers to address the root causes. A revised, more robust patch is expected to be proposed for a future release, such as Linux 7.4, after further refinement.</p>
<p>For end users, this means a specific feature is postponed, not abandoned. The episode demonstrates the trade-offs in community-driven development, where the health of the overall system is prioritized, ensuring that progress on new hardware support doesn't come at the cost of existing reliability.</p>
<hr>
<p>原本計劃納入即將推出的 Linux 7.3 內核的一項功能已被撤回，以保障系統穩定性。一項透過 HID++ 協議啟用 Logitech Bolt 無線接收器支援的補丁原本已被合併，但大量用戶報告的問題促使維護者撤銷了這項變更。</p>
<p>該功能在 7.3 開發週期早期被加入至 <code>hid-logitech-dj</code> 驅動程式，旨在為新款羅技周邊裝置提供兼容性。然而，隨著測試人員開始使用早期內核版本，眾多問題浮現，包括 Bolt 設備出現滑鼠游標異常移動及連線不穩等情況。這些退化問題相當普遍，足以觸發回滾操作。</p>
<p>此舉體現了 Linux 內核開發哲學的一項核心原則：主幹穩定性優先於未經驗證的功能。透過撤回補丁，開發者確保最終發行版對所有用戶維持可靠，而非推出可能破壞現有硬件的功能。</p>
<p>此過程突顯了社群在測試與強化開源程式碼方面的關鍵角色。儘管這次挫折延遲了官方 Bolt 支援的推出，但為開發者解決根本問題提供了穩定基礎。預計在進一步完善後，一份修正後、更穩健的補丁將會提交至未來的發行版本（例如 Linux 7.4）。</p>
<p>對終端用戶而言，這意味著某項特定功能被推遲，而非放棄。此事件展示了社群主導開發中的權衡取捨：系統整體健康狀況被優先考慮，確保新增硬件支援的進展不會以犧牲現有可靠性為代價。</p>
