Title: Manjaro contributors form independent non-profit to sit alongside project's commercial arm
Content:
<p>Contributors to Manjaro Linux have established a German non-profit association, the Manjaro Project e.V., that is formally independent of Manjaro GmbH &amp; Co KG, the company associated with the project's commercial activities. Contributor Frank Vandermeiren announced the formation; LWN.net reported the announcement this month, having documented a governance dispute between a group of contributors and project lead Philip Müller earlier in the year.</p>
<p>An e.V. — eingetragener Verein — is a registered association under German law. The point that matters for a volunteer project is that it is a legal person in its own right: it can hold funds, enter contracts, own assets and sue or be sued, separately from any company. Until now, Manjaro's community appears to have had no collective legal vehicle of its own, while the commercial side of the project operated as a GmbH &amp; Co KG. The e.V. gives contributors a counterweight — a body with standing that exists independently of the commercial arm.</p>
<p><strong>What the e.V. does — and doesn't — settle</strong></p>
<p>The dispute LWN.net reported earlier in the year pitted a number of contributors against Philip Müller over how the project is directed. The e.V. can be read as an institutional response: it converts informal contributor discontent into an organisation with legal personality and, potentially, enforceable rights.</p>
<p>What the association will actually do, however, remains unspecified. Whether it controls trademarks, funding, infrastructure or the release process — and how it relates operationally to Manjaro GmbH &amp; Co KG — has not been detailed. Two questions carry the most weight. First, who owns the Manjaro name, and does the e.V. hold any claim to it? Second, how are sponsorship, hosting and support revenue divided between the association and the company? Until the e.V. exercises rights over specific assets, its influence is untested: the announcement provides leverage, but does not yet demonstrate it.</p>
<p><strong>Why this matters beyond Manjaro</strong></p>
<p>Governance by member association is the established model for many large open-source projects — Debian, the Apache Software Foundation and the Python Software Foundation all operate as member-governed non-profits in which contributors hold formal standing. The contrast case is the fork: LibreOffice and MariaDB both emerged from projects where community-versus-company tensions could not be resolved in place. Manjaro is attempting something in between — formalising representation without splitting the project — and the outcome is genuinely undetermined, which is why the arrangement is worth watching.</p>
<p>Whether the e.V. resolves the earlier dispute or institutionalises it depends on whether both sides cooperate. If the association and the company divide responsibilities amicably, contributors gain a seat with legal weight; if they cannot, the stage is set for a prolonged contest over the assets of a widely used distribution.</p>
<p>For IT professionals running or recommending Manjaro, the indicators to track are the e.V.'s first concrete actions: any claim over trademarks, and any published arrangement for how money flows between the association and the company.</p>
<hr>
<p>Manjaro Linux 的貢獻者已成立一家德國非營利協會「Manjaro Project e.V.」，在形式上獨立於負責項目商業活動的 Manjaro GmbH &amp; Co KG。貢獻者 Frank Vandermeiren 宣布了協會的成立；LWN.net 本月報導了該消息，此前該網今年初曾報導一群貢獻者與項目負責人 Philip Müller 之間的治理爭議。</p>
<p>e.V.（eingetragener Verein）是德國法律下的註冊協會。對一個志願者項目而言，重點在於它本身即是獨立的法人：它可以持有資金、簽訂合約、擁有資產，並進行訴訟或被訴，與任何公司完全分開。此前，Manjaro 社群似乎並沒有自己的集體法律載體，而項目的商業部分則以 GmbH &amp; Co KG 形式運作。e.V. 為貢獻者提供了一股制衡力量——一個獨立於商業部門、具備法律地位的團體。</p>
<p><strong>e.V. 能解決什麼——以及不能解決什麼</strong></p>
<p>LWN.net 年初報導的爭議，涉及多名貢獻者與 Philip Müller 在項目治理方向上的分歧。e.V. 可被視為一種制度上的回應：它把貢獻者非正式的不滿，轉化為一個具法律人格、並可能擁有可強制執行權利的組織。</p>
<p>然而，協會實際將從事什麼工作，目前仍未具體說明。它是否掌控商標、資金、基礎設施或發布流程——以及在運作上如何與 Manjaro GmbH &amp; Co KG 配合——均未詳述。其中兩個問題最為關鍵。第一，Manjaro 名稱由誰擁有，e.V. 是否對此擁有任何權利主張？第二，贊助、託管及支援收入如何在協會與公司之間分配？在 e.V. 就具體資產行使權利之前，其影響力尚未經考驗：這次宣布提供了籌碼，但尚未證明其成效。</p>
<p><strong>為何此事的意義不限於 Manjaro</strong></p>
<p>由會員協會治理是眾多大型開源項目沿用的模式——Debian、Apache Software Foundation 及 Python Software Foundation 均以會員治理的非營利組織形式運作，貢獻者在其中享有正式地位。相反的案例是分叉（fork）：LibreOffice 與 MariaDB 均源於社群與公司之間的張力無法在原項目內解決的情況。Manjaro 嘗試的是一條中間路線——在不分裂項目的前提下把代表機制制度化——而結果確實未有定論，這正是此安排值得留意的原因。</p>
<p>e.V. 究竟會化解早前的爭議，還是將其制度化，取決於雙方能否合作。若協會與公司能友善劃分職責，貢獻者將獲得一個具法律份量的席位；若不能，則一場圍繞這個廣泛使用的發行版本資產的長期角力已蓄勢待發。</p>
<p>對負責營運或推薦 Manjaro 的 IT 專業人士而言，需要留意的指標是 e.V. 的首批具體行動：對商標的任何權利主張，以及任何已公布的、關於協會與公司之間資金流向的安排。</p>
