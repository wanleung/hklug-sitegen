Title: Linux Mint 27 Beta Marks the Finish Line of Mint's Long Move to Wayland
Content:
<p>Linux Mint has put the beta of Linux Mint 27 out for public testing, and the headline detail in the release is not any single feature but a milestone: with this cycle, the distribution completes its long-running transition away from X11, shipping what Phoronix described as "full Wayland support" alongside a set of new applications.</p>
<p>The beta lands well ahead of the stable release, which is expected around Christmas — an expectation rather than a committed date. For a distribution whose release cadence has historically tracked the holiday window, that leaves roughly a month of testing before the version reaches Mint's wide base of desktop users.</p>
<h4>What the beta brings</h4>
<p>Phoronix's coverage of the release points to two headline items: new applications arriving with Linux Mint 27, and the completion of Wayland support across the desktop. Beyond the Wayland work itself, testers should note the practical context: screen sharing moves to portal-based brokers, Hi-DPI scaling behaves differently, and NVIDIA's proprietary driver history with Wayland — much improved in recent generations — remains a combination worth verifying rather than assuming.</p>
<p>It is worth being precise about the wording here. "Full Wayland support" is not the same claim as "Wayland is the default session." The report does not, in itself, settle whether the beta boots into a Wayland session by default or simply offers one alongside X11 — a distinction that matters a great deal for upgrade behaviour, screen-sharing workflows, and graphics driver combinations. Users planning to test should confirm the session details against Linux Mint's own blog and release notes before drawing conclusions.</p>
<p>Likewise, the beta should be treated as a beta: fresh installs on test hardware or in virtual machines are the sensible path, not in-place upgrades from a working daily-driver system, and beta releases commonly carry known-broken items or unfinished bits that get cleaned up before the stable tag.</p>
<h4>Why a Mint Wayland rollout matters more than most</h4>
<p>Plenty of distributions have shipped Wayland sessions for years. Mint's adoption is a different kind of signal because of who uses it: a large share of its audience is comparatively non-technical — switchers from Windows and macOS, older hardware in households and offices, users who do not read release notes. When Wayland reaches that base, it reaches people who will never choose a session type from a login screen; whatever ships as default is simply what they get.</p>
<p>For testers working through the run-up to the stable release, the practical checkpoints are straightforward: confirm that Teams and Zoom screen sharing behaves as expected under the session you are running, verify Hi-DPI scaling on high-resolution laptop panels, and check that your NVIDIA driver version is behaving before recommending anything beyond a test machine. The beta-to-Christmas window is a reasonable period for exactly that kind of validation — on spare hardware, not production desks.</p>
<p>The beta is available for download now for anyone who wants to help shake down what is shaping up to be one of the most consequential Mint releases in several cycles.</p>

<p>Linux Mint 已推出 Linux Mint 27 beta 供公眾測試，而此版本的重點並非任何單一功能，而是一個里程碑：藉著這個開發周期，該發行版完成了長久以來脫離 X11 的轉型，並如 Phoronix 所形容，出貨具備「full Wayland support（完整 Wayland 支援）」以及一系列全新應用程式。</p>
<p>此次 beta 版遠早於穩定版推出，穩定版預計在聖誕節前後面世——這只是一個預期，並非承諾日期。對於一個歷來發行節奏均圍繞假期時段的發行版而言，在此版本抵達 Mint 廣大的桌面用戶群之前，尚餘約一個月的測試時間。</p>
<h4>Beta 版帶來了什麼</h4>
<p>Phoronix 對此版本的報道指出兩大重點：隨 Linux Mint 27 而來的新應用程式，以及桌面全面完成 Wayland 支援。除了 Wayland 相關工作本身，測試者亦應留意實際使用情境：螢幕分享轉用基於 portal 的 broker、Hi-DPI 縮放的行為有所不同，而 NVIDIA 專有驅動程式與 Wayland 的歷史兼容問題——近幾代已大幅改善——仍屬需要實測驗證而非假設成立的組合。</p>
<p>此處的措辭值得釐清。「Full Wayland support」與「Wayland 是預設 session」並非同一說法。該報道本身並未確定 beta 版是預設以 Wayland session 啟動，還是僅在 X11 之外額外提供一個選項——這點對升級行為、螢幕分享工作流程及顯示驅動程式組合均有重大影響。計劃測試的用戶應先查閱 Linux Mint 自身的 blog 及 release notes，核實 session 細節後再作結論。</p>
<p>同樣地，beta 版就應視為 beta 版看待：在測試硬件或虛擬機器上全新安裝才是合理途徑，而非從日常使用的正常系統就地升級；beta 版通常會附帶已知問題或未完成的部分，這些均會在正式穩定版發布前清理完畢。</p>
<h4>為何 Mint 的 Wayland 推出比多數情況更重要</h4>
<p>不少發行版多年來已出貨 Wayland session。Mint 的採用則是另一種信號，原因在於其用戶構成：相當大部分的受眾技術程度相對不高——來自 Windows 和 macOS 的轉換用戶、家庭及辦公室的舊硬件、不會閱讀 release notes 的使用者。當 Wayland 觸及這個用戶群，就等於觸及一群從不會在登入畫面選擇 session 類型的人；預設出貨什麼，他們就用什麼。</p>
<p>對於在穩定版推出前持續測試的人來說，實際檢查點相當直接：確認 Teams 及 Zoom 的螢幕分享在你正運行的 session 下運作如常、在高解析度手提電腦面板上驗證 Hi-DPI 縮放，並在向測試機以外的設備推薦之前，先確認你的 NVIDIA 驅動程式版本運作正常。beta 版至聖誕節這段窗口，正正適合進行此類驗證——應在備用硬件上進行，而非生產環境的桌上型電腦。</p>
<p>Beta 版現已開放下載，歡迎所有希望協助測試的人參與——這次看來將是幾個周期以來其中一次最具影響力的 Mint 發布。</p>
