Title: Arch-Based Omarchy 4.5 Arrives With Native Apple Silicon Support
Content:
<p><strong>The most consequential part of Omarchy 4.5, released today, is not the desktop polish — it is that the Arch Linux-based distribution now installs natively on Apple Silicon Macs.</strong></p>
<p>As Phoronix notes in its coverage of the release, the version bump also carries a batch of desktop customisations. Those changes will animate the changelog threads, but they are not the story. For anyone who has wanted an Arch-derived Linux laptop without hand-assembling the stack, native M-series support is the reason to pay attention.</p>
<h4>Why Apple Silicon is the real news</h4>
<p>Until now, running Linux on Apple's ARM laptops has essentially meant starting from Asahi Linux: a capable but deliberately incremental effort that asks users to wire together a kernel, a Wayland compositor, device enablement, and their own configuration before anything resembling a daily-driver desktop exists. Experienced Arch users can do that work. For everyone else, it is the barrier.</p>
<p>Omarchy 4.5 collapses that assembly step by shipping a pre-integrated path onto the same underlying support. Instead of curating the pieces individually, users get an opinionated distribution that aims to boot and work on M-series machines out of the box. For a project whose entire premise is a batteries-included, opinionated setup, treating Apple's hardware as a first-class install target is a natural extension of that philosophy — and a signal that the hard part is no longer the kernel, but the packaging.</p>
<h4>Read the fine print</h4>
<p>"Supported" here should be read as "installs and works in the common case," not as parity with macOS. Rendering on Apple Silicon depends on the open-source Asahi graphics stack rather than Apple's proprietary drivers, and the slower-maturing pieces — Wi-Fi, GPU acceleration, suspend and resume, external display handling — typically lag the base boot experience. Verify the specifics against the project's own documentation before wiping a machine.</p>
<p>Two further caveats apply regardless of hardware. Rolling releases trade long-term stability for continuously updated packages, so occasional breakage after updates is part of the bargain. And Omarchy is opinionated by design: it replaces the traditional "build it yourself" Arch experience with a preconfigured desktop and a fixed set of choices. That makes it a poor fit for anyone who wants to hand-pick every component, and a good fit for someone who wants Arch's packages, documentation, and ecosystem without spending a weekend on dotfiles.</p>
<h4>The wider ARM signal</h4>
<p>The direction matters more than the version number. An opinionated, batteries-included Arch derivative deciding that Apple's ARM laptops warrant first-class support is a practical marker of ARM moving from niche curiosity to mainstream install target — the sort of shift that changes which hardware local buyers consider viable.</p>
<p>For Hong Kong IT professionals and open-source enthusiasts, that is worth noting as a general observation about where desktop Linux hardware support is heading. It is explicitly not a deployment recommendation: the release carries no Hong Kong-specific implications of its own.</p>
<p>Omarchy 4.5 is available for download from the project's distribution channels.</p>
<hr>
<p><strong>今日發佈的 Omarchy 4.5，最重要之處不在於桌面外觀的完善——而是這個基於 Arch Linux 的發行版如今可在 Apple Silicon Mac 上原生安裝。</strong></p>
<p>正如 Phoronix 在其發佈報導中指出，此次版本升級同時帶來一批桌面自訂功能。這些改動勢必會在 changelog 討論串中引起熱議，但並非焦點所在。對於一直希望擁有一部衍生自 Arch 的 Linux 筆記本、卻不想親自組裝整個技術堆疊的用家而言，原生 M 系列支援才是值得關注的原因。</p>
<h4>為何 Apple Silicon 才是真正的新聞</h4>
<p>在此之前，在 Apple 的 ARM 筆記本上運行 Linux，基本上意味著從 Asahi Linux 開始：這是一個能力出眾但刻意採取漸進方式的項目，要求用家自行將核心、Wayland compositor、裝置啟用以及個人配置逐一串接起來，才能建構出一個可用作日常操作的桌面環境。資深 Arch 用家固然可以完成這項工作，但對其他所有人來說，這正是最大的門檻。</p>
<p>Omarchy 4.5 將這一步驟整合到發行版之內，在同一套底層支援之上提供預先整合的途徑。用家毋須逐一挑選各個組件，而是獲得一個主張鮮明的發行版，目標是開箱即可在 M 系列機器上啟動並運作。對於一個整個前提就是「電池內置」（batteries-included）、主張鮮明的設定方案的項目而言，將 Apple 硬體列為一級安裝目標，正是該理念的自然延伸——同時也是一個信號：困難的部分已不再是核心，而是封裝（packaging）。</p>
<h4>細閱注意事項</h4>
<p>此處的「受支援」應理解為「在一般情況下可以安裝並運作」，而非與 macOS 等同。Apple Silicon 上的圖像渲染依賴開源的 Asahi 圖形堆疊，而非 Apple 的專有驅動程式；成熟較慢的組件——Wi-Fi、GPU 加速、休眠與喚醒、外接顯示器處理——通常會落後於基本啟動體驗。抹除機器資料之前，請先根據項目本身的文件核實具體情況。</p>
<p>另有兩項注意事項，與硬體無關。Rolling release（滾動發行版本）以長期穩定性換取持續更新的套件，因此更新後偶爾出現故障是代價的一部分。此外，Omarchy 設計上主張鮮明：它以預先配置的桌面和一套固定的選擇，取代傳統 Arch「自己動手組裝」的體驗。這令它不適合希望逐一手挑每個組件的用家，但就適合那些想要 Arch 的套件、文件與生態系統、又不想花上一個週末整理 dotfiles 的人。</p>
<h4>更廣泛的 ARM 信號</h4>
<p>方向比版本號更為重要。一個主張鮮明、電池內置的 Arch 衍生版本決定將 Apple 的 ARM 筆記本列為一級支援對象，這實際上標誌著 ARM 正由小眾新奇事物轉變為主流安裝目標——這種轉變會改變本港用家對哪些硬體可行的判斷。</p>
<p>對香港 IT 專業人士和開源愛好者而言，這值得作為一項關於桌面 Linux 硬體支援走向的整體觀察加以留意。這明確並非部署建議：本次發佈本身並無任何針對香港的特定影響。</p>
<p>Omarchy 4.5 現已可於項目的發佈渠道下載。</p>
