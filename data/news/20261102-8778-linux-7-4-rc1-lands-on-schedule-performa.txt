Title: Linux 7.4-rc1 Lands On Schedule: Performance Work, Zen 6 Groundwork, AMDGPU HDMI 2.1 Default
Content:
<p>Linux 7.4-rc1 is out, arriving on schedule even though Linus Torvalds has been on holiday, and capping a merge window that carries meaningful performance improvements, early preparations for AMD's forthcoming Zen 6 processors, and a switch making AMDGPU HDMI 2.1 output the default — per Phoronix's report on the release.</p>
<p>For teams running AMD-based servers and workstations, the practical question is what enters the test queue first.</p>
<p><strong>Performance work first</strong></p>
<p>The performance-oriented changes are the most immediately tangible content in the window. Phoronix characterises the gains as spread across subsystems rather than concentrated in one headline feature — the kind of incremental scheduler, filesystem and driver-level tuning that tends to surface in benchmark suites once a cycle stabilises. Teams maintaining performance baselines should treat this as the category warranting early regression testing, since it is the one most likely to interact with existing tuning, profiling tooling and custom build options in a production image.</p>
<p><strong>Zen 6 groundwork second</strong></p>
<p>Merge-window commits laying groundwork for AMD's Zen 6 architecture matter more to procurement and capacity planners than to current fleets. They do not change how today's hardware behaves, but they signal that upstream is positioning for platforms entering refresh cycles over the coming year. Organisations tracking hardware roadmaps alongside kernel support status can use this as a cue to confirm that driver, firmware and distribution support paths will be mature by the time new machines are actually ordered — a relevant consideration for Hong Kong IT teams specifying new AMD infrastructure.</p>
<p><strong>AMDGPU HDMI 2.1 default third</strong></p>
<p>AMDGPU has moved to enable HDMI 2.1 modes by default. Previously, users had to fall back to older HDMI revisions or rely on workaround options to obtain them. The change simplifies display configuration on affected hardware, which matters for desktop deployments, signage and lab environments where display output is part of the standard build.</p>
<p><strong>Sequencing the work</strong></p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Track rc cadence and known regressions</strong> in your subsystems of interest, particularly the performance changes if you maintain benchmark baselines.</li>
<li><strong>Defer hardware decisions</strong> tied to Zen 6 until distribution kernels and firmware paths for that platform are confirmed, not merely present upstream.</li>
<li><strong>Fold the HDMI default into desktop images</strong> at the next routine refresh rather than backporting it in isolation.</li>
</ol>
<p>None of this requires acting on release day. The value of watching a merge window closely is knowing where each change belongs in the cycle — upstream merges early, distributions follow, and each item above slots into a different point on that timeline.</p>

<hr>
<p>Linux 7.4-rc1 已經發布，儘管 Linus Torvalds 一直休假，仍如期推出，為是次 merge window 劃上句號。當中包含具實質意義的效能改進、AMD 旗下即將推出的 Zen 6 處理器的早期準備工作，以及一項將 AMDGPU HDMI 2.1 輸出設為預設的變更——詳情可參閱 Phoronix 對此次發布的報導。</p>
<p>對於營運 AMD 伺服器及工作站的團隊而言，實際要回答的問題是：哪些改動會最先進入測試佇列。</p>
<p><strong>效能改進首當其衝</strong></p>
<p>以效能為導向的變更是今次 merge window 中最立竿見影的內容。Phoronix 將這些改進形容為分散於各個 subsystem，而非集中在單一旗艦功能——這類漸進式的 scheduler、filesystem 及驅動層面調校，通常要待一個周期穩定後，才會在基準測試套件中顯現效果。維護效能基線的團隊應將此類改動列為需要優先進行回歸測試的項目，因為它們最有可能與生產環境映像中的現有調校、profiling 工具及自訂編譯選項產生互動影響。</p>
<p><strong>Zen 6 準備工作次之</strong></p>
<p>merge window 中為 AMD Zen 6 架構鋪路的提交，對採購及容量規劃人員的意義，大於對現有機群的影響。它們不會改變現今硬件的運作方式，但顯示上游（upstream）正為未來一年進入更新周期的平台作好準備。同時追蹤硬件路線圖及 kernel 支援狀態的機構，可藉此確認驅動、韌體及發行版的支援路徑，會在真正訂購新機器之前已經成熟——這是香港 IT 團隊規劃新 AMD 基礎設施時的相關考慮因素。</p>
<p><strong>AMDGPU HDMI 2.1 設為預設，位列第三</strong></p>
<p>AMDGPU 已改為預設啟用 HDMI 2.1 模式。此前，用戶須退回較舊的 HDMI 版本，或依賴 workaround 選項才能取得這些模式。此項變更簡化了相關硬件上的顯示配置，對於桌面部署、數碼標牌（signage）及實驗室環境尤為重要，因為這些場合的顯示輸出屬於標準建置的一部分。</p>
<p><strong>工作排定先後次序</strong></p>
<ol>
<li><strong>追蹤 rc 節奏及已知回歸問題</strong>，尤其是在你負責的 subsystem 範圍內；若你維護效能基準測試基線，則須特別留意效能方面的變更。</li>
<li><strong>延後與 Zen 6 相關的硬件決定</strong>，直至該平台的發行版 kernel 及韌體路徑得到確認，而非僅僅存在於 upstream。</li>
<li><strong>在下一次例行更新時將 HDMI 預設設定納入桌面映像</strong>，而非單獨進行 backport。</li>
</ol>
<p>以上各項均無需在發布當日採取行動。密切留意 merge window 的價值，在於掌握每項改動在整個周期中所處的位置——上游較早合併，發行版隨後跟進，而上文各項則分別落在該時間線的不同節點上。</p>
