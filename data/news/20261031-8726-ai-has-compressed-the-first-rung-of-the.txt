Title: AI Has Compressed the First Rung of the Security Career Ladder — and Made Competence Harder to Spot
Content:
<p>BleepingComputer has published a feature arguing that generative AI has compressed the first rung of the security career ladder — while making it considerably harder for triage teams and hiring managers to tell genuine competence from polished output. Titled "From Beginner to Bug Hunter: Learning Cybersecurity in the AI Era," the piece sets out how aspiring practitioners should learn the field today, from their first lab exercises to their first bug bounty submission.</p>
<p>Its central claim is that the tools have changed what a beginner can produce, but not what a beginner understands. Learners can now scaffold scripts, decode vulnerable code, draft proof-of-concept exploits and summarise advisories with a fluency that previously took months of grinding through documentation. The result is a cohort that can move fast, but may never have built the mental models those outputs depend on.</p>
<p>That compression has a second, less comfortable consequence: saturation. Bug bounty platforms and junior job applications are crowded with candidates whose portfolios look superficially similar, because the same tools produce the same style of report. When everyone can generate a plausible-looking write-up, the differentiators revert to fundamentals that tooling does not supply — a disciplined methodology, the ability to reproduce a finding reliably, genuine depth when reading source code, clear and unambiguous writing, and an understanding of coordinated disclosure norms.</p>
<p>The roadmap the feature offers to newcomers is correspondingly unfashionable: fundamentals first, tooling second. Networking, operating systems, a scripting language read closely enough to audit, and deliberately practised vulnerability classes remain the spine of an education. AI accelerates each of those stages, the piece suggests, but only for learners who already know what questions to ask. Used as a substitute for understanding, the same tools produce candidates who can describe a flaw without being able to defend their reasoning under scrutiny — which is exactly what technical interviews and bug bounty triage both test.</p>
<p>The burden does not stop at the learner. Hiring managers, mentorship programmes and triage teams all face the same verification problem: an influx of material that is polished but shallow. For organisations building or recruiting security teams, the signal to look for is no longer tool fluency — it is evidence of process. Can the candidate explain why they tested what they tested? Can they scope a finding, rank its impact, and communicate it to a non-specialist? Those questions increasingly separate a shortlisted applicant from the pile.</p>
<p>For readers across the region, that shift is already visible in junior hiring and in the volume of near-identical submissions reaching programme owners. The candidate who can still reason from first principles — and prove it in writing — remains the one worth waiting for.</p>
<hr>
<p>BleepingComputer 近日刊登一篇專文，指生成式 AI 已經壓縮了網絡安全職業階梯的第一級，同時令分流團隊與招聘經理更難從表面完備的產出中分辨真正的實力。文章題為「From Beginner to Bug Hunter: Learning Cybersecurity in the AI Era」，探討有志入行者今天應如何學習網絡安全 — 由第一次實驗室練習，到第一次 bug bounty 提交。</p>
<p>其核心論點是：工具改變了初學者「能做出甚麼」，卻沒有改變初學者「理解了甚麼」。學習者如今可以快速產生腳本、解讀含漏洞的程式碼、撰寫概念驗證 exploit，以及扼要總結安全公告，其流暢程度在過往需要數月鑽研文件才能達到。結果是一批行動迅速、卻可能從未建立這些產出所依賴的底層思維模型的學習者。</p>
<p>這種壓縮帶來第二個較不自在的後果：飽和。bug bounty 平台與初級職位的求職申請，充斥著履歷看起來大同小異的應徵者，因為同一批工具產出了同一種風格的報告。當每個人都能生成一份看似合理的報告，真正的分別便回歸到工具本身無法提供的基本功 — 有紀律的方法論、可靠重現漏洞的能力、閱讀源碼的真實深度、清晰而不含糊的書面表達，以及對協調披露規範的理解。</p>
<p>文章為新晉學習者提供的路線圖，相應地並不花巧：基本功先行，工具其次。網絡、作業系統、一門足以細讀審核的腳本語言，以及刻意練習的漏洞類型，仍然是整個教育過程的骨幹。AI 可以加速每一個階段，但只適用於早已知道該問甚麼問題的學習者。若把工具當作理解的替代品，同一套工具只會造就能夠描述漏洞、卻無法在追問下為自己的推理辯護的應徵者 — 而技術面試與 bug bounty 分流所考驗的，正是這一點。</p>
<p>負擔並不止於學習者。招聘經理、師徒計劃與分流團隊面對的是同一個驗證問題：大量措辭精美但流於淺薄的材料。對正在建立或招聘安全團隊的機構而言，現在應該尋求的訊號不再是工具熟練度，而是過程的證據。應徵者能否解釋自己為何測試那些項目？能否界定發現的範圍、評定其影響，並向非專門人士清楚說明？這些問題正日益決定誰能通過篩選、誰只會留在原地。</p>
<p>對整個地區的讀者而言，這種轉變已見於初級職位招聘，以及湧向計劃負責人的大量雷同提交。那個仍然能夠由第一性原理出發推理 — 並以書面證明這一點的應徵者，依然是值得等待的人選。</p>
