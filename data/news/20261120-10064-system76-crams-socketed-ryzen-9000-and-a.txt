<h3>System76 Crams Socketed Ryzen 9000 and a Discrete GPU into a 9.78-Litre Thelio Prime</h3>
<p>System76 this week launched a redesigned Thelio Prime mini desktop, fitting AMD's Ryzen 9000 series into a chassis measuring just 9.78 litres. The machine supports processors up to the Ryzen 9 9900X, discrete graphics options from AMD or NVIDIA, up to 96GB of memory and up to 16TB of NVMe storage. Phoronix has a review sample on hand and says testing is still underway, with full results yet to be published.</p>
<h4>Small box, no sacrificed upgrades</h4>
<p>The 9.78-litre figure is the most interesting number here. Desktops that drop below 10 litres typically give up one of four things: a socketed processor, DIMM slots, storage expansion bays, or a full discrete GPU. According to the configuration details Phoronix compiled from official specifications, the Thelio Prime keeps all four — a swappable Ryzen 9000 CPU, expandable memory, high-capacity NVMe storage and discrete graphics options. In other words, the density gain does not appear to come at the cost of upgradeability.</p>
<p>For users who need local compute power without devoting an entire desk to a tower, that combination of small footprint and expansion room addresses a long-standing gap in the mini-PC market.</p>
<h4>Open-source firmware as a differentiator</h4>
<p>System76 has long made open-source firmware a core selling point, and the Thelio line continues that approach. On a workstation where the processor and other components can be swapped by the owner, open firmware means the boot process can be inspected and modified rather than taken on trust from a closed binary.</p>
<p>For IT teams that care about supply-chain transparency and long-term maintainability, that matters more than benchmark scores — especially when machines are deployed for years and serviced by different people. Being able to read and trace firmware behaviour reduces black-box risk, and it also keeps boot behaviour consistent after hardware swaps, a practical advantage in desktop environments with long upgrade cycles.</p>
<h4>Testing is incomplete — no verdict yet</h4>
<p>Phoronix says it has been testing the Thelio Prime for several days and that early impressions are positive, but full sustained-load, thermal and acoustic data have not been released. Whether the cooling design can handle a high-end Ryzen 9000 processor and a discrete GPU inside a 9.78-litre enclosure over extended workloads remains to be seen in the finished review.</p>
<h4>Market context</h4>
<p>System76 builds to order and assembles its systems in Denver, Colorado; pricing and delivery terms for individual regions are published on the company's configuration page rather than through local distributors.</p>
<p>That places the Thelio Prime in an interesting position relative to familiar alternatives. Apple's Mac mini delivers an extremely small footprint within a closed ecosystem; Minisforum and similar brands compete on price in the x86 mini-PC space but typically trade off expansion or firmware openness; Dell and HP's traditional small-form-factor desktops retain upgradeability at a noticeably larger size. The Thelio Prime aims for the space between them: near mini-PC dimensions, near-traditional desktop upgradeability, and auditable open firmware.</p>
<p><strong>Source:</strong> Phoronix, "System76 Thelio Prime: A Petite But Performant AMD-Powered Linux PC."</p>

<h3>System76 將插槽式 Ryzen 9000 與獨立顯卡裝進 9.78 升 Thelio Prime</h3>
<p>System76 本週推出重新設計的 Thelio Prime 迷你桌面電腦，把 AMD Ryzen 9000 系列處理器裝進體積僅 9.78 升的機箱。該機最高支援 Ryzen 9 9900X 處理器，提供 AMD 或 NVIDIA 獨立顯卡選項，配備最高 96GB 記憶體及最高 16TB NVMe 儲存空間。Phoronix 已取得評測樣機，表示測試仍在進行中，完整結果尚未發布。</p>
<h4>機身細小，升級能力沒有犧牲</h4>
<p>9.78 升是這項規格中最值得留意的數字。體積低於 10 升的桌面電腦，通常要在四者其中之一作出取捨：插槽式處理器、DIMM 插槽、硬盤擴充位，或完整的獨立顯卡。根據 Phoronix 從官方規格整理出的配置資料，Thelio Prime 四項全部保留——可更換的 Ryzen 9000 處理器、可擴充的記憶體、大容量 NVMe 儲存，以及獨立顯卡選項。換言之，密度的提升似乎並未以犧牲升級能力為代價。</p>
<p>對於需要本地運算能力、卻不想讓一台塔式主機佔用整張辦公桌的用戶而言，這種小巧機身與擴充空間兼備的組合，正好填補了 mini-PC 市場長期存在的空缺。</p>
<h4>開源韌體作為差異化賣點</h4>
<p>System76 一直以來都把開源韌體作為核心賣點，Thelio 系列亦延續此方針。在一台處理器及其他組件可由用戶自行更換的工作站上，開源韌體意味著開機流程可以被檢視和修改，而不必盲目信任供應商封閉的二進位程式。</p>
<p>對於關注供應鏈透明度與長期可維護性的 IT 團隊而言，這一點比跑分數字更重要——尤其是當機器需要部署多年、並由不同人員負責維護時。能夠閱讀並追蹤韌體行為可降低黑盒風險，同時確保更換硬件後開機行為保持一致，這對升級週期較長的桌面電腦環境是一項實際優勢。</p>
<h4>測試尚未完成——暫無定論</h4>
<p>Phoronix 表示已對 Thelio Prime 進行數天測試，初步印象正面，但完整的持續負載、散熱與噪音數據尚未發布。散熱設計能否在 9.78 升的機箱內容納高階 Ryzen 9000 處理器與獨立顯卡並應付長時間工作負載，仍有待完整評測結果作實。</p>
<h4>市場定位</h4>
<p>System76 採用按單生產模式，系統於美國科羅拉多州丹佛組裝；各地區的定價與配送條款均於公司配置頁面發布，而非透過當地經銷商發布。</p>
<p>這使 Thelio Prime 相對於市場上熟悉的選擇處於一個有趣的位置。蘋果 Mac mini 在封閉生態系統內提供極小的機身；Minisforum 及同類品牌以價格競爭 x86 mini-PC 市場，但通常在擴充能力或韌體開放度上有所取捨；Dell 與 HP 的傳統小型機箱（SFF）桌面電腦則保留升級能力，但體積明顯較大。Thelio Prime 目標正是這三者之間的空間：接近 mini-PC 的尺寸、接近傳統桌面電腦的升級能力，以及可審計的開源韌體。</p>
