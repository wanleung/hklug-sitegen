<h3>openSUSE Tumbleweed October Roundup: 23 Snapshots Land, KDE Plasma 6.8.0 Arrives</h3>
<p>In its Tumbleweed monthly roundup published on November 1, openSUSE reported that the well-known rolling release had a month heavy on both features and fixes: between October 1 and October 28, 23 snapshots landed in the rolling update queue, bringing a wave of changes to desktop environments and core packages. Across the 31 days of the month, that works out to one snapshot roughly every 1.3 days.</p>
<p>The month's headline updates came from the KDE project. KDE Plasma 6.8.0 and KDE Frameworks 6.31.0 arrived in Tumbleweed together, marking the two most representative version leaps of October. Plasma is KDE's desktop environment core, while Frameworks is its underlying set of libraries; when both move in lockstep, the desktop experience and application development interfaces are typically refreshed within the same batch of snapshots. The Plasma 6 line, which debuted in 2024, completed the migration to Qt 6 and switched to Wayland as the default display protocol with HDR support added; 6.8.0 is a subsequent iteration in that series, and Qt itself advanced to 6.12.0 this month.</p>
<p>Beyond the desktop, core packages saw their own version jumps: Firefox shipped 157.0.1, 158.0 and 158.0.1 in quick succession; the Linux kernel progressed through the three 7.2.x point releases 7.2.9, 7.2.10 and 7.2.11 before crossing into the new 7.3.0 series at month's end; Mesa reached 26.2.5, OpenSSH moved to 10.6p1, Git to 2.56.0, GnuPG to 2.5.24, sqlite3 to 3.54.1, harfbuzz to 15.0.0, and mariadb to 13.0.2. In addition, package patterns no longer recommend installing YaST for fresh installations.</p>
<p><strong>Security fixes were a major part of the month as well.</strong> curl patched two dozen (24) vulnerabilities in one go, libsoup fixed a long list of WebSocket heap overflows, and Pillow addressed 28 issues at once; the three 7.2.x point kernel releases collectively carried 91 CVE fixes. On the Firefox side, the month's security updates correspond to three Mozilla Foundation Security Advisories: MFSA 2026-97 (39 issues) accompanied 157.0, while the 157.0.1 Tumbleweed subsequently carried in its snapshots was a follow-up mitigation bypass fix in file handling rather than a separate advisory release; the remaining two advisories, MFSA 2026-106 and 2026-111, correspond to 158.0 and 158.0.1 respectively. Readers who need to track the full fix lists should still consult openSUSE's official security advisories and the change logs for each snapshot.</p>
<p>For rolling release users, 23 snapshots in a single month is a notably dense cadence. As background, Tumbleweed has long relied on the OpenQA automated testing pipeline to gate releases, which is why the burden of package syncing and regression testing falls on automation. The simultaneous arrival of both KDE versions also gives users planning a Plasma upgrade a clear point of reference. This kind of monthly roundup holds real value for Chinese-language open source communities as background material for planning workstation updates and troubleshooting.</p>
<p>This article was compiled from the <a href="https://news.opensuse.org/2026/11/01/tw-monthly-update-october/">openSUSE official news site's Tumbleweed monthly roundup</a>.</p>

<h3>openSUSE Tumbleweed 十月密集更新：23 次快照落地，KDE Plasma 6.8.0 到貨</h3>
<p>openSUSE 官方新聞站在 11 月 1 日發布的 Tumbleweed 十月度回顧中指出，這個知名的滾動更新發行版在 10 月迎來了功能與修補並重的一個月份：從 10 月 1 日至 10 月 28 日，共有 23 次快照（snapshot）進入滾動更新佇列，為使用者帶來一批桌面環境與基礎套件的重要變動。全月共 31 天，23 次快照相當於平均每 1.3 天就有一次更新落地。</p>
<p>本月最受矚目的更新來自 KDE 社群。回顧指出，KDE Plasma 6.8.0 與 KDE Frameworks 6.31.0 同步抵達 Tumbleweed，是十月份兩項最具代表性的版本躍進。Plasma 是 KDE 的桌面環境核心，Frameworks 則是其底層函式庫集合，兩者版本同步推進，通常意味著桌面體驗與應用程式開發介面會在同一批快照中一起更新。Plasma 6 系列自 2024 年首次亮相以來，已完成向 Qt 6 的遷移，並改以 Wayland 為預設顯示協定、加入 HDR 支援；6.8.0 是此一系列的後續迭代，本月 Qt 也同步推進至 6.12.0。</p>
<p>桌面之外，基礎套件同樣有多項版本躍進：Firefox 接連推出 157.0.1、158.0 與 158.0.1 三個版本；Linux 核心則由 7.2.9、7.2.10、7.2.11 三個 7.2.x 點版本一路推進，並在月底跨入新的 7.3.0 系列；Mesa 來到 26.2.5，OpenSSH 升至 10.6p1，Git 為 2.56.0，GnuPG 為 2.5.24，sqlite3 為 3.54.1，harfbuzz 為 15.0.0，mariadb 則是 13.0.2。此外，套件組合（patterns）在全新安裝時已不再建議安裝 YaST。</p>
<p><strong>資安修補同樣是本月重點。</strong> curl 一次修補了兩打（24 個）漏洞，libsoup 修正了一連串 WebSocket 堆積區溢位（heap overflow），Pillow 則一口氣處理了 28 項問題；Linux 核心本月的三個 7.2.x 點版本合計帶入 91 個 CVE 修正。Firefox 方面，本月的安全更新對應三份 Mozilla 基金會安全公告：MFSA 2026-97（39 項問題）隨 157.0 發布，Tumbleweed 後續在快照中帶入的 157.0.1 則是針對檔案處理繞過防護的追蹤修正，並非獨立安全公告；其餘兩份公告 MFSA 2026-106 與 2026-111 分別對應 158.0 與 158.0.1。讀者若需追蹤完整修正清單，仍應查閱 openSUSE 官方安全性公告頁面與各快照的變更紀錄。</p>
<p>對 rolling release 的使用者而言，單月 23 次快照屬於相當密集的節奏。作為背景補充，Tumbleweed 長期透過 OpenQA 自動化測試把關，套件同步與回歸測試的負擔因此落在自動化流程上。而 KDE 雙版本同時到位，也讓計畫升級 Plasma 的使用者有明確的時間點可供參考。這類月度盤點對中文開源社群同樣具參考價值，可作為規劃工作站更新與排除問題時的背景資料。</p>
