Title: AlmaLinux Unveils Self-Service Certification Portal, Opening Software Validation Path
Content:
<p>The AlmaLinux project has rolled out a major update to its certification ecosystem, launching a centralized online portal and a new self-service application. This marks a significant expansion from its original hardware-only certification program to now include a formal pathway for third-party software validation.</p>
<p>The AlmaLinux Certification Special Interest Group (SIG) first introduced a hardware certification program roughly two years ago. With this latest move, the community-driven distribution provides a single hub for vendors and users to browse and manage certification details for both hardware and software.</p>
<p>The cornerstone of the update is the self-service "self-certify" application. Vendors and developers can install this tool on their own systems to run validation checks, directly verifying and publishing that their products work correctly on AlmaLinux. This process decentralizes what was previously a more centralized, and often costly, validation effort.</p>
<p>This approach is explicitly designed to lower barriers to entry. For smaller vendors, independent software vendors (ISVs), and regional system integrators, traditional vendor-led certification programs can represent significant financial and logistical challenges. AlmaLinux’s new model offers a practical, lower-cost alternative, helping these organizations build customer trust and drive commercial adoption.</p>
<p>Broadening its certification scope to encompass software is a strategic step in AlmaLinux’s evolution as a platform. The development fosters greater ecosystem maturity, building the trust and reliability needed for wider enterprise adoption in production environments.</p>
<p>The launch positions AlmaLinux as a formidable community-driven alternative in the enterprise Linux space. It directly challenges the traditional single-vendor certification model by offering a more open, accessible, and collaborative framework for software and hardware validation.</p>
<hr>
<p>AlmaLinux 項目對其認證生態系統進行了重大更新，推出一個集中化的線上門戶及全新的自助申請系統。此舉標誌著其認證計劃從原先專注於硬件，正式擴展至為第三方軟件驗證提供正式途徑。</p>
<p>AlmaLinux 認證特別興趣小組（SIG）大約兩年前首次推出了硬件認證計劃。透過這次最新舉動，這個社群驅動的發行版為供應商及用戶提供了一個單一樞紐，用以瀏覽和管理硬件與軟件的認證詳情。</p>
<p>本次更新的核心是「自助認證」應用程式。供應商及開發者可在其自有系統上安裝此工具，以進行驗證檢查，直接確認並公佈其產品能在 AlmaLinux 上正常運作。此過程將以往較為集中且往往成本高昂的驗證工作去中心化。</p>
<p>這種方法明確旨在降低入門門檻。對於規模較小的供應商、獨立軟件供應商（ISVs）及區域性系統整合商而言，傳統由供應商主導的認證計劃可能帶來重大的財務及後勤挑戰。AlmaLinux 的新模式提供了一個實用且成本更低的替代方案，協助這些組織建立客戶信任並推動商業採用。</p>
<p>將認證範圍擴展至涵蓋軟件，是 AlmaLinux 作為平台演進的策略性一步。此發展促進了生態系統更趨成熟，並建立起更廣泛採用於生產環境的企業級應用所需的信任及可靠性。</p>
<p>此次推出將 AlmaLinux 定位為企業級 Linux 項目中一個實力雄厚的社群驅動替代方案。透過提供更開放、易於獲取且協作式的軟硬件驗證框架，它直接挑戰了傳統的單一供應商認證模式。</p>
