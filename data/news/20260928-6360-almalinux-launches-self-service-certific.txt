Title: AlmaLinux Launches Self-Service Certification, Decentralizing Enterprise Linux Validation
Content:
<p>The AlmaLinux project is overhauling its ecosystem compatibility program, introducing a self-service certification application and a new public portal. This shift fundamentally decentralizes the validation process, moving it away from centralized testing labs to a vendor-driven model with community oversight.</p>
<p>Two years after launching a hardware certification initiative via its Special Interest Group, AlmaLinux now extends the program to third-party software. The core of this update is a downloadable application that lets vendors and administrators run a standardized suite of tests on their own systems. Results are submitted for peer review, and successful validations are listed on the new public directory at <code>certs.almalinux.org</code>.</p>
<p>This approach stands in stark contrast to the traditional method used by major commercial distributors like Red Hat Enterprise Linux (RHEL). Under that model, partners typically ship hardware or provide software to the vendor's own labs for a formal, and often costly, testing process. AlmaLinux's new system inverts this structure, empowering vendors to perform the validation work themselves while relying on community governance to maintain quality standards.</p>
<p>For small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) in technology centers such as Hong Kong, this could be a significant equalizer. The expense and logistical hurdles of formal certification have often been prohibitive for smaller companies, potentially excluding them from markets where certified compatibility is a key purchasing factor. By offering a free, accessible tool, AlmaLinux aims to broaden participation in its hardware and software ecosystem.</p>
<p>The certification overhaul is a strategic move for AlmaLinux as it solidifies its position as a community-driven enterprise Linux alternative. The new portal functions as both a directory of validated products and a resource for users seeking verified solutions.</p>
<p>The self-certify application guides users through a consistent testing battery, while the subsequent community review process is designed to ensure reliability without centralized overhead. This initiative not only enhances AlmaLinux's commercial appeal to partners but also serves as a case study in scalable open-source governance and fostering sustainable ecosystem growth.</p>
<hr>
<p>AlmaLinux 項目正在全面改革其生態系統兼容性計劃，推出自助式認證申請流程及全新公共入口網站。此舉將驗證過程從根本上去中心化，將其從集中的測試實驗室轉移至由廠商主導、社區監督的模式。</p>
<p>AlmaLinux 兩年前透過其特別興趣小組啟動了硬件認證計劃，現將該計劃擴展至第三方軟件。此次更新的核心是一個可下載的應用程式，允許廠商及管理員在自家系統上執行一組標準化測試。測試結果將提交進行同儕審查，成功通過驗證的產品將被收錄於新的公共目錄 <code>certs.almalinux.org</code>。</p>
<p>此方法與 Red Hat Enterprise Linux (RHEL) 等主要商業發行版採用的傳統模式形成鮮明對比。在傳統模式下，合作夥伴通常需將硬件運送或提供軟件至廠商自身的實驗室，進行正式且往往成本高昂的測試流程。AlmaLinux 的新系統顛覆了此結構，賦予廠商自行執行驗證工作的能力，同時依賴社區治理以維持品質標準。</p>
<p>對於香港等科技中心的中小企業 (SME) 而言，這可能是一個重要的平衡器。正式認證的費用和後勤障礙往往令小型企業望而卻步，可能使其被排除在那些以認證兼容性作為關鍵採購因素的市場之外。透過提供免費且易於使用的工具，AlmaLinux 旨在擴大其硬件及軟件生態系統的參與度。</p>
<p>這次認證改革是 AlmaLinux 巩固其作為社區驅動之企業級 Linux 替代方案地位的戰略性舉措。新入口網站既是已驗證產品的目錄，也是用戶尋求經過核實解決方案的資源。</p>
<p>自助認證應用程式會引導用戶完成一套一致的測試程序，而隨後的社區審查流程旨在確保可靠性，無需集中化的管理開銷。此舉不僅提升了 AlmaLinux 對合作夥伴的商業吸引力，也成為一個可擴展的開源治理模式及促進可持續生態系統增長的案例研究。</p>
