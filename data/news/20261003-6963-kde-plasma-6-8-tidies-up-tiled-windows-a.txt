Title: KDE Plasma 6.8 Tidies Up Tiled Windows Ahead of Release
Content:
<p>KDE Plasma 6.8 is now just over a week from its official release, and the final stretch of development has turned to a batch of last-minute fixes — most notably improvements to how tiled and snapped windows sit together on screen.</p>
<p>A Phoronix report on the changes points to long-standing geometry mismatches in Plasma's tiling and snapping behaviour: tiled windows failing to close gaps completely, edges that do not line up, and the final window in a row or column not filling its remaining space cleanly. These are polish-level corrections rather than new features — the kind of small refinements that tend to surface in the closing days of a development cycle.</p>
<p>The fixes land in an area Plasma has been actively maturing across recent releases. Native tiling has become one of the desktop's clearer selling points on Wayland, where window compositions are less forgiving than they once were, and users coming from tiling window managers such as i3 or Sway generally expect pixel-precise layouts out of the box. Historically, Plasma users had to reach for third-party scripts — Krohnkite being the best known example before it was archived — to approximate that behaviour. Addressing these gaps in the desktop itself, rather than leaving them to community tooling, removes a persistent friction point for anyone running Linux workstations who wants predictable window layouts without maintaining custom scripts.</p>
<p>The same Phoronix report notes that early feature work for Plasma 6.9 is already under way in the next development branch, reportedly including work tracked under the codename "Moose." Details remain provisional until KDE publishes its 6.9 announcements, so it is worth holding judgment on what that cycle will contain until then.</p>
<p>Rollout timing will follow the usual pattern for major KDE releases: rolling distributions such as Arch Linux and its derivatives typically pick up new Plasma versions within days of the official announcement, while fixed-cycle distributions such as Fedora and Ubuntu/Kubuntu generally wait for their next scheduled release. Users on rolling distributions can expect 6.8 shortly after launch; those on fixed-cycle releases should plan to wait for the next cycle or consult upstream KDE repositories directly. Distribution-specific guidance will follow once KDE confirms the release channels.</p>
<p>For a release cycle that has been largely about refinement, these tiling corrections are a good example of the cycle's direction: not headline features, but the kind of fixes that quietly make everyday use more predictable.</p>
<hr>
<p>KDE Plasma 6.8 距離正式發布只餘下一個多星期，開發最後階段已轉向一批最後關頭的修正——當中最突出的是改進平鋪及貼附（snap）視窗在螢幕上的排列方式。</p>
<p>一份 Phoronix 報道指出了 Plasma 平鋪及貼附行為中長期存在的幾何對位問題：平鋪視窗未能完全填補空隙、邊緣未能對齊、以及一行或一列中的最後一個視窗未能乾淨地填滿其剩餘空間。這些屬打磨性質的修正，而非新增功能——正是那類往往在開發周期尾聲才會浮現的細微調整。</p>
<p>這批修正落在 Plasma 過往數個版本持續積極完善的領域。原生平鋪已成為該桌面環境在 Wayland 上其中一項較明顯的賣點，因為在 Wayland 之下，視窗組合的要求比以往更為嚴格，由 i3 或 Sway 等平鋪視窗管理器轉移過來的用戶，一般期望開箱即有像素級精確的佈局。過去，Plasma 用戶往往需要借助第三方腳本——最著名的例子是現已停止維護的 Krohnkite——才能大致重現這種行為。在桌面環境本身處理這些差距，而非將其交給社群工具，為所有使用 Linux 工作站、希望擁有可預測視窗佈局而不必自行維護自訂腳本的人，移除了一個長期摩擦點。</p>
<p>同一份 Phoronix 報告指出，下一條開發分支中，Plasma 6.9 的早期功能開發已展開，據報其中包括以「Moose」代號追蹤的工作。在 KDE 正式公布 6.9 公告之前，所有細節仍屬暫定，因此在此之前不宜過早評論該周期將包含什麼內容。</p>
<p>推出時間將沿用 KDE 大版本發布的慣常模式：Arch Linux 及其衍生版本等 rolling 發行版，通常在官方公布後數天內便會引入新的 Plasma 版本；Fedora、Ubuntu/Kubuntu 等固定周期發行版，一般則會等待下一個既定發布。Rolling 發行版的用戶可以預期在發布後不久便可用上 6.8；固定周期版本的用戶則應預計需等待下一個周期，或直接查閱上游 KDE 倉庫。待 KDE 確認發布渠道後，個別發行版的指引將隨後提供。</p>
<p>對於一個整體以打磨為主的開發周期而言，這些平鋪修正正是該周期方向的好例子：它們並非主打功能，而是那種令人在日常使用中無聲無息地變得更可預測的修正。</p>
