Title: Four enterprise platforms land on CISA's Known Exploited list: which of Expressway, NetWeaver, vCenter and Roller should you patch first?

<h3>Four enterprise platforms land on CISA's Known Exploited list: which of Expressway, NetWeaver, vCenter and Roller should you patch first?</h3>
<p>The U.S. Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) has added four vulnerabilities — spanning network-edge, virtualisation-management, core-application and content-platform software — to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog. Security Affairs reports that the products involved are Cisco Expressway, SAP NetWeaver, Broadcom VMware vCenter and Apache Roller.</p>
<p>For IT teams at Hong Kong enterprises, the significance lies less in U.S. policy than in the triage signal it sends: these flaws have moved from "theoretically exploitable" to "confirmed exploited in the wild." In day-to-day patch prioritisation, KEV membership can carry more weight than a CVSS score — the score measures potential impact, while KEV confirms real-world threat. The ranking and advice below are this publication's own analysis, based on how these products are typically deployed, and are offered as a reference for IT teams.</p>
<h4>Four platforms, four exposure profiles</h4>
<p>The four products differ too widely to be ranked by a single "severity" figure. A more useful approach filters them on three layers: exposure surface, privilege level and data sensitivity.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Cisco Expressway</strong> is typically deployed at the network edge for collaboration and traversal scenarios, and often faces the internet directly. Once an edge device is compromised, the attacker gains a foothold at the perimeter, so this generally goes first.</li>
<li><strong>VMware vCenter</strong> is the virtualisation management plane, holding control over an entire host fleet. Even if it is not directly exposed, a compromise effectively hands over control at the hypervisor layer, placing it next in priority.</li>
<li><strong>SAP NetWeaver</strong> carries core business data and therefore has the highest asset value; its specific vulnerability details should be taken from the relevant CISA KEV entry and the vendor advisory rather than assumed in advance.</li>
<li><strong>Apache Roller</strong> is a content-management application. If it runs only on the internal network it normally ranks last — but if its administration interface is open to the internet, it should be treated as an edge asset and moved up immediately.</li>
</ul>
<h4>Why a Hong Kong team should still track a U.S. list</h4>
<p>Under CISA's Binding Operational Directive 22-01, the remediation mandate applies to U.S. federal agencies and carries no legal force for local firms. In practice, many security teams also treat the KEV catalog as a useful external signal — alongside cyber-insurance requirements, audit frameworks and vendor tooling — and fold it into a weekly vulnerability review rather than chasing news after the fact. Once an item appears on the list, CISA sets a remediation deadline for that entry; readers can confirm the specific due date against each KEV record in the CISA catalog.</p>
<h4>Before the patch arrives, buy time</h4>
<p>Until fixes are available, stopgap measures include restricting management interfaces to IP allowlists or a jump host, moving administration consoles off the internet, and tightening log monitoring on the affected systems. These buy time; they do not substitute for patching. Where a version is no longer supported, isolation or replacement should be evaluated instead of relying on further configuration tweaks.</p>
<p>Specific CVE identifiers, affected versions and per-item remediation deadlines should be confirmed against the CISA KEV catalog entries and the official security advisories from Cisco, SAP, Broadcom and Apache before being queued as patch tickets.</p>

<h3>四款企業平台登上 CISA「已知遭利用」名單：Expressway、NetWeaver、vCenter、Roller 用戶應先修補哪一項？</h3>
<p>美國網絡安全與基礎設施安全局（CISA）把四個漏洞——橫跨網絡邊界、虛擬化管理、核心應用與內容平台軟件——一併納入「已知遭利用漏洞」（Known Exploited Vulnerabilities，KEV）目錄。Security Affairs 報道，涉及產品為 Cisco Expressway、SAP NetWeaver、Broadcom VMware vCenter 以及 Apache Roller。</p>
<p>對香港企業的 IT 團隊而言，重點與其說在美國政策本身，不如說在它發出的排序信號：這些漏洞已從「理論上可被利用」變成「確認在野外遭利用」。在日常修補排序中，列入 KEV 可能比 CVSS 分數更具參考價值——分數衡量的是潛在影響，KEV 確認的則是現實威脅。以下排序與建議屬本刊自行分析，根據這些產品的典型部署方式整理，供 IT 團隊參考。</p>
<h4>四個平台，四種暴露形態</h4>
<p>四款產品的性質差異太大，難以用單一「嚴重性」數字排序。較有用的做法是按三個層面篩選：暴露面、權限層級與資料敏感度。</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Cisco Expressway</strong> 通常部署於網絡邊界，用於協作與穿透場景，往往直接面向互聯網。邊界設備一旦被攻陷，攻擊者即在周界取得據點，因此一般應排在最前。</li>
<li><strong>VMware vCenter</strong> 是虛擬化管理平面，掌握整個主機群的控制權。即使不直接對外暴露，一旦失陷等同交出 hypervisor 層的控制權，優先級排在其次。</li>
<li><strong>SAP NetWeaver</strong> 承載核心業務資料，資產價值最高；其具體漏洞細節應以相關 CISA KEV 條目及廠商安全公告為準，不宜在未核實前預設。</li>
<li><strong>Apache Roller</strong> 屬內容管理應用。若只在內網運行，通常排在最後；但若其管理介面對互聯網開放，就應按邊界資產處理，即時前移。</li>
</ul>
<h4>為何香港團隊仍應跟進一份美國名單</h4>
<p>根據 CISA 的《具約束力行動指令 22-01》（Binding Operational Directive 22-01），該修補規定只適用於美國聯邦機構，對本港企業不具法律約束力。不過在實務上，不少安全團隊也把 KEV 目錄視為有用的外部信號——連同網絡保險要求、稽核框架與廠商工具一併參考，將其納入每週例行的漏洞檢視，而非事後逐次追新聞。一旦項目出現在名單上，CISA 會為該條目訂定修補限期；讀者可逐一對照 CISA 目錄中的 KEV 條目，核實具體到期日。</p>
<h4>補丁到位之前，先爭取時間</h4>
<p>在修復方案推出前，可用的過渡措施包括：將管理介面限於 IP 白名單或跳板主機存取、把管理主控台移出互聯網、加強受影響系統的日誌監控。這些措施能爭取時間，但不能取代修補。對已停止支援的版本，則應評估隔離或更換，而非倚賴進一步的設定微調。</p>
<p>具體 CVE 編號、受影響版本與各項修補到期日，應先對照 CISA KEV 目錄條目，以及 Cisco、SAP、Broadcom 與 Apache 的官方安全公告核實，再排入修補工單。</p>
