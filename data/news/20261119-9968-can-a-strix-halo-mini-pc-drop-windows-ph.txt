Title: Can a Strix Halo Mini PC Drop Windows? Phoronix Puts Ubuntu 26.10 to the Test
Content:
<p>For IT buyers in Hong Kong and elsewhere weighing a compact Linux workstation, the question is no longer whether Linux runs on high-end AMD hardware — it is whether it runs <em>well enough</em> to justify dropping Windows entirely. A new Phoronix review puts that question to a concrete machine: the GMKtec EVO-X2 mini PC, powered by AMD's Ryzen AI Max+ 395 "Strix Halo" SoC and shipped with Windows 11 Pro, tested against Ubuntu 26.10 across gaming, AI and creator workloads.</p>
<p>The framing matters more than any single score. Strix Halo is the first AMD platform to pair a large CPU/GPU/NPU complex with the kind of memory bandwidth that historically forced Linux users to keep a Windows partition for GPU-accelerated and NPU work. AMD's published specifications list configurations with up to 128GB of unified memory — a figure that makes it plausible as a single-OS machine rather than a dual-boot compromise.</p>
<p><strong>What the test actually covers</strong></p>
<p>Phoronix benchmarked the review sample — an EVO-X2 pre-loaded with Windows 11 Pro — against Ubuntu 26.10, spanning three workload families: gaming, AI inference, and creator applications. That breadth is the useful part for buyers. A machine that trades blows in games but loses badly in AI acceleration is a different proposition from one that does the reverse, and the review's per-workload breakdown is where the answer to "can this be my only OS?" lives. The result tables, test configuration and methodology all sit in the Phoronix piece itself, and they are the right place to map scores onto your own workload: identify the two or three applications you use daily, find those rows, and ignore the rest.</p>
<p><strong>Important caveats before you read the numbers</strong></p>
<p>Two caveats apply. First, Ubuntu 26.10 is a short-term release with roughly nine months of standard support, whereas 24.04 LTS carries updates through 2029. If you are provisioning a machine you intend to keep in service for years, it is worth checking whether the Strix Halo support landing in 26.10's kernel and Mesa stack has since reached an LTS channel — or whether you plan to upgrade in cycle. Second, Ubuntu 26.10's kernel and graphics stack are still moving under this hardware. Any figures reported now are a snapshot of where the platform is today, not a ceiling on where it will land; matured drivers can and do change Linux's position relative to Windows.</p>
<p><strong>The verdict: no clear winner</strong></p>
<p>The review's overall verdict is that neither OS wins outright: its results are workload-dependent, and the right choice depends on whether your day-to-day tools are the ones Linux handles well on this chip. A reader who lives in compilers, containers and terminals will weigh the results differently from one who needs specific GPU-accelerated creative suites or Windows-only AI tooling.</p>
<p>What the review does establish is the substantive question local IT buyers should be asking: whether a Strix Halo mini PC can retire the Windows partition for good — and the answer, for now, is "it depends on your workload." Check your workloads against the Phoronix tables before committing.</p>
<hr>
<p>對於香港及其他地區正在考慮購入緊湊型 Linux 工作站的 IT 採購者而言，問題已不再是 Linux 能否在高端 AMD 硬件上運行——而是它是否運行得<em>足夠好</em>，足以令你放棄 Windows。一篇新的 Phoronix 評測將此問題套用到一台具體機器上：GMKtec EVO-X2 mini PC，搭載 AMD Ryzen AI Max+ 395「Strix Halo」SoC，出廠預載 Windows 11 Pro，並在遊戲、AI 及創作工作負載方面與 Ubuntu 26.10 進行對比測試。</p>
<p>框架的意義比任何單一分數更為重要。Strix Halo 是首個將大型 CPU/GPU/NPU 綜合體與過往令 Linux 用戶不得不保留 Windows 分區以進行 GPU 加速及 NPU 工作的記憶體帶寬結合的 AMD 平台。AMD 公布的規格列出最高達 128GB 統一記憶體的配置——這數字令單一作業系統運行變得可行，而非雙啟動的折衷方案。</p>
<p><strong>測試實際涵蓋什麼</strong></p>
<p>Phoronix 以評測樣本——一台預載 Windows 11 Pro 的 EVO-X2——對比 Ubuntu 26.10 進行基準測試，涵蓋三大類工作負載：遊戲、AI 推論及創作應用。對採購者而言，這種廣度正是最有價值之處。一台在遊戲方面不分上下但在 AI 加速方面慘敗的機器，與表現恰好相反的機器，是截然不同的選擇；而評測按工作負載劃分的細項，正是「這能否成為我唯一的作業系統？」這個問題的答案所在。結果表格、測試配置及方法論均載於 Phoronix 文章本身，而那正是將分數對應到你自身工作負載的正確地方：識別你每日使用的兩三個應用程式，找出相應的行，其餘一概忽略。</p>
<p><strong>閱讀數據前的重要注意事項</strong></p>
<p>有兩點須留意。第一，Ubuntu 26.10 是短期版本，標準支援期限約九個月，而 24.04 LTS 的更新則延續至 2029 年。若你正部署一台打算使用多年的機器，值得查核 Strix Halo 在 26.10 kernel 及 Mesa stack 中的支援是否已進入 LTS 通道——抑或你計劃按周期升級。第二，Ubuntu 26.10 的 kernel 及圖形 stack 在此硬件上仍持續演進。目前公布的任何數據都是平台現階段的快照，而非最終上限；成熟的驅動程序確實能夠改變 Linux 相對於 Windows 的位置。</p>
<p><strong>結論：沒有明顯勝方</strong></p>
<p>評測的整體結論是兩者並無全面勝方：結果取決於工作負載，正確選擇取決於你日常使用的工具是否屬於 Linux 在這顆晶片上處理得好的類別。長期活在 compiler、container 及 terminal 中的讀者，與需要特定 GPU 加速創作套件或僅限 Windows 的 AI 工具的讀者，對結果的權衡方式會截然不同。</p>
<p>評測確實確立的是本地 IT 採購者應當提出的實質問題：一台 Strix Halo mini PC 能否永久撤除 Windows 分區——而目前的答案是「取決於你的工作負載」。在作決定之前，先對照 Phoronix 的表格檢視你的工作負載。</p>
