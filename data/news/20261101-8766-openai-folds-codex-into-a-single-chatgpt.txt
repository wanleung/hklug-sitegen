Title: OpenAI folds Codex into a single ChatGPT usage pool as Pro limits double
Content:
<p>OpenAI is merging the separate usage allowances for ChatGPT and Codex into a single shared pool, while also folding capabilities from its ChatGPT Work offering into the standard Chat product and doubling usage limits on its Pro tier, according to BleepingComputer.</p>
<p>The pooled-allowance change is the more consequential of the announcements for anyone who pays for the tools. Until now, ChatGPT conversations and Codex coding sessions drew down independent quotas, meaning a developer could exhaust one while the other still had headroom — or, conversely, sit on unused capacity in one product while paying for the other. Combining them into one allowance removes that friction, but it also collapses two previously separate budget lines into one. Teams that plan around monthly AI spend will now need to govern chat and coding usage as a single item: a heavy run of Codex work can consume the budget that would otherwise have covered conversational queries, and vice versa.</p>
<p>The second half of the announcement cuts the other way. Promoting Work features into standard Chat potentially erodes one of the reasons an organisation would pay separately for the Work tier in the first place. If the collaborative and administrative capabilities that once distinguished Work now appear in ordinary ChatGPT, the remaining differentiation for a paid enterprise layer becomes an open question — one the source material does not answer. Whether Work plans retain exclusive compliance, data-retention and administrative controls, or whether those too migrate downward, is not stated.</p>
<p>The headline figure — a doubling of Pro limits — will grab attention, but it is difficult to translate into practical budgeting terms without knowing the baseline. Exact per-plan limit figures are not published in the report, so a "doubled" allowance is meaningless for cost modelling until the underlying numbers are known. Organisations weighing a tier change should confirm current limits directly with OpenAI before adjusting subscriptions rather than infer them from the multiplier.</p>
<p>The same gap applies to rollout geography. Regional availability is not covered in the source, including whether Hong Kong receives the changes on the same schedule as the general rollout. Local teams will need to check OpenAI's own rollout notes for confirmation before assuming the new allowances apply to their accounts on day one.</p>
<p>For readers in Hong Kong budgeting AI tooling for development teams, the practical takeaway is straightforward: the pooled-usage change makes forecasting both simpler and trickier at once. Simpler, because there is one quota to track instead of two; trickier, because the boundary between "chat work" and "coding work" no longer maps to a boundary in the budget. Any internal chargeback model that split spend by product will need revisiting.</p>
<p>What to watch next falls into three buckets. First, the actual per-plan numbers, which determine whether the Pro increase materially changes the value proposition or merely offsets earlier increases. Second, regional availability, including Hong Kong, confirmed against OpenAI's rollout notes rather than assumed from a global announcement. Third, the positioning of the enterprise tier — specifically what Work plans will still offer exclusively once their features land in standard Chat. That answer will shape whether this is a feature parity move or the beginning of a tier consolidation.</p>
<p>Specific per-plan limits and regional availability were not detailed in the report; both remain questions for OpenAI to answer directly.</p>
<hr>
<p>據 BleepingComputer 報道，OpenAI 正將 ChatGPT 與 Codex 各自獨立的用量限額合併為單一共用池，同時把 ChatGPT Work 產品的功能併入標準 Chat 產品，並將 Pro 方案的用量限額加倍。</p>
<p>對於所有付費用戶而言，用量合併的變動是各項公布中影響較為深遠的一項。在此之前，ChatGPT 對話與 Codex 編程工作各自消耗獨立配額，意味著開發者可能耗盡其中一項配額，而另一項仍有餘量；反過來說，也可能一邊為另一個產品付費，一邊閒置著某個產品未用盡的容量。將兩者合併為單一限額可消除這種摩擦，但同時也把兩條原本獨立的預算開支合而為一。按每月 AI 支出編制預算的團隊，日後須把對話與編程用量視為單一項目來管理：密集使用 Codex 工作可能耗用原本用於對話查詢的預算，反之亦然。</p>
<p>公布內容的另一半則朝相反方向發展。將 Work 功能提升至標準 Chat，可能削弱機構最初願意為 Work 方案額外付費的理由之一。若原先令 Work 層級與眾不同的協作與管理功能如今出現於一般 ChatGPT，付費企業層級餘下的差異化優勢便成疑問——原始報道並未解答此問題。Work 方案是否保留獨佔的合規、資料留存及管理控制權限，抑或這些功能同樣會向下遷移，報道均未言明。</p>
<p>標題數字——Pro 限額倍增——固然吸睛，但在未知基準數值的情況下，難以轉化為實際的預算規劃。報告未刊出各方案的確切限額數字，因此在掌握底層數據之前，「倍增」的限額對成本建模而言毫無意義。考慮調整方案層級的機構，應在修改訂閱前直接向 OpenAI 確認現行限額，而非從倍數推算。</p>
<p>同樣的資訊空缺也適用於推出地區。原始報道未涵蓋各地區的可用性，包括香港是否與全球推出同步接收有關變動。本地團隊在假設新限額於首日適用於其帳戶之前，須查閱 OpenAI 官方的推出說明以作確認。</p>
<p>對於需要為開發團隊編制 AI 工具預算的香港讀者，實際結論相當直接：用量合併使預測同時變得更簡單也更棘手。更簡單，因為只需追蹤一項配額而非兩項；更棘手，因為「對話工作」與「編程工作」之間的界線，已不再對應預算上的界線。任何按產品拆分支出的內部成本分攤模式，均須重新檢視。</p>
<p>接下來值得關注的要點分為三方面。第一，各方案的實際數字，這將決定 Pro 加碼是實質改變價值主張，抑或僅是抵消早前的增幅。第二，各地區可用性（包括香港），須以 OpenAI 的推出說明核實，而非從全球公布中假設。第三，企業層級的定位——具體而言，Work 功能落入標準 Chat 之後，Work 方案仍會獨佔提供哪些內容。這個答案將決定此舉是一次功能對齊（feature parity），還是層級整合的開端。</p>
<p>報告未詳述各方案的具體限額及地區可用性；兩者仍有待 OpenAI 直接回應。</p>
