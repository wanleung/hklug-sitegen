Title: ARCTIC Dual-Licenses Fan Controller Driver With BSD-2-Clause, Opening Doors for Cross-Platform Use
Content:
<p>Hardware vendor ARCTIC has expanded the reach of its open-source fan controller driver by adding a permissive BSD-2-Clause license alongside its original GPL release. This dual-licensing approach bridges a significant legal divide between the Linux and BSD open-source ecosystems.</p>
<p>As reported by Phoronix, the driver source code is now available under both the GNU General Public License and the more flexible BSD-2-Clause license. This addition directly addresses a well-documented friction point: GPL-licensed code cannot be incorporated into BSD-licensed operating system kernels, such as those in FreeBSD or OpenBSD, due to license incompatibility.</p>
<p>The decision builds on an already notable pattern of proactive community engagement from ARCTIC. The company previously broke from typical industry practice by developing and submitting a mainline Linux driver for its Fan Controller peripheral before the product launched—a rarity for an enthusiast-oriented hardware vendor, where driver support is typically left to the community to develop.</p>
<p>The new dual-licensing extends that support philosophy across platform boundaries. For developers and maintainers in the BSD ecosystem—a space often lacking vendor-provided drivers for modern hardware—the BSD-2-Clause option provides a clean, legally unencumbered foundation. It enables direct integration and modification without workarounds or complete reimplementation.</p>
<p>By adopting this model, ARCTIC demonstrates a pragmatic approach to open-source enablement that acknowledges the diversity of licensing frameworks across the broader free software community. The move lowers the barrier for the driver to be ported, maintained, and improved on platforms beyond Linux, ultimately benefiting a wider audience of users and developers.</p>
<hr>
<p>硬體製造商 ARCTIC 擴展了其開源風扇控制器驅動程式的覆蓋範圍，在原有 GPL 版本基礎上新增寬鬆的 BSD-2-Clause 許可證。這項雙重授權策略彌合了 Linux 與 BSD 開源生態系統之間的重大法律分歧。</p>
<p>據 Phoronix 報導，該驅動程式原始碼現時同時以 GNU 通用公共許可證及更靈活的 BSD-2-Clause 許可證提供。此舉直接解決了一項有據可查的摩擦點：由於授權不相容，以 GPL 授權的代碼無法被整合到 FreeBSD 或 OpenBSD 等 BSD 授權的作業系統核心中。</p>
<p>這項決定延續了 ARCTIC 一貫值得肯定的積極社區參與模式。該公司先前打破典型業界做法，在產品正式推出前便開發並提交主線 Linux 驅動程式——對一家面向發燒友的硬體製造商而言實屬罕見，因為此類驅動支援通常留待社區自行開發。</p>
<p>新的雙重授權將此支援理念延伸至跨平台範疇。對 BSD 生態系統中的開發者和維護者——一個常常缺乏供應商提供現代硬體驅動的領域——BSD-2-Clause 選項提供了一個乾淨、不受法律束縛的基礎。它使直接整合和修改成為可能，無需採用變通方案或完全重新實作。</p>
<p>透過採用此模式，ARCTIC 展現了務實的開源推動方針，承認更廣泛的自由軟件社群中授權框架的多樣性。此舉降低了驅動程式在 Linux 以外平台被移植、維護和改良的門檻，最終惠及更廣泛的用戶和開發者群體。</p>
