Title: Drupal warns attackers are hijacking unpatched sites through new access-bypass flaw
Content:
<p>Attackers are actively exploiting a critical access-bypass vulnerability in Drupal to break into and take over unpatched websites, the project's security team has warned. Exploitation is confirmed in the wild — this is not a theoretical risk waiting for a proof-of-concept, and unpatched sites are being targeted now.</p>
<p>The warning lands as the second critical Drupal flaw to be exploited in attacks in a short window, a stretch that undercuts any assumption that the earlier advisory closed the book on CMS patching. The flaw was disclosed through Drupal's SA-CORE advisory channel, is rated critical, and lets an unauthenticated attacker bypass access controls — a route to unauthorised entry that can escalate into full site takeover (站點劫持) on installations that have not applied the patch.</p>
<p><strong>Patch first, investigate later</strong></p>
<p>Drupal has published no workaround short of upgrading. Administrators should apply the released fixes immediately rather than queue them behind a maintenance window — the standard defender logic when a flaw is confirmed as being abused in the wild is "patch first, investigate afterwards" (先修補、後調查).</p>
<p>Sites running currently supported core branches should upgrade to the patched releases identified in the SA-CORE advisory; the advisory is the authoritative reference for affected and fixed version numbers. Organisations running unsupported or end-of-life (EOL) versions face a harder reality: those branches receive no fix at all, and migration to a supported release is the only remediation path.</p>
<p>For teams in the middle of triage, the practical checks are straightforward:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Review access and admin logs</strong> for unexpected authentication attempts or sessions from unfamiliar addresses.</li>
<li><strong>Look for unfamiliar administrator accounts</strong> or privilege changes that nobody on the team made.</li>
<li><strong>Restrict and harden admin interfaces</strong> — network-level restrictions (網絡層級限制) on where the admin path is reachable, plus enforced multi-factor authentication (多因素驗證, MFA), reduce the value of a bypass even if one exists.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Why this matters beyond the single advisory</strong></p>
<p>The broader signal is cadence. Two actively exploited critical flaws in quick succession is a reminder that content management system security is a continuous process, not a one-off task. Organisations that treated the earlier exploited advisory as a completed ticket item now have concrete evidence that the patch cycle does not pause.</p>
<p>That matters for IT and web operations teams in Hong Kong maintaining Drupal-based sites, as it does for operators everywhere: the window between disclosure and mass exploitation of unpatched CMS installations is short, and inventory accuracy — knowing exactly which core version runs where — determines whether a patch can be pushed in hours or days.</p>
<p>The Drupal security team's message is unambiguous, and it leaves teams four immediate steps: verify current core versions against the patched releases listed in the SA-CORE advisory, patch without delay, review access and admin logs for signs of compromise, and put any unsupported or EOL installation on a migration plan rather than a waiting list.</p>
<hr>
<p>Drupal 專案安全團隊警告，攻擊者正積極利用一項嚴重的存取繞過（access-bypass）漏洞，入侵並接管未修補的網站。該漏洞已確認在現實環境中遭利用——這並非等待概念驗證（proof-of-concept）的理論風險，未修補的網站正遭受即時攻擊。</p>
<p>此警告距離上一個 Drupal 嚴重漏洞遭攻擊者利用僅相隔極短時間，是短期內第二宗遭積極利用的 critical 漏洞，動搖了此前「一紙安全通告已令 CMS 修補工作告一段落」的假設。該漏洞透過 Drupal 的 SA-CORE 安全通告渠道披露，評級為 critical，可讓未經認證的攻擊者繞過存取控制——這條未經授權入侵的途徑，可進一步升級為對未套用修補程式的安裝進行全面站點劫持。</p>
<p><strong>先修補、後調查</strong></p>
<p>Drupal 並未提供升級以外的任何權宜措施。管理員應立即套用已發布的修補程式，而非將其排入下一個維護時段——當漏洞確認已在現實環境中遭濫用時，防禦方的標準做法是「先修補、後調查」（patch first, investigate afterwards）。</p>
<p>目前仍受支援的 core 分支，應升級至 SA-CORE 通告所列明的已修補版本；該通告是受影響版本及已修復版本編號的權威參考。仍在使用不受支援或已達終止支援（end-of-life, EOL）版本的機構，則面對更嚴峻的現實：這些分支完全不會獲得修補，遷移至受支援的版本是唯一的補救途徑。</p>
<p>對於正進行分類評估（triage）的團隊，實際檢查工作相當直接：</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>檢視存取及管理日誌</strong>，查找來自陌生地址的異常認證嘗試或 session。</li>
<li><strong>尋找非團隊成員建立的管理員帳戶</strong>或權限變更。</li>
<li><strong>限制及強化管理介面</strong>——以網絡層級限制（network-level restrictions）規限管理路徑的可達範圍，並強制執行多因素驗證（multi-factor authentication, MFA），即使繞過漏洞存在，也能降低其價值。</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>為何此事遠超單一通告的意義</strong></p>
<p>更宏觀的訊號在於漏洞出現的節奏。兩宗遭積極利用的 critical 漏洞接連出現，提醒業界內容管理系統（content management system）安全是一項持續進行的流程，而非一次性任務。此前將遭利用的通告視為已完成事項的機構，如今有了具體證據，證明修補周期不會因此暫停。</p>
<p>這對於香港維護 Drupal 網站的 IT 及網頁營運團隊同樣重要，正如對各地營運者一樣：從漏洞披露到未修補 CMS 安裝遭大規模利用，其間窗口極短，而資產清單的準確性——清楚掌握何處運行哪個 core 版本——將決定修補程式能在數小時還是數天內推送出去。</p>
<p>Drupal 安全團隊的訊息毫不含糊，並為團隊列出了四項即時行動：核對目前 core 版本與 SA-CORE 通告所列已修補版本、毫不延遲地進行修補、檢視存取及管理日誌尋找入侵跡象，以及為任何不受支援或 EOL 的安裝訂立遷移計劃，而非將其置於等待名單之上。</p>
