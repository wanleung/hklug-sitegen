Title: Comma.ai Unveils Open-Firmware PCIe-to-USB4 Docks for Portable Compute Workloads
Content:
<p>Comma.ai founder George Hotz has announced the Chestnut and Tiny Chestnut external compute docks, distinguishing the hardware with a fully open-source firmware stack. According to the announcement, the enclosures are built around a PCIe Gen4 x4 to USB4 bridge and will be offered with an AMD Radeon RX 9060 8GB GPU configuration. The project positions the docks as a reference platform for Linux development and machine learning workloads.</p>
<p>Both models route PCIe Gen4 x4 signals over a single USB4 connection. This architecture is designed to bypass the bandwidth limitations of traditional external GPU enclosures, delivering near-desktop performance for graphics rendering, AI/ML inference, and CI/CD pipelines. By adhering to the USB4 standard, the hardware maintains compatibility with modern laptops and mini workstations without requiring proprietary host controllers.</p>
<p>The release publishes the complete controller firmware under an open license. Conventional eGPU docks typically depend on closed-source microcontroller code, which has historically fragmented Linux driver support and delayed security patches. Comma.ai’s open stack enables independent code audits, community-driven maintenance, and direct configuration of thermal and power profiles. The company states this approach is intended to ensure long-term kernel compatibility and reduce reliance on manufacturer update cycles.</p>
<p>Commercial and technical specifications remain undisclosed. Pricing, retail availability, power delivery limits, active cooling capacity, and maximum GPU clearance have not been published. The project has also not released formal contribution guidelines, a firmware update cadence, or a long-term maintenance roadmap. Independent community testing will be required to verify real-world throughput, latency, and cross-distribution Linux compatibility before the hardware can be evaluated for production deployments.</p>
<hr>
<p>Comma.ai 創辦人 George Hotz 宣佈推出 Chestnut 與 Tiny Chestnut 外置運算擴展底座，並以完全開放源碼的韌體堆疊作為該硬件的主要特色。根據公佈內容，該機箱建基於 PCIe Gen4 x4 至 USB4 橋接架構，並將提供配備 AMD Radeon RX 9060 8GB GPU 的配置選項。該項目將擴展底座定位為面向 Linux 開發及機器學習工作負載的參考平台。</p>
<p>兩款型號均透過單一 USB4 連接線傳輸 PCIe Gen4 x4 訊號。此架構旨在繞過傳統外置 GPU 機箱的頻寬限制，為圖形渲染、AI/ML 推論及 CI/CD pipeline 提供接近桌面級的性能。透過遵循 USB4 標準，該硬件能與現代手提電腦及迷你工作站保持兼容，同時無需依賴專有主機控制器。</p>
<p>是次發佈以開放授權公開完整的控制器韌體。傳統 eGPU 擴展底座通常依賴閉源微控制器代碼，歷史上此舉往往導致 Linux 驅動程式支援碎片化，並令安全修補程式延遲。Comma.ai 的開放堆疊允許進行獨立代碼審計、由社群驅動的維護，以及直接配置散熱與電源設定。公司表示，此做法旨在確保長期的核心兼容性，並降低對原廠更新週期的依賴。</p>
<p>多項商業與技術規格仍未公開。定價、零售供應情況、供電上限、主動散熱能力及最大 GPU 容納空間尚未公佈。該項目亦未發佈正式的貢獻指引、韌體更新週期，或長遠的維護路線圖。在評估該硬件是否適合用於生產環境部署前，仍需進行獨立社群測試，以驗證實際吞吐量、延遲表現及跨發行版 Linux 的兼容性。</p>
