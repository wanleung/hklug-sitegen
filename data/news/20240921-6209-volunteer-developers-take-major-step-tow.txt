Title: Volunteer Developers Take Major Step Toward Native Linux Audio for Apple Silicon
Content:
<p>A major effort to bring official Linux audio support for Apple Silicon Macs into the mainline kernel has entered a critical review phase. A set of 28 patches has been submitted to the Linux kernel mailing list, aiming to integrate working speaker and headset jack support directly into the standard kernel used by all major distributions.</p>
<p>This submission marks a pivotal transition from a specialized, downstream workaround to the universal upstream codebase. The work is built on years of reverse engineering by the Asahi Linux project, which has successfully provided audio functionality through its custom kernel builds. However, that solution has remained outside the official Linux kernel, requiring users to rely on a non-standard software stack.</p>
<p>Mainline integration is the key milestone, promising universal availability, security, and long-term maintenance. Downstream patches place a burden on users and distributors, whereas mainline support ensures the drivers are reviewed, maintained, and improved by the core kernel community, becoming a standard component for all users on compatible hardware.</p>
<p>The patches now enter a rigorous, peer-driven review process. Kernel maintainers will scrutinize the code for quality, security, and architectural integrity. This process is essential but has no guaranteed timeline; revisions or delays are possible based on community feedback.</p>
<p>The journey from a volunteer project's hardware workaround to a potential mainline contribution exemplifies the open-source model's strength. It shows how community-driven innovation can solve complex problems and then be contributed back to benefit the entire ecosystem.</p>
<p>For IT professionals, this progress is a significant positive indicator. While audio support today may depend on the Asahi Linux kernel, the potential for these capabilities to enter mainline Linux would greatly simplify deployment and maintenance across diverse environments.</p>
<hr>
<p>一項旨在將 Apple Silicon Mac 的官方 Linux 音訊支持納入主線 kernel 的重大努力，已進入關鍵審查階段。一套包含 28 個補丁的提交已送交 Linux kernel 郵件列表，旨在將可用的揚聲器和耳機插孔支持直接整合至所有主要發行版使用的標準 kernel 中。</p>
<p>此次提交標誌著從專門的、下游變通方案向上游通用代碼庫的關鍵過渡。這項工作建立在 Asahi Linux 項目多年逆向工程的基礎上，該項目已透過其自訂 kernel 編譯成功提供了音訊功能。然而，該解決方案一直位於官方 Linux kernel 之外，要求用戶依賴非標準的軟件架構。</p>
<p>整合至主線是關鍵里程碑，承諾實現普遍可用性、安全性及長期維護。下游補丁給用戶和發行版帶來負擔，而主線支持則確保驅動程式經過核心 kernel 社區的審查、維護和改進，成為所有相容硬件上用戶的標準組件。</p>
<p>這些補丁現進入嚴格的、同行驅動的審查過程。Kernel 維護者將仔細審查代碼的品質、安全性和架構完整性。此過程至關重要，但無保證時間表；根據社區回饋，可能需要修改或面臨延遲。</p>
<p>從志願項目的硬件變通方案到可能的主線貢獻，這一歷程體現了開放源碼模式的優勢。它展示了社區驅動的創新如何能解決複雜問題，然後貢獻回去以惠及整個生態系統。</p>
<p>對於 IT 專業人士而言，此進展是一個重要的正面指標。雖然目前的音訊支持可能依賴於 Asahi Linux kernel，但這些功能潛在地進入主線 Linux，將大大簡化跨不同環境的部署和維護。</p>
