Title: Purism Unveils Librem 5 v2 Plans, Betting on RISC-V for Its Next Smartphone
Content:
<p>Purism has announced intentions to build a successor to its Librem 5 privacy-focused smartphone, with the new device — billed as the Librem 5 v2 — set to move away from its ARM-based NXP i.MX 8M Quad processor to a RISC-V SoC. Phoronix carried the announcement, which is best read as a statement of direction rather than a product launch, given the company's track record.</p>
<h4>A history that warrants caution</h4>
<p>The original Librem 5 became one of the most delay-plagued projects in the Linux mobile space. As Phoronix and others have documented, Purism repeatedly pushed back shipping dates, revised hardware mid-stream, and asked supporters to wait through multiple production runs before most orders actually arrived. Those delays are the essential backdrop to this week's news: an announcement of a v2 device says very little about when — or whether — units will reach customers on any particular schedule.</p>
<p>That history is why the framing matters. This is a plan, not a pipeline.</p>
<h4>Why RISC-V</h4>
<p>The architectural shift is the substantive part of the story. RISC-V is an open instruction set architecture, and for a company whose entire pitch rests on auditable, user-sovereign hardware, the appeal is obvious: an open ISA aligns with the same philosophy that drives Purism's open-source firmware and hardware schematics. It also sidesteps the licensing constraints associated with more entrenched architectures.</p>
<p>RISC-V is no longer a curiosity confined to microcontrollers. The architecture already holds a footprint in embedded systems, single-board computers and enthusiast boards — but a phone form factor is a different order of challenge, and a meaningful milestone if delivered.</p>
<p>The trade-offs are equally clear. Mobile-class RISC-V system-on-chips remain scarce, and the supporting software stack — GPU drivers, modem integration, power management — is less mature than what exists for established ARM parts. Those gaps have historically been the hardest part of shipping a Linux phone, and they do not disappear because the ISA is open.</p>
<h4>A crowded field, little to show for it</h4>
<p>Purism is not alone in trying to deliver a Linux-based smartphone. The PinePhone and F(x)tec's slider devices offer alternative hardware, while mobile environments including Phosh, Plasma Mobile and Ubuntu Touch compete to provide a usable phone experience. What none of them have yet delivered is a mainstream-competitive device at scale — a gap that the Librem 5 itself was meant to close, and largely did not.</p>
<p>For the open-source community, the announcement is worth watching on two levels. First, RISC-V in a phone form factor would be a meaningful data point for the architecture's progress beyond embedded and enthusiast boards. Second, if Purism can demonstrate improved schedule discipline on v2, it would suggest lessons learned from the original campaign's difficulties.</p>
<p>Whether either outcome materialises remains to be seen. For now, the Librem 5 v2 exists as a roadmap item — one with an interesting architectural direction, and a company behind it whose delivery record encourages a healthy dose of scepticism.</p>

<p>Purism 宣布有意為其 Librem 5 隱私重視型智能手機推出後續機型，新設備據稱命名為 Librem 5 v2，將由現有的 ARM 架構 NXP i.MX 8M Quad 處理器轉向採用 RISC-V SoC。Phoronix 報道了此項公布，考慮到該公司的往績，這最好理解為一份方向聲明，而非產品發布。</p>
<h4>值得警惕的歷史</h4>
<p>初代 Librem 5 成為 Linux 流動裝置領域中延誤最嚴重的項目之一。正如 Phoronix 及其他媒體所記錄，Purism 多次推遲出貨日期、中途修改硬件設計，並要求支持者等待多個生產批次才令大多數訂單真正到手。這些延誤正是本周消息的重要背景：宣布一台 v2 設備，對於產品何時——甚至是否——能按任何特定時間表送達客戶，其實透露不了多少資訊。</p>
<p>正因這段歷史，這份計劃的表述方式才至關重要。這是一項計劃，而非一條生產線。</p>
<h4>為何選用 RISC-V</h4>
<p>架構轉向才是這則新聞的實質所在。RISC-V 是一套開放的指令集架構（ISA），對於一家整個賣點都建立在可審計、用戶主導硬件之上的公司來說，其吸引力顯而易見：開放 ISA 與驅動 Purism 開源 firmware 及硬件電路圖的理念一脈相承，同時也避開了與更根深蒂固的架構相關的授權限制。</p>
<p>RISC-V 已不再是局限於微控制器的新奇事物。該架構在嵌入式系統、單板電腦和愛好者開發板中已佔有一席之地——但手機形態則是另一個量級的挑戰，若能實現，將是一個意義重大的里程碑。</p>
<p>取捨同樣清楚。流動級 RISC-V system-on-chips 仍然稀缺，而配套的軟件堆棧——GPU 驅動程式、modem 整合、電源管理——亦不如既有 ARM 部件成熟。這些缺口歷來是推出 Linux 手機最艱難的一環，並不因為 ISA 是開放的就會消失。</p>
<h4>群雄並起、成績寥寥</h4>
<p>嘗試交付 Linux 智能手機的並不只有 Purism。PinePhone 和 F(x)tec 的滑蓋設備提供了替代硬件，而包括 Phosh、Plasma Mobile 和 Ubuntu Touch 在內的流動環境則競相提供可用的手機體驗。但它們至今都未能大規模交付一款具主流競爭力的設備——這正是 Librem 5 本身試圖填補的缺口，而且基本上做到了。</p>
<p>對開源社區而言，這次公布有兩個層面值得關注。首先，RISC-V 進入手機形態，將是該架構超越嵌入式及愛好者開發板進程的一個有意義數據點。其次，若 Purism 能在 v2 上展現更佳的進度管理紀律，將意味著他們從初代計劃的種種困難中汲取了教訓。</p>
<p>這兩個結果能否實現，尚屬未知。目前而言，Librem 5 v2 只是 roadmap 上的一項——它擁有一個有趣的架構方向，但背後公司的交付紀錄，令人難免抱持相當程度的懷疑。</p>
