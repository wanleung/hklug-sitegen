Title: openSUSE Leap 16.1 Moves Into Release Candidate Phase, Targets November Launch
Content:
<p>openSUSE Leap 16.1 is officially one step closer to its final release, having entered the Release Candidate (RC) phase with a target launch window in November. The project's release team is now focused on validation and stabilization ahead of general availability.</p>
<p>Leap Release Manager Luboš Kocman announced on the openSUSE project mailing list that the upcoming build is expected to qualify as the RC. This signals the completion of feature development for the distribution, with all subsequent efforts dedicated to ensuring stability and quality.</p>
<p>A critical checkpoint for this build is passing the project's openQA automated testing suite. This comprehensive quality gate is designed to catch regressions and verify system functionality. Successfully navigating these automated checks is a prerequisite for the build to be declared the final Release Candidate and advance toward public launch.</p>
<p>The confirmed November timeline offers valuable planning clarity for administrators. Leap has a unique role in the Linux ecosystem, serving as the community counterpart to the enterprise-focused SUSE Linux Enterprise (SLE). The two distributions share a common core, meaning updates and fixes often flow between them. Consequently, the Leap release cycle is a key event for organizations managing fleets that include both community and commercially-supported Linux environments.</p>
<p>With the RC phase underway and a November release in view, system administrators can now begin to evaluate this forthcoming version for their upgrade plans and project schedules. The final, critical validation has begun.</p>
<hr>
<p>openSUSE Leap 16.1 已正式邁向最終發布的關鍵一步，該版本現已進入候選版本（RC）階段，目標發布窗口定於今年11月。項目發布團隊現正集中精力進行驗證和穩定性測試，為正式發佈做準備。</p>
<p>Leap 發佈經理 Luboš Kocman 在 openSUSE 項目郵件列表中宣布，即將推出的構建版本預計將被確認為候選版本。這標誌著該發行版的功能開發工作已經完成，後續所有努力將專注於確保穩定性和質量。</p>
<p>該構建版本的一個關鍵檢查點是通過項目的 openQA 自動化測試套件。這個全面的質量關卡旨在捕捉回歸問題並驗證系統功能。成功通過這些自動化檢查是該構建版本被宣告為最終候選版本並推進至公開發布的先決條件。</p>
<p>已確認的11月時間表為管理員提供了清晰的規劃依據。Leap 在 Linux 生態系統中扮演著獨特角色，作為專注於企業級市場的 SUSE Linux Enterprise（SLE）的社區版對應部分。這兩個發行版共享一個核心基礎，這意味著更新和修復程序通常會在兩者之間流動。因此，Leap 的發布週期對於管理同時包含社區版和商業支持 Linux 環境的機構而言，是一個重要事件。</p>
<p>隨著候選版本階段的啟動以及11月發布的臨近，系統管理員現在可以開始評估這個即將發布的版本，並將其納入升級計劃和項目日程。最終、關鍵的驗證工作已經展開。</p>
