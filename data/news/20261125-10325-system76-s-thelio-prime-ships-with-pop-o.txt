Title: System76's Thelio Prime Ships With Pop!_OS 26.04 as Phoronix Benchmarks the New Release
Content:
<p>System76 has released Pop!_OS 26.04 LTS, the newest version of its Ubuntu-derived distribution and the first long-term-support release to feature the company's in-house COSMIC desktop environment. In a review published this week, Phoronix has benchmarked the new release against the outgoing 24.04 LTS, with the full per-workload tables available on the site.</p>
<p>The detail that gives this benchmark round unusual weight is hardware. Phoronix reports that System76's new Thelio Prime small-form-factor PC has been shipping with Pop!_OS 26.04 LTS preinstalled since last week.</p>
<h4>A benchmark tied to a product you can order</h4>
<p>Routine comparisons between adjacent Linux releases rarely change anyone's buying decision: same Ubuntu lineage, similar toolchain, modest deltas. What distinguishes this one is that the tested software is the same build customers receive on current hardware. The results are not a lab exercise on a hand-assembled test image — they reflect the configuration that ships in the box.</p>
<p>For anyone evaluating a desktop Linux rollout, that hardware-and-software pairing is the most useful part of the story. It places COSMIC in a product that is actually being sold, rather than a preview build running on spare parts.</p>
<h4>Where to find the numbers</h4>
<p>Phoronix's review, published this week, contains the per-workload benchmark tables comparing 26.04 with 24.04. Readers weighing a migration should consult those tables directly: the meaningful findings sit in the workload-by-workload breakdowns rather than in any single headline figure, and the original review carries the complete set of results.</p>
<h4>What should actually drive an upgrade decision</h4>
<p><strong>LTS horizon.</strong> 26.04 defines the support window teams will live with for years. Organisations that standardised on 24.04 are now on the older half of that cycle, and upgrade timing is typically dictated less by benchmark deltas than by when the organisation can absorb a desktop refresh.</p>
<p><strong>Hardware pairing.</strong> If you are ordering new machines rather than upgrading in place, the question is straightforward: the Thelio Prime ships with 26.04. Building an in-house image then becomes a matter of choice rather than necessity.</p>
<p><strong>Desktop fit.</strong> Benchmarks between two Ubuntu-derived releases tend to move in small increments. The larger variable for a workstation fleet is how well a changed desktop environment maps onto how developers and operations staff actually work — a question the numbers can't settle.</p>
<h4>The strategic through-line</h4>
<p>System76 built COSMIC in-house to reduce its reliance on an external desktop stack, and every release now serves as evidence for that bet. 26.04 is the first LTS that will be judged on COSMIC's merits across a full support cycle rather than on its novelty.</p>
<p>For Linux workstation fleets, the practical takeaway is modest: consult the benchmark tables for the deltas, and weigh them alongside the workflow cost of changing desktop paradigms across a fleet, which tends to be the larger number.</p>
<p>Phoronix's full results, including the per-workload tables, are available in the original review.</p>
<p><em>Source: Phoronix, "Pop!_OS 26.04 LTS vs. Pop!_OS 24.04 LTS Benchmarks."</em></p>
<hr>
<p>System76 已發布 Pop!_OS 26.04 LTS，這是其 Ubuntu 衍生發行版的最新版本，也是首個搭載公司自家研發 COSMIC 桌面環境的長期支援版本。Phoronix 於本週發布的評測中，已將新版本與即將退役的 24.04 LTS 進行基準測試比較，各項工作負載的完整表格可在該網站查閱。</p>
<p>令本輪基準測試非比尋常的關鍵，在於硬件。Phoronix 報道，System76 新推出的小型機身電腦 Thelio Prime 自上週起，出貨即預載 Pop!_OS 26.04 LTS。</p>
<h4>與可訂購產品掛鉤的基準測試</h4>
<p>相鄰 Linux 版本之間的例行比較，鮮少會改變任何人的購買決定：同樣的 Ubuntu 血統、相近的工具鏈、輕微的差異。本輪測試與別不同之處在於，受測軟件正是客戶在現有硬件上所收到的同一版本。測試結果並非實驗室中以手動組裝測試映像進行的練習——而是反映實際連同機身附送的配置。</p>
<p>對於任何正在評估桌面 Linux 部署方案的人士而言，這套硬件與軟件的組合正是整件事最有價值的部分。它令 COSMIC 出現在真正發售的產品之中，而非僅是運行於備用零件上的預覽版本。</p>
<h4>數據在哪裡查閱</h4>
<p>Phoronix 於本週發布的評測載有比較 26.04 與 24.04 的各項工作負載基準測試表格。正考慮遷移的讀者宜直接查閱這些表格：真正有意義的發現存在於逐項工作負載的分項數據之中，而非任何單一的總結數字，而原始評測載有完整的一套結果。</p>
<h4>什麼才是升級決定的真正依據</h4>
<p><strong>LTS 支援期限。</strong> 26.04 釐定了團隊日後多年所依賴的支援窗口。已將標準化建立於 24.04 的機構，如今正處於該週期中較舊的一半，升級時機通常取決於機構何時有能力應付一次桌面更新，而非基準測試的差異。</p>
<p><strong>硬件搭配。</strong> 若你正訂購全新機器而非原地升級，問題相當直接：Thelio Prime 連同 26.04 附送。此時自行製作映像，便成為一種選擇而非必要。</p>
<p><strong>桌面契合度。</strong> 兩個 Ubuntu 衍生版本之間的基準測試，差異往往只在小幅變動。對工作站機隊而言，較大的變量是更替後的桌面環境，能在多大程度上配合開發人員與營運及維護人員的實際工作方式——這一點是數據無法定奪的。</p>
<h4>貫穿全局的策略主線</h4>
<p>System76 自行研發 COSMIC，以減少對外部桌面 stack 的依賴，而此後每次發布都成為這場押注的佐證。26.04 是首個將在完整支援週期內，以 COSMIC 的實質表現而非新鮮感來受考驗的 LTS 版本。</p>
<p>對於 Linux 工作站機隊，實際的啟示並不宏大：查閱基準測試表格了解各項差異，並將之與全機隊轉換桌面模式所帶來的工作流程成本一併衡量——後者通常才是較大的數字。</p>
