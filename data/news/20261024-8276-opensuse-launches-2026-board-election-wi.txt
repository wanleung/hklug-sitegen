<h3>openSUSE Launches 2026 Board Election With Three Seats and a 1 December Finish Line</h3>
<p>The openSUSE Election Committee has opened the project's 2026 Board Election, with three seats on the line and a completion target of 1 December 2026 — a deadline the Board itself requested and recorded in Board ticket #108, referenced in the announcement published on the openSUSE news site on 24 October.</p>
<p>All eligible openSUSE members are invited to take part. The announcement publishes no candidate slate and names no regional or community representatives, but it does set out the full schedule.</p>
<h4>Full election timeline</h4>
<p>The announcement marks 24 October 2026 as the start of the call for nominations and applications, with the nomination period open to extension. The final candidate list is due on 14 November, the same date the membership database is sealed for voter eligibility. Ballots open on 15 November and close on 22 November, with results announced on 23 November — a week ahead of the 1 December completion target.</p>
<p>Applications must be sent to project@lists.opensuse.org and election-officials@opensuse.org. Candidates are required to publish their platform before 14 November. Should too few candidates be elected, the announcement notes that seats can be filled by appointment under the project's rules.</p>
<p>The <a href="https://news.opensuse.org/2026/10/24/board-election-2026-announcement/">openSUSE announcement</a> and the project's election pages carry the live procedure and any updates; no dedicated voting link is published in the notice.</p>
<h4>Why three seats matter</h4>
<p>openSUSE's Board is a small governing body, so three seats in play at once is a substantial share of it. The Board sets project priorities, manages the project's relationship with its corporate sponsor, and serves as the interface between maintainers and the downstream users who run the distribution in production.</p>
<p>For those users, the practical stake is predictability: how openly decisions are communicated, how quickly packager and packaging-policy concerns get a response, and how directly volunteer interests are weighed against sponsor interests. The election determines who signs off on the direction of a distribution widely deployed in enterprise and mixed-vendor environments.</p>
<h4>A comparatively broad electorate</h4>
<p>Participation is open to all eligible members — not confined to developers or contributors holding commit rights. That broad electorate means the outcome can be shaped by users and advocates outside the core engineering team, a feature not common among large open-source projects.</p>
<p>For IT professionals in the region weighing Linux distribution choices, the process is a reminder that the projects behind their tooling are governed by elected members — and that this cycle's window closes on 1 December.</p>
<p>The full announcement is available at the <a href="https://news.opensuse.org/2026/10/24/board-election-2026-announcement/">openSUSE news site</a>.</p>

<h3>openSUSE 啟動 2026 年理事會選舉　三席職位　12 月 1 日截止</h3>
<p>openSUSE 選舉委員會已展開該項目 2026 年理事會選舉，共有三個席位有待選出，完成目標日期為 2026 年 12 月 1 日——此期限由理事會本身提出，並記錄於理事會工單 #108，該工單在 10 月 24 日於 openSUSE 新聞網站刊出的公告中被引用。</p>
<p>所有符合資格的 openSUSE 會員均獲邀參與。公告未列出候選人名單，亦未點名任何地區或社群代表，但已列明完整的時間表。</p>
<h4>選舉完整時間表</h4>
<p>公告將 2026 年 10 月 24 日定為接受提名及申請的開始日期，提名期可延長。最終候選人名單須於 11 月 14 日確定，同日會員數據庫將封存以確定投票資格。投票於 11 月 15 日開始、11 月 22 日結束，結果於 11 月 23 日公佈——比 12 月 1 日的完成目標提前一星期。</p>
<p>申請必須發送至 project@lists.opensuse.org 及 election-officials@opensuse.org。候選人須於 11 月 14 日前發表其政綱。公告指出，若當選人數過少，當局可依項目規則以委任方式填補席位。</p>
<p><a href="https://news.opensuse.org/2026/10/24/board-election-2026-announcement/">openSUSE 公告</a> 及該項目的選舉專頁載有即時程序及最新更新；公告本身並未提供專門的投票連結。</p>
<h4>為何三個席位重要</h4>
<p>openSUSE 理事會規模不大，因此同時選出三個席位，佔其相當大的比例。理事會負責訂定項目優先事項、管理項目與其企業贊助商的關係，並擔當維護者與在生產環境運行該發行版的下游用戶之間的橋樑。</p>
<p>對這些用戶而言，實際關鍵在於可預測性：決策對外溝通的透明程度、打包者及打包政策的關注獲得回應的速度，以及志願者利益與贊助商利益之間的權衡方式。是次選舉決定誰會簽批這款廣泛部署於企業及多供應商環境的發行版的發展方向。</p>
<h4>相對廣泛的選民範圍</h4>
<p>所有符合資格的會員均可參與投票——並不限於擁有 commit 權限的開發者或貢獻者。這種廣泛的選民範圍意味著選舉結果可以由核心工程團隊之外的用戶及支持者塑造，在大型開放源碼項目中並不多見。</p>
<p>對本區需要衡量 Linux 發行版選擇的 IT 專業人士而言，是次流程提醒了他們：支撐其工具的項目，是由選舉產生的會員所管治——而本輪選舉的窗口將於 12 月 1 日關閉。</p>
