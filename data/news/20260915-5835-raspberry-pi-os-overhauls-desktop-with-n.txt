Title: Raspberry Pi OS Overhauls Desktop with New Icon Dock
Content:
<p>The Raspberry Pi Foundation has overhauled the desktop interface on Raspberry Pi OS, introducing a new icon dock as part of an effort to modernize the computing experience on ARM64 single-board computers.</p>
<p>The addition of a persistent dock brings the platform's interface closer to conventions found on mainstream consumer operating systems, potentially reducing the learning curve for users accustomed to Windows or macOS environments.</p>
<p>This type of modernization effort could have meaningful implications for the Foundation's reach in education and maker communities, where a more approachable interface may lower barriers to adoption and reduce support overhead for institutions deploying Raspberry Pi hardware.</p>
<p>Any visual refresh of Raspberry Pi OS must also navigate the challenge of maintaining performance across the platform's diverse hardware lineup, from older, resource-constrained models to newer boards.</p>
<p>A more intuitive desktop experience can benefit educators and hobbyists alike, potentially translating to smoother learning outcomes and more polished project presentations. This update reflects the Foundation's ongoing effort to evolve the platform while preserving its core ethos of affordability and accessibility.</p>

<p>一個更直觀的桌面體驗可惠及教育工作者及愛好者，有望帶來更流暢的學習成果及更精緻的項目展示。此次更新反映了基金會在堅持平台價格實惠與易於獲取的核心理念同時，致力於使平台與時俱進的持續努力。</p>
