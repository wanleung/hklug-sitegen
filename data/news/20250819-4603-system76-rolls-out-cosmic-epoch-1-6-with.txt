Title: System76 Rolls Out COSMIC Epoch 1.6 with Per-App Audio Controls and Compositor-Level Remote Access
Content:
<p>System76 has released COSMIC Epoch 1.6 for public testing, introducing native per-application audio routing and laying the groundwork for a compositor-level remote desktop framework. The update continues the company’s development of a Rust-based, Wayland-native desktop environment intended to eventually replace GNOME in its Pop!_OS Linux distribution.</p>
<p>Historically, managing per-application audio on Linux workstations required third-party mixers or manual PipeWire configuration. Epoch 1.6 integrates granular volume controls directly into the COSMIC shell and system settings panel. This eliminates the need for external utilities or script-based routing, simplifying audio management for communication-heavy and multimedia enterprise workflows.</p>
<p>The release also introduces foundational components for native remote desktop support. Rather than relying on standalone VNC or RDP wrappers, System76 is embedding session management and connection brokering directly into the compositor. This architectural approach aims to reduce the attack surface associated with legacy tunneling protocols and align with modern zero-trust security models. The remote access features remain in early development and are not yet feature-complete.</p>
<p>Underpinning these additions is a continued focus on memory safety and strict Wayland compliance. By building the desktop environment in Rust, the COSMIC team targets the elimination of memory-related vulnerabilities and compositor crashes, while also addressing persistent multi-monitor scaling inconsistencies. For IT departments, this engineering strategy addresses long-standing stability concerns that have historically complicated large-scale Linux workstation deployments and compliance audits.</p>
<p>System76 has clearly labeled Epoch 1.6 as a pre-release milestone. The environment remains under active development, and enterprise integrations alongside niche hardware support are still maturing. While the update is available through standard open-source channels, the company explicitly advises against deploying it in production environments until further stabilization occurs. A technical breakdown of the remote session protocols is expected once they reach public beta.</p>
<p>COSMIC’s development trajectory reflects a broader industry push toward memory-safe desktop architectures and native protocol integration. As independent Linux vendors prioritize foundational engineering over incremental feature additions, enterprise IT teams will be monitoring how COSMIC’s compositor-level remote access and Rust-driven stability model perform under real-world workloads in upcoming release cycles.</p>
<hr>
<p>System76 已推出 COSMIC Epoch 1.6 供公眾測試，引入原生的個別應用程式音訊路由功能，並為 compositor 層級的遙距桌面框架奠定基礎。是次更新延續該公司開發基於 Rust 及原生支援 Wayland 的桌面環境之進程，該環境最終旨在取代其 Pop!_OS Linux 發行版中的 GNOME。</p>
<p>過往在 Linux 工作站管理個別應用程式的音訊，往往需要依賴第三方混音器或手動設定 PipeWire。Epoch 1.6 將精細的音量控制直接整合至 COSMIC shell 及系統設定面板。此舉省卻了使用外部工具或依賴腳本進行路由的需要，簡化了以通訊為主及多媒體企業工作流程的音訊管理。</p>
<p>是次發布亦引入了原生遙距桌面支援的基礎組件。System76 並未依賴獨立的 VNC 或 RDP 封裝程式，而是將會話管理及連線協調直接嵌入 compositor 之中。此架構設計旨在減少與傳統隧道協議相關的攻擊面，並與現代零信任安全模型接軌。目前的遙距存取功能仍處於早期開發階段，尚未具備完整功能。</p>
<p>這些新增功能背後，是團隊對記憶體安全及嚴格遵循 Wayland 規範的持續重視。透過以 Rust 編寫桌面環境，COSMIC 團隊致力消除與記憶體相關的漏洞及 compositor 當機問題，同時解決長期存在的多螢幕縮放不一致情況。對資訊科技部門而言，此工程策略解決了過往一直困擾大規模 Linux 工作站部署及合規審計的穩定性隱憂。</p>
<p>System76 已明確將 Epoch 1.6 標示為預發布里程碑。該桌面環境仍處於積極開發階段，企業級整合及特定硬件支援亦仍在完善中。儘管更新已可透過標準開源渠道取得，但公司明確建議在進一步穩定前，切勿將其部署於生產環境。待遙距會話協議進入公開測試版後，預計將公布相關技術剖析。</p>
<p>COSMIC 的發展軌跡反映了業界更廣泛的趨勢，即推動具備記憶體安全的桌面架構及原生協議整合。隨著獨立 Linux 供應商將基礎工程置於漸進式功能新增之上，企業 IT 團隊將在未來的發布週期中，密切留意 COSMIC 的 compositor 層級遙距存取及由 Rust 驅動的穩定性模型在實際工作負載下的表現。</p>
