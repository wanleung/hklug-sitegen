Title: Synology DSM and LiteLLM flaws land in CISA's exploited-vulnerability catalogue — plus two long-tail targets
Content:
<p>The U.S. Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) has added vulnerabilities affecting four distinct software stacks to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalogue, according to Security Affairs: flaws in Synology DSM, BerriAI LiteLLM, Vanderbilt REDCap, and Apache Axis.</p>
<p>The listing matters because inclusion in the KEV catalogue is not a routine housekeeping exercise. CISA only adds a vulnerability once it has reliable evidence of active exploitation in the wild. For administrators, that converts an abstract CVE into a live operational question: is this software on my network, and has it already been reached?</p>
<p><strong>Where administrators should look first</strong></p>
<p>The two additions with the broadest day-to-day impact are Synology DSM and LiteLLM.</p>
<p>Synology's DiskStation Manager underpins a large installed base of network-attached storage appliances in small offices, home labs, and enterprise branch deployments alike. NAS boxes are attractive targets precisely because they sit at the intersection of valuable data and often-lax patch cadence; they are frequently exposed directly to the internet for remote access. Any DSM flaw with evidence of exploitation should trigger an immediate review of firmware level, remote-access configuration, and — critically — whether default or weak credentials remain in place.</p>
<p>LiteLLM, the open-source gateway layer used to route and manage requests across large language model APIs, represents a newer class of exposure. As organisations increasingly place an AI gateway at the front of their model infrastructure, that gateway becomes a single point of failure: it typically holds API keys, sits on internal networks with wide reach, and is often exposed to developer tooling or external traffic. A vulnerability being actively exploited in this layer is a reminder that AI infrastructure inherits the same patching discipline as any other internet-facing service — and that this layer is currently being treated as a lower-priority component than it deserves.</p>
<p><strong>The long tail: research tooling and legacy middleware</strong></p>
<p>Vanderbilt REDCap — the research data-capture platform widely used in academic and clinical research — occupies a specialised niche. Installations are often managed by individual research groups rather than central IT, which means they are prone to lingering unpatched instances holding sensitive study data.</p>
<p>Apache Axis 1.x is the harder case. The SOAP toolkit has long been out of active development, and for many estates there is no practical upgrade path — organisations are left with compensating controls: removing the component, isolating it behind strict network rules, or filtering the specific request patterns the flaw depends on. Where a vulnerable Axis deployment cannot be patched, isolation is not optional.</p>
<p><strong>What the listing actually obliges</strong></p>
<p>CISA's Binding Operational Directive 22-01 establishes the remediation clock for federal civilian executive branch agencies: KEV-listed vulnerabilities must generally be patched within two weeks of the due date CISA assigns, with the exact mechanics set out on the directive's own page. Private-sector organisations are not bound by the directive, but the security community has effectively adopted the KEV catalogue as a de facto patching benchmark — it is one of the few publicly maintained, evidence-based lists of what is genuinely being attacked right now.</p>
<p><strong>A practical checklist for tonight</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Inventory Synology NAS devices and confirm current DSM firmware, including any units outside formal asset management.</li>
<li>Locate LiteLLM (and comparable AI gateway) deployments; verify version, exposure surface, and key rotation.</li>
<li>Audit REDCap instances for upstream availability and patch level, especially departmental installs outside central IT.</li>
<li>Identify any Axis 1.x components; where no patch exists, apply isolation and monitoring as a stopgap.</li>
<li>Subscribe to CISA's machine-readable KEV feed so new listings surface automatically in your vulnerability-management workflow.</li>
</ul>
<p>Exact CVE identifiers, severity scores, and per-item remediation due dates for all four additions are published directly in CISA's KEV catalogue, which remains the authoritative source for verification before any patching decisions are made.</p>
<hr>
<p>據 Security Affairs 報導，美國網絡安全和基礎設施安全局（CISA）已將影響四套不同軟件系統的漏洞加入其已知遭利用漏洞（KEV）目錄，涉及 Synology DSM、BerriAI LiteLLM、Vanderbilt REDCap 及 Apache Axis 的漏洞。</p>
<p>此次收錄意義重大，因為漏洞獲納 KEV 目錄並非例行的清單整理工作。CISA 只會在掌握可靠證據證明漏洞已在現實環境中遭主動利用後，才會將其加入。對系統管理員而言，這會把抽象的 CVE 轉化為切身的營運問題：這款軟件是否在我網絡上，以及它是否已經遭到入侵？</p>
<p><strong>系統管理員應優先檢視的地方</strong></p>
<p>對日常運作影響最廣泛的兩項新增項目，是 Synology DSM 與 LiteLLM。</p>
<p>Synology 的 DiskStation Manager 是大量網絡附加存儲（NAS）設備的基礎，廣泛見於小型辦公室、家庭實驗室及企業分支架構。NAS 設備之所以成為熾手可熱的攻擊目標，正因它處於有價值數據與往往鬆懈的修補節奏的交匯點；這類設備經常直接暴露於互聯網以供遙距存取。任何有遭利用證據的 DSM 漏洞，都應立即觸發對 firmware 版本、遙距存取配置的檢視——以及至關重要的一點：默認或弱密碼憑證是否仍然存在。</p>
<p>LiteLLM 是用於路由及管理大型語言模型 API 請求的開源 gateway 層，代表了一類較新的暴露形式。隨著越來越多機構在模型基礎架構前端設置 AI gateway，該 gateway 便成為單一故障點：它通常持有 API key、坐擁覆蓋範圍廣泛的內部網絡，而且往往對開發者工具或外部流量開放。這一層出現遭主動利用的漏洞，提醒我們 AI 基礎設施須同樣遵守任何其他連接互聯網服務的修補紀律——而目前這一層所獲重視的優先次序，明顯低於其應有的水平。</p>
<p><strong>長尾目標：研究工具與遺留中間件</strong></p>
<p>Vanderbilt REDCap——廣泛應用於學術及臨床研究的數據採集平台——位處專門利基。其安裝往往由個別研究小組而非中央 IT 部門管理，這意味著容易遺留長期未修補、存放敏感研究數據的實例。</p>
<p>Apache Axis 1.x 則是更棘手的情況。這套 SOAP 工具套件早已停止活躍開發，對許多系統而言並無實際升級路徑——機構只能採用補償性控制措施：移除相關組件、以嚴格網絡規則將其隔離，或過濾該漏洞所依賴的特定 request 模式。對於無法修補的 vulnerable Axis 部署，隔離並非可選項。</p>
<p><strong>收錄清單的實際約束</strong></p>
<p>CISA 的具約束力行動指令 22-01 為聯邦民事行政分支機構訂明了修補時限：KEV 列出的漏洞一般須在 CISA 指定的到期日後兩週內完成修補，具體機制則詳載於該指令本身的頁面。私營機構並不受該指令約束，但安全社群實質上已將 KEV 目錄採納為事實上的修補基準——它是少數由公眾維護、以證據為基礎的清單之一，列明當前真正正遭攻擊的目標。</p>
<p><strong>今晚即可執行的實用清單</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>盤點 Synology NAS 設備，確認現時 DSM firmware 版本，包括所有未納入正式資產管理的機器。</li>
<li>找出 LiteLLM（及同類 AI gateway）部署；核實版本、受攻擊面及 key 輪換情況。</li>
<li>審計 REDCap 實例的上游可用性與修補水平，尤其是脫離中央 IT 管轄的部門自行安裝版本。</li>
<li>識別任何 Axis 1.x 組件；如無修補程式可用，須以隔離及監控作為過渡措施。</li>
<li>訂閱 CISA 的機器可讀 KEV feed，使新增條目自動呈現在漏洞管理工作流程之中。</li>
</ul>
<p>四項新增漏洞的準確 CVE 編號、嚴重性評分及逐項修補到期日，均直接刊載於 CISA 的 KEV 目錄；在作出任何修補決定之前，該目錄仍是核實資訊的權威來源。</p>
