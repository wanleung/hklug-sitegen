Title: Firefox 157 Delivers Smarter Video Calls and a Cleaner Interface
Content:
<p>Mozilla has pushed out Firefox 157, the latest open-source browser update with a focus on enhancing remote work efficiency. The release introduces hardware-accelerated AV1 decoding for video calls and extends its modern design to more interface elements.</p>
<p>The headline feature is the addition of hardware-accelerated decoding for the AV1 codec within WebRTC streams. AV1 is a next-generation video format that compresses high-quality video more efficiently than older codecs, making it ideal for internet streaming and video conferencing. By delegating this decoding task from the CPU to specialized hardware like a graphics card, Firefox 157 aims to significantly improve the video calling experience.</p>
<p>For users, this technical shift translates to concrete benefits: lower CPU usage, reduced power draw, and potentially longer battery life on laptops. In video meetings, it could also enable clearer, higher-fidelity video even on less stable or shared network connections—a frequent reality in remote and hybrid work scenarios.</p>
<p>Complementing the performance upgrade, Mozilla has also expanded its "Proton" design language to cover menus and dialog boxes throughout the browser. The update aims to create a cleaner, more uniform styling and a less cluttered visual experience, bringing consistency to parts of the interface that had previously lagged behind the main design refresh.</p>
<p>For IT professionals, developers, and remote workers who rely on robust browser performance for collaboration, these enhancements target a core daily tool. More efficient video processing means smoother meetings with distributed teams and less strain on system resources during long work sessions. The cleaner interface also contributes to a more focused and modern workflow.</p>
<p>The release of Firefox 157 is part of Mozilla's regular cadence of updates, reinforcing its commitment to performance and standards compliance. The updated browser is now available for download on Windows, macOS, Linux, and Android.</p>
<hr>
<p>Mozilla 推出了 Firefox 157，這是最新一款開源瀏覽器更新，重點在於提升遠程工作效率。此次版本更新為視像通話引入了硬件加速的 AV1 解碼功能，並將其現代化設計擴展至更多介面元素。</p>
<p>其標誌性功能是在 WebRTC 串流中加入了對 AV1 編解碼器的硬件加速解碼支持。AV1 是一種新一代的視像格式，比舊式編解碼器能更高效地壓縮高質量視像，因此非常適用於互聯網串流和視像會議。透過將解碼任務從 CPU 委託給圖像處理器等專用硬件，Firefox 157 旨在顯著提升視像通話體驗。</p>
<p>對用戶而言，此技術轉變帶來了實質的好處：更低的 CPU 使用率、減少的功耗，以及手提電腦上可能更長的電池續航力。在視像會議中，即便在較不穩定或共享的網絡連接下，也可能實現更清晰、更高保真度的影像——這是遠程及混合辦公模式中常見的實際情況。</p>
<p>配合性能升級，Mozilla 亦將其「Proton」設計語言擴展至瀏覽器內的所有選單與對話框。此次更新旨在創造更簡潔、統一的風格以及更不雜亂的視覺體驗，為先前未跟上主要設計更新的介面部分帶來一致性。</p>
<p>對於依賴瀏覽器強大性能進行協作的 IT 專業人員、開發者及遠程工作者而言，這些增強功能針對的是其核心日常工具。更高效的視像處理意味著與分布式團隊的會議更加流暢，同時減輕了長時間工作期間系統資源的壓力。更簡潔的介面也有助於建立更專注和現代化的工作流程。</p>
<p>Firefox 157 的發布是 Mozilla 定期更新節奏的一部分，彰顯了其對性能與標準合規性的承諾。更新後的瀏覽器現已可在 Windows、macOS、Linux 及 Android 上下載。</p>
