Title: Microsoft Warns Windows 11 24H2 Home and Pro Users of October Support Cut-off
Content:
<p>Microsoft has confirmed that security updates for Windows 11 version 24H2 will cease for Home and Pro editions after the October 2024 Patch Tuesday. The announcement, reported by BleepingComputer, clarifies that devices running these specific versions will no longer receive monthly security patches or official technical support.</p>
<p>This end-of-support date is determined by Microsoft's standard 24-month servicing lifecycle, which applies to its consumer and prosumer operating system editions. Once this period concludes, the affected systems will not get updates designed to patch newly discovered vulnerabilities.</p>
<p>It is important to distinguish this from other editions. The Enterprise and Education variants of Windows 11 24H2 follow a longer 36-month support schedule and are not impacted by this October deadline. Their extended servicing provides continued security updates well into the future.</p>
<p>For both individual users and IT administrators managing fleets of devices, the clear directive is to migrate to a supported release before the deadline. The most straightforward step is upgrading to an actively serviced version, such as Windows 11 23H2 or any newer release that remains within its support window.</p>
<p>Allowing systems to run on unsupported software creates substantial security risks. Without ongoing patches, these machines become prime targets for cybercriminals exploiting unpatched flaws, potentially leading to malware infections, data breaches, or ransomware attacks. For businesses, this also introduces regulatory compliance challenges and significantly weakens the overall security posture.</p>
<p>This situation highlights a critical aspect of IT governance: proactive lifecycle management. Organizations must regularly audit their software estate, track vendor support timelines, and plan migrations well before end-of-life milestones. Users on the affected Windows 11 24H2 Home or Pro editions should check their version immediately and begin the update process to prevent a lapse in security coverage.</p>
<hr>
<p>微軟已確認，Windows 11 24H2版本的家用版及專業版將於2024年10月Patch Tuesday後停止接收安全更新。據BleepingComputer報道，這項公告明確指出，運行這些特定版本的設備將不再獲得每月安全修補程式或官方技術支援。</p>
<p>這項支援終止日期是根據微軟標準的24個月服務週期所定，該週期適用於其消費者及專業消費者作業系統版本。當此期限結束後，受影響的系統將不會收到旨在修補新發現漏洞的更新。</p>
<p>必須注意將此與其他版本區分開來。Windows 11 24H2的企業版及教育版遵循較長的36個月支援計劃，因此不受此次十月期限的影響。其延長服務將在未來較長時間內持續提供安全更新。</p>
<p>對於個人用戶及管理設備群組的IT管理員而言，明確的指示是在期限前遷移至受支援的版本。最直接的做法是升級至持續服務中的版本，例如Windows 11 23H2或任何仍在支援窗口內的更新版本。</p>
<p>讓系統在不受支援的軟件上運行會產生重大安全風險。在缺乏持續修補的情況下，這些設備將成為網絡罪犯利用未修補漏洞的首要目標，可能導致惡意軟件感染、資料外洩或勒索軟件攻擊。對企業而言，這也帶來監管合規方面的挑戰，並顯著削弱整體安全態勢。</p>
<p>此情況凸顯了IT治理的一個關鍵方面：主動式生命週期管理。組織必須定期審核其軟件資產、追蹤供應商支援時間表，並在生命週期結束里程碑前規劃遷移。受影響的Windows 11 24H2家用版或專業版用戶應立即檢查其版本，並開始更新流程，以防止安全覆蓋出現缺口。</p>
