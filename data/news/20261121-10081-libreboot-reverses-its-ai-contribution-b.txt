Title: Libreboot Reverses Its AI Contribution Ban After About a Month
Content:
<p>Libreboot, the downstream distribution built on the coreboot firmware project, has scrapped a policy banning contributions written with the help of AI tools — roughly a month after introducing it, according to Phoronix.</p>
<p>The reversal is notable less for what it says about Libreboot's views on AI than for how quickly a governance decision lasted. A policy that survives barely four weeks sends a mixed signal to contributors: those who held back patches under the ban, or who reworked submissions to avoid AI assistance, did so for a rule that no longer exists.</p>
<h4>What is known — and what is not</h4>
<p>Phoronix reported that Libreboot enacted the anti-AI policy last month and has now dropped it, with AI-assisted submissions once again permitted. The source does not identify who made the decision, does not state a rationale, and does not give the precise date the ban took effect. This report does not fill those gaps with speculation; the absence of a stated reason is itself part of the record as it currently stands.</p>
<p>Equally unclear: whether the original ban applied to all contributions or only some categories, and whether any patch was actually rejected under it. Neither claim is made here.</p>
<h4>Why enforceability is the real issue</h4>
<p>The deeper problem — and this analysis is inference, not something Libreboot has commented on — is that no reliable technical test exists to determine whether a patch was generated by a large language model. Code review can flag style, correctness, and provenance questions, but it cannot prove a negative. A ban on AI-assisted contributions therefore collapses into an honour system.</p>
<p>For a project maintaining security-sensitive firmware code, where reviewer attention is a scarce resource, policing an unverifiable rule adds overhead without adding assurance. That tension is not unique to Libreboot: projects including curl and PostgreSQL have spent the past couple of years grappling with where, if anywhere, to draw the line on AI-generated patches, with opinions running strongly in both directions.</p>
<h4>A lesson for other maintainers</h4>
<p>The speed of the U-turn arguably matters more than the policy itself. Contributors who filtered their work through an AI ban, only to see it lifted a month later, may hesitate before trusting the next stated policy. For maintainers weighing their own rules, the takeaway is procedural: a policy that cannot be enforced, and whose adoption was not accompanied by a published rationale, is difficult to sustain — and reversing it compounds the confusion.</p>
<p>Whether Libreboot's decision will influence other firmware or low-level projects is too early to say. What is clear is that the FOSS community's unresolved question — how (or whether) to regulate AI-assisted contributions — remains open, and that even well-intentioned rules face a durability test once they meet the realities of volunteer-driven review. For anyone tracking upstream firmware and security projects, the practical lesson is the same: governance decisions can shift quickly, and project mailing lists and announcement channels remain the most reliable way to stay current.</p>

<p>據 Phoronix 報導，基於 coreboot firmware 項目建立的下游發行版 Libreboot，已撤回禁止借助 AI 工具撰寫貢獻的政策——該政策推出至今約一個月。</p>
<p>這次政策逆轉值得注意之處，與其說在於它反映 Libreboot 對 AI 的立場，不如說在於一項治理決定竟然如此短命。一項僅維持四個星期的政策，向貢獻者傳遞了混亂的信號：那些在禁令下押後提交 patch，或為了避開 AI 協助而重寫貢獻的人，所依據的規則如今已不復存在。</p>
<h4>已知的事實——以及尚不明朗的部分</h4>
<p>Phoronix 報道，Libreboot 上個月實施了反 AI 政策，如今已將其撤回，容許再次提交 AI 協助完成的貢獻。消息來源並未指明是誰作出決定，沒有交代理由，也沒有給出禁令生效的準確日期。本報道不以猜測填補這些空白；缺乏公開理由這一點本身，就是現有紀錄的一部分。</p>
<p>同樣不明朗的是：最初的禁令是否適用於所有貢獻，還是僅限於某類別，以及實際上是否有 patch 因此被拒。以上兩種說法本報均未提出。</p>
<h4>真正的問題在於可執行性</h4>
<p>更深層的問題——以下屬本報分析推斷，Libreboot 並未就此置評——是目前並無可靠的技術測試可以判斷某個 patch 是否由大型語言模型生成。Code review 可以標記風格、正確性及來源方面的問題，卻無法證明一個否定命題。因此，禁止 AI 協助貢獻的規則，最終只能依賴榮譽制度來維持。</p>
<p>對於一個維護安全敏感 firmware 代碼的項目而言，reviewer 的注意力本屬稀缺資源，執行一項無法核實的規則只會增加額外負擔，卻不能增加保障。這種張力並非 Libreboot 獨有：包括 curl 和 PostgreSQL 在內的項目，過去兩年一直就在 AI 生成的 patch 方面應於何處（如有需要）劃下界線而爭論不休，雙方意見均相當強烈。</p>
<h4>對其他維護者的啟示</h4>
<p>這次急速掉頭的意義，某程度上比政策本身更重要。曾為遵守 AI 禁令而調整、篩選自身工作的貢獻者，在一個月後見到禁令解除，日後或會對項目公布的下一次政策有所保留。對於正在衡量自身規則的維護者，結論屬程序層面：一項無法執行的政策，且其推出時未有同時公布理據，是很難長期維持的——而事後撤回只會令混亂加劇。</p>
<p>Libreboot 的決定會否影響其他 firmware 或底層項目，現階段言之尚早。可以確定的是，FOSS 社羣懸而未決的問題——如何（或是否應該）規管 AI 協助的貢獻——仍然開放，而且即使出於善意的規則，一旦遇上義工主導的 review 現實，亦要接受可持續性的考驗。對於任何追蹤上游 firmware 及安全項目的人士而言，實際啟示是一樣的：治理決定可能迅速轉變，而項目郵件列表及公告渠道，始終是掌握最新動態最可靠的途徑。</p>
