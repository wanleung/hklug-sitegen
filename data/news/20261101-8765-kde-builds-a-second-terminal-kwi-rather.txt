Title: KDE Builds a Second Terminal, Kwi, Rather Than Modernise Konsole
Content:
<p>KDE is building a second terminal emulator — and that decision is the story, not the app. According to Phoronix, developers have chosen to start from a clean slate with a project called Kwi, built on the Ghostty terminal library, rather than continue refactoring Konsole, the long-standing terminal that ships with Plasma. The report carries a headline claim of performance gains between 3x and 35x.</p>
<p>Those numbers warrant caution. Per the report, they come from the developer's own test conditions, and the gap between a threefold and a thirty-fivefold result says more about workload variance than about a uniform speedup. Kwi is also an early developer preview: no stable release, no feature parity with Konsole, no independent verification of the benchmarks. Read the figures as a directional signal, not a settled comparison.</p>
<h4>The architectural case</h4>
<p>The project's logic rests on architecture rather than arithmetic. Kwi borrows Ghostty's modern rendering and I/O core — the parts of a terminal that determine how responsive it feels under heavy output, fast scrolling, and multiplexed sessions — and wraps it in a KDE-native interface. The result is a Plasma application that presents itself as a first-party KDE offering while running on a foundation designed for contemporary hardware acceleration.</p>
<p>Konsole, by contrast, carries decades of accumulated code. It can be modernised in place, but every improvement is hemmed in by design decisions made when the terminal landscape looked very different. Running a second terminal in parallel lets KDE experiment without endangering the tool many Plasma users depend on daily.</p>
<p>That trade-off is not unique to KDE. The terminal world has been grappling with it for years: kitty and Alacritty staked their reputations on GPU-accelerated rendering pipelines that, their authors argue, deliver noticeably smoother behaviour than legacy terminals — particularly with large scrollbacks or Unicode-heavy output. Kwi brings that same argument inside the KDE fold, with KDE styling, settings integration, and conventions layered on top.</p>
<p>The same question recurs across the open-source ecosystem: when a long-shipping component carries architectural debt, should it be refactored in place, or should a parallel replacement be built while the original stays in service? KDE's answer — develop Kwi alongside Konsole rather than retire it — is a pattern downstream distributions and other desktop projects will recognise as a familiar compromise.</p>
<h4>Current status and what it means</h4>
<p>Kwi today is an early build — a developer preview, not a shipped, supported application. Users should expect rough edges and missing functionality relative to Konsole, and should treat it as a demonstration of direction rather than a drop-in replacement.</p>
<p>Should the project mature, Plasma users gain something rare on the Linux desktop: a choice between two terminals from the same project, one battle-tested and one built for modern performance. Even if Kwi never graduates beyond proof of concept, it raises a question KDE will eventually have to answer — whether Konsole's architecture is worth revisiting, or whether the future lies in composing KDE experiences on shared, cross-platform engines.</p>
<p>For now, the benchmarks remain unverified and no release date exists. What is clear is that KDE is willing to question its own assumptions — and that, more than any multiplier, is the news worth watching.</p>

<p>KDE 正在開發第二款終端模擬器——而這個決定本身才是新聞焦點，並非該應用程式。據 Phoronix 報導，開發人員選擇另起爐灶，開展名為 Kwi 的新項目，以 Ghostty 終端函式庫為基礎構建，而非繼續重構隨 Plasma 出貨的老牌終端 Konsole。該報導的標題聲稱效能提升介乎 3 倍至 35 倍之間。</p>
<p>這些數字值得審慎看待。據報導，它們來自開發者自身的測試條件，而三倍與三十五倍之間的差距，反映的與其說是普遍的加速，不如說是工作負載的差異。Kwi 目前亦只是早期開發者預覽版：沒有穩定版本，功能未及 Konsole，基準測試亦未經獨立驗證。讀者應將這些數據視為方向性指標，而非已成定論的比較。</p>
<h4>架構上的理據</h4>
<p>項目的邏輯建基於架構而非數字。Kwi 借用了 Ghostty 的現代化渲染及 I/O 核心——即決定終端在大量輸出、快速捲動及多工分隔畫面下回應表現的部分——並在外層包覆 KDE 原生介面。成果是一款 Plasma 應用程式，對外定位為 KDE 第一方應用程式，底層卻運行於專為當代硬件加速而設計的基礎之上。</p>
<p>相比之下，Konsole 承載著數十年累積的代碼。它固然可以就地現代化，但每一項改進都受制於終端生態截然不同時所作出的設計決定。平行運行第二款終端，讓 KDE 得以在不危及眾多 Plasma 日常用戶所依賴工具的前提下進行實驗。</p>
<p>這種取捨並非 KDE 獨有。終端領域多年來一直在應對同一課題：kitty 和 Alacritty 將聲譽押注於 GPU 加速渲染流水線，其作者認為它們較傳統終端帶來明顯更流暢的表現——尤其在大量捲動緩衝或 Unicode 密集輸出時。Kwi 將同一論述引入 KDE 陣營，並在其上疊加 KDE 樣式、設定整合及使用慣例。</p>
<p>同一問題在開放源碼生態中反覆出現：一個推出已久的元件若累積了架構負債，究竟應就地重構，還是另建平行替代方案並保留原有元件繼續服役？KDE 的答案——與 Konsole 並行開發 Kwi 而非將其退役——是下游發行版及其他桌面項目都會視為熟悉的折衷做法。</p>
<h4>目前進度及其意義</h4>
<p>Kwi 目前只是早期版本——屬開發者預覽，而非已推出並獲支援的應用程式。用戶應預期較 Konsole 為多的粗糙之處及功能缺失，並應視之為方向展示，而非可直接替換的方案。</p>
<p>倘若項目日後成熟，Plasma 用戶將可獲得 Linux 桌面上罕見的選擇：同一項目提供的兩款終端，一款久經考驗，一款為現代效能而建。即使 Kwi 最終未能超越概念驗證階段，它亦拋出了一個 KDE 終須回答的問題——Konsole 的架構是否值得重新檢視，還是未來在於以共享的跨平台引擎來組合 KDE 體驗。</p>
<p>目前而言，基準測試仍未經驗證，亦不存在發佈日期。可以確定的是，KDE 願意質疑自身的既定假設——而這一點，比任何倍數都更是值得關注的新聞。</p>
