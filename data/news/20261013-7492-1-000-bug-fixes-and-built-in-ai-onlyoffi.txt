Title: 1,000+ Bug Fixes and Built-in AI: ONLYOFFICE 10.0 Lands With a Stability-First Pitch
Content:
<p>The most consequential number in ONLYOFFICE 10.0's release announcement is not the one about artificial intelligence. As <a href="https://www.phoronix.com/news/ONLYOFFICE-10.0">Phoronix reported on 13 October 2026</a>, the update to the open-source office productivity suite carries more than 1,000 bug fixes — a figure that says more about the project's engineering maturity than any single headline feature.</p>
<p><strong>Stability as the maturity signal</strong></p>
<p>For enterprise IT teams weighing alternatives to Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, a four-figure bug-fix count is the detail that matters most. It signals sustained investment in hardening the codebase — the kind of groundwork that typically precedes production approval. New features attract attention during evaluation; reliability determines whether a suite survives a pilot. On that measure, 10.0 reads as a maturation milestone for one of the more prominent open-source challengers to LibreOffice.</p>
<p><strong>AI inside the editors, with an unanswered question</strong></p>
<p>Version 10.0 embeds AI assistance directly into the editing workflow, rather than bolting it on through external plugins or browser extensions. For organisations evaluating AI-augmented productivity, however, the critical question is where the inference actually runs — and that remains open.</p>
<p>The release material reviewed for this article does not confirm whether ONLYOFFICE's AI layer operates entirely on-premises or depends on cloud-hosted models. That distinction is not academic for any deployment with strict data-handling or cross-border transfer constraints: a mandatory third-party AI endpoint complicates compliance reviews in ways that on-premises inference does not. IT decision-makers should query the vendor directly before assuming the AI components can be pointed at self-hosted models or private inference endpoints. If they can, ONLYOFFICE would occupy a genuinely distinct niche: AI-augmented editing without a mandatory dependency on an external cloud provider — a combination neither Copilot nor Google's Workspace AI currently offers to sovereignty-conscious deployments.</p>
<p>A similar verification step applies to language coverage. Traditional Chinese support has been a routine expectation for office suites deployed in Hong Kong, but the release material examined for this article did not spell out the localisation status for version 10.0. Teams with zh-Hant document workflows should confirm interface and proofing-tool coverage in a test deployment before committing.</p>
<p><strong>The familiar self-hosting proposition</strong></p>
<p>Beyond AI, ONLYOFFICE's core pitch is unchanged and remains relevant. The suite offers browser-accessible collaborative editing and native OOXML support for DOCX, XLSX and PPTX files, which reduces the formatting-fidelity problems that plague teams migrating away from Microsoft Office. Version 10.0 layers AI onto an architecture already designed for on-premises or private-cloud deployment — a foundation that matters more as SaaS licensing costs rise and data-sovereignty requirements harden across the region.</p>
<p><strong>Competitive context</strong></p>
<p>LibreOffice remains the most widely deployed open-source office suite, and its breadth of format support and language coverage is unmatched in the open-source field. But ONLYOFFICE's combination of Microsoft-format compatibility, real-time collaboration and now native AI positions it as a credible bridge for organisations transitioning out of proprietary ecosystems in stages rather than in one disruptive cutover — and, in the case of Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace subscribers, a way out of recurring cloud lock-in without sacrificing the formats colleagues still use.</p>
<p><strong>What to watch</strong></p>
<p>Two questions will determine whether 10.0 translates into enterprise adoption: whether the AI features can be fully self-hosted, and whether localisation and enterprise administration tooling keep pace with the feature cadence. Until the first is answered on the record, ONLYOFFICE 10.0 is a strong candidate for a sandbox pilot — with AI data-handling details treated as a compliance question, not a checkbox.</p>

<hr>
<p>ONLYOFFICE 10.0 發布公告中最重要的數字，並非關於人工智能的那一項。根據 <a href="https://www.phoronix.com/news/ONLYOFFICE-10.0">Phoronix 於 2026 年 10 月 13 日的報道</a>，是次開源辦公室生產力套件更新修復了超過 1,000 個 bug——這個數字比任何單一主打功能更能說明該項目的工程成熟度。</p>
<p><strong>穩定性是成熟的指標</strong></p>
<p>對於正在評估 Microsoft 365 或 Google Workspace 替代方案的企業 IT 團隊而言，四位數的 bug 修復數量正是最關鍵的細節。它意味著項目持續投入強化 codebase——這類基礎工作通常是產品獲批投入生產環境的先聲。新功能在評估階段吸引目光；可靠性則決定一套軟件能否通過試點測試。以此衡量，10.0 可視為 LibreOffice 最具知名度的開源挑戰者之一走向成熟的里程碑。</p>
<p><strong>AI 內嵌於編輯器，但關鍵問題仍未解答</strong></p>
<p>10.0 版將 AI 輔助功能直接嵌入編輯工作流程，而非透過外掛插件或瀏覽器 extension 勉強接駁。然而，對於正在評估 AI 增強生產力方案的機構而言，關鍵問題在於推論（inference）實際在哪裡運行——而這一點目前仍然懸而未決。</p>
<p>本文所查閱的發布材料並未證實 ONLYOFFICE 的 AI 層面是否完全在本地（on-premises）運作，抑或依賴雲端託管模型。對於任何在數據處理及跨境轉移方面有嚴格限制的部署而言，這項區別絕非紙上談兵：強制依賴第三方 AI endpoint 會令合規審查複雜化，這是本地推論所不會出現的問題。IT 決策者在假設 AI 組件可以指向自建模型或私有推論 endpoint 之前，應直接向供應商查詢。若答案是肯定的，ONLYOFFICE 將佔據一個真正獨特的市場定位：無需強制依賴外部雲端供應商的 AI 增強編輯體驗——這正是 Copilot 和 Google Workspace AI 目前均未為重視數據主權的部署提供的組合。</p>
<p>語言涵蓋方面亦需要進行同樣的驗證。繁體中文支援向來是香港部署辦公室套件的常規要求，但本文檢視的發布材料並未交代 10.0 版的本地化狀況。使用 zh-Hant 文件工作流程的團隊，應在正式採用前先於測試部署中確認介面及校對工具的涵蓋範圍。</p>
<p><strong>熟悉的自主託管方案</strong></p>
<p>撇開 AI 不談，ONLYOFFICE 的核心賣點維持不變，依然切合市場需要。該套件提供可透過瀏覽器存取的協作編輯功能，以及對 DOCX、XLSX 和 PPTX 檔案的原生 OOXML 支援，從而減輕從 Microsoft Office 遷移團隊常見的格式保真度問題。10.0 版在本來就為本地或私有雲部署而設計的架構之上疊加 AI——隨著 SaaS 授權成本上升，以及各地區數據主權要求日趨嚴格，這項基礎的價值正與日俱增。</p>
<p><strong>競爭形勢</strong></p>
<p>LibreOffice 仍是部署最廣泛的開源辦公室套件，其格式支援及語言涵蓋的廣度在開源領域無出其右。但 ONLYOFFICE 結合 Microsoft 格式相容性、即時協作，加上如今的原生 AI，使其成為一個可信的橋樑方案——適合以分階段方式、而非一次性顛覆性切換過渡出專有生態系統的機構；對於 Microsoft 365 或 Google Workspace 用戶而言，更是在不犧牲同事仍在使用的格式之下，擺脫雲端長期鎖定的出路。</p>
<p><strong>未來觀察重點</strong></p>
<p>兩個問題將決定 10.0 能否轉化為企業採用：AI 功能能否完全自建託管，以及本地化和企業管理工具能否跟上功能更新的步伐。在第一個問題獲得正式回應之前，ONLYOFFICE 10.0 適合作為沙盒試點的有力候選——但 AI 數據處理細節應視為合規問題處理，而非打勾了事的清單項目。</p>
