Title: Linux Networking Adds Another Maintainer as AI-Generated Patches Strain Review Capacity
Content:
<p>The Linux kernel's networking subsystem has gained another maintainer, Phoronix reported — a small but notable staffing boost for an area of the tree that has spent the past year struggling to absorb a surge of AI-assisted contributions.</p>
<p>The appointment arrives against a backdrop that maintainers have described in unusually blunt terms. Linux networking developers have previously said they felt "completely overwhelmed" by the volume of AI- and LLM-driven activity directed at their subsystem, spanning everything from automated bug reports to machine-drafted patches. The pressure has been persistent enough that in a growing number of cases, maintainers have deferred fixes to the next kernel release simply to keep pace with what they describe as seemingly endless incoming work.</p>
<p>That overflow has had concrete consequences beyond scheduling. The same wave of AI activity around seldom-used drivers and other niche networking areas contributed to the removal of various pieces of old networking code.</p>
<p>The promotion of an additional developer into a maintainer role is therefore less a routine announcement than a signal about where the strain sits. Kernel maintainers have long been the scarce resource in the open-source supply chain, but the current cycle has sharpened the imbalance in a specific way: AI tools dramatically lowered the cost of <em>writing</em> patches without lowering the cost of <em>reviewing</em> them. Anyone with a language model can now produce a plausible-looking diff in seconds, but validating it — checking it against subsystem conventions, spotting subtle regressions, and judging whether it should exist at all — still demands the same experienced, time-intensive scrutiny it always did. The bottleneck has shifted decisively from authorship to review.</p>
<p>That asymmetry explains why adding reviewers and maintainers, rather than authors, is the intervention that matters. It also explains the fatigue: maintainers are effectively performing quality control on a firehose of contributions whose authors may have little understanding of the code they have submitted. For projects outside the kernel, the pattern is a useful preview — the same dynamic appears wherever AI-assisted contributions meet volunteer review queues.</p>
<p>Whether the community rebalances through tooling, stricter submission norms or simply more reviewers stepping forward remains the open question — and one the networking subsystem's newest maintainer will be living with directly.</p>
<p><em>Source: Phoronix, "Amid All The AI Generated Patches &amp; Bug Reports, Linux Gains Another Network Maintainer."</em></p>
<hr>
<p>據 Phoronix 報道，Linux kernel 的網絡子系統（networking subsystem）新增了一名 maintainer——對於過去一年一直難以消化 AI 輔助貢獻浪潮的這部分核心代碼而言，雖然只是小幅擴充，卻意義顯著。</p>
<p>是次任命的背景，maintainer 們罕有地以直白措辭加以描述。Linux 網絡開發者此前表示，面對湧向其子系統的 AI 及 LLM 驅動活動，他們感到「完全應付不來」，當中涵蓋由自動化 bug report 到機器撰寫的 patch 等各式各樣的內容。壓力持續不斷，以致越來越多個案中，maintainer 不得不把修復延至下一個 kernel release，只為跟上這批他們形容為源源不絕的 incoming work。</p>
<p>這股溢出效應的影響不限於排程，更有實質後果。同樣一波針對鮮少使用的 driver 及其他冷門網絡範疇的 AI 活動，促成了多段舊有網絡代碼被移除。</p>
<p>因此，多委任一名開發者出任 maintainer，與其說是一則例行公告，不如說是反映壓力所在的一個信號。Kernel maintainer 一直是開源供應鏈中的稀缺資源，惟本輪周期以一種特定方式加劇了這種失衡：AI 工具大幅降低了<em>撰寫</em> patch 的成本，卻沒有降低<em>審核</em> patch 的成本。任何擁有語言模型的人，如今都能在數秒內產生一份看來合理的 diff，然而驗證——對照子系統慣例逐一檢查、找出細微 regression、判斷該 patch 是否根本應當存在——仍然需要一如既往由經驗豐富者投入大量時間的審視。瓶頸已決定性地由撰寫轉移到 review。</p>
<p>這種不對稱，正說明為何增加 reviewer 與 maintainer 而非作者，才是關鍵所在。它同樣解釋了疲態的由來：maintainer 實質上正對一批批 contribution 進行質量控制，而這些 contribution 的作者對自己提交的代碼可能所知無幾。對於 kernel 以外的項目，這種模式是一個有用的預演——凡 AI 輔助貢獻遇上 volunteer review queue 之處，皆會出現相同動態。</p>
<p>社群會否透過工具、更嚴謹的提交規範，抑或只是有更多 reviewer 主動站出來以恢復平衡，仍是未解之題——而網絡子系統最新的 maintainer 將直接與這道難題共處。</p>
