<h4>Active C3RB3R Ransomware Campaign Prompts Urgent Synology Patch Warning</h4>
<p>Synology has told customers to update their network-attached storage appliances immediately, warning that C3RB3R ransomware attacks against its devices are ongoing and rising. The warning was reported by BleepingComputer on 2 November.</p>
<p>Synology has not disclosed a CVE or technical root cause in its advisory, and administrators will not find an attack-vector description to help them triage. What the company does provide is a firm instruction to install the update now, alongside the affected model numbers and DSM version ranges published on its own advisory pages. Fleet decisions should be made against that listing rather than a third-party summary.</p>
<p>The urgency here is structural. A NAS typically holds the files an organisation most needs, and it often sits on the same network as the backups meant to restore them after an attack. When the storage box itself is the target, the data and the recovery path are in the same blast radius. That combination is why a vendor-issued "update immediately" should be treated as an operational task with a deadline — a practical consideration for administrators in Hong Kong and elsewhere running Synology hardware, not a distant threat-intelligence note.</p>
<h5>Admins should also check</h5>
<p>Alongside applying Synology's update, administrators should:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Inventory the fleet.</strong> Identify every unit — including branch, lab, and never-decommissioned boxes — and record its model and current DSM version, treating older releases as in scope until Synology's advisory says otherwise.</li>
<li><strong>Cut off direct exposure.</strong> Confirm the NAS is not reachable from the public internet; remote access should sit behind a VPN or the vendor's authenticated relay, not an open port.</li>
<li><strong>Trim the attack surface.</strong> Disable unused services, close SMB, WebDAV, or FTP endpoints with no remaining business reason, and remove stale or shared credentials.</li>
<li><strong>Harden access and review it.</strong> Enable multi-factor authentication on administrative and remote-access accounts, then check recent login and access logs for unfamiliar sources or off-hours activity.</li>
</ul>
<h5>Verify the backups — and isolate them</h5>
<p>This is the load-bearing recommendation. Ransomware that reaches the NAS frequently reaches backups stored on or beside it, so recovery capacity should not depend on the compromised device.</p>
<ul>
<li>Confirm at least one backup copy exists that the NAS cannot reach — offsite, on separate infrastructure, or offline.</li>
<li>Test-restore a sample file or dataset today; an untested backup is an assumption, not a plan.</li>
<li>Check that backup credentials are separate from NAS administrative credentials.</li>
<li>Where immutable or object-lock backup storage is available, use it for the last known-good copy.</li>
</ul>
<p>Because Synology has not named a specific vulnerability as the only route in, admins should not assume that one patch closes every path an attacker might use. Defence here stays layered: current firmware, restricted exposure, strong logins, and a recovery copy that survives the incident.</p>
<hr>
<h4>C3RB3R 勒索軟件攻擊持續 催生 Synology 緊急修補警告</h4>
<p>Synology 已要求客戶立即更新其 NAS 設備，並警告針對其裝置的 C3RB3R 勒索軟件攻擊正在持續且不斷上升。該警告由 BleepingComputer 於 11 月 2 日報道。</p>
<p>Synology 在其公告中未披露相關 CVE 或技術根因，管理員亦無法從中找到 attack vector 描述以協助分類處理。該公司提供的，是一項明確指示：立即安裝更新，同時在其公告頁面列出受影響型號及 DSM 版本範圍。設備管理決策應依據該清單，而非第三方摘要。</p>
<p>此處的緊迫性源自結構性問題。NAS 通常存放機構最為重要的檔案，而且往往與用於攻擊後還原資料的備份處於同一網絡。當儲存設備本身成為攻擊目標，資料與恢復路徑便同處於同一打擊範圍。正因如此，供應商發出的「立即更新」指示應被視為一項有期限的營運任務——對於香港及其他地區使用 Synology 硬件的管理員而言，這是實際的營運考量，而非遙遠的情報提示。</p>
<h5>管理員亦應檢視以下各項</h5>
<p>在套用 Synology 更新之餘，管理員應：</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>盤點整個設備組合。</strong> 識別每一部機器——包括分支辦公室、實驗室以及從未退役的設備——記錄其型號及目前 DSM 版本，並將較舊版本納入範圍，直至 Synology 公告另有說明為止。</li>
<li><strong>切斷直接暴露。</strong> 確認 NAS 無法從公共互聯網直接存取；遠端存取應透過 VPN 或供應商的認證中轉服務，而非開放端口。</li>
<li><strong>收窄 attack surface。</strong> 停用未使用的服務，關閉已無業務理由的 SMB、WebDAV 或 FTP 端點，並移除過期或共用的憑證。</li>
<li><strong>強化並檢視存取控制。</strong> 在管理及遠端存取帳戶啟用 multi-factor authentication，然後檢查近期登入及存取記錄，留意陌生來源或非工作時間的活動。</li>
</ul>
<h5>驗證備份——並將其隔離</h5>
<p>這是最關鍵的建議。到達 NAS 的勒索軟件通常也會感染儲存在其上或其旁邊的備份，因此恢復能力不應依賴受感染的設備。</p>
<ul>
<li>確認至少存在一份 NAS 無法觸及的備份——可為異地備份、設於獨立基礎設施，或離線保存。</li>
<li>今日即測試還原一個檔案或資料集樣本；未經測試的備份只是假設，並非計劃。</li>
<li>確認備份憑證與 NAS 管理憑證互相分離。</li>
<li>如備份儲存支援不可變（immutable）或 object-lock 功能，應將其用於保存最後一份已知良好的副本。</li>
</ul>
<p>由於 Synology 未指明某一特定漏洞為攻擊者的唯一入侵途徑，管理員不應假設單靠一次修補便能封閉攻擊者可能使用的所有路徑。防禦必須維持多層次部署：最新 firmware、限制暴露、強登入驗證，以及一份能夠在事件中存活的恢復副本。</p>
