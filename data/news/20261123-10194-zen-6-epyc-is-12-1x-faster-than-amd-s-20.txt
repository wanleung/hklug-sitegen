Title: Zen 6 EPYC Is 12.1x Faster Than AMD's 2017 Server Chips — and 133x in AI
Content:
<p>AMD's newest EPYC 9006 "Venice" server processors deliver a 12.1x geometric-mean performance uplift over the company's first-generation EPYC "Naples" parts, with AI workloads showing gains of up to 133x, according to benchmarks published by Phoronix.</p>
<p>The comparison, released as AMD made the Zen 6-based EPYC 9006 SP7 lineup available this month, measures the flagship EPYC 9996 and EPYC 9686F against the Zen 1 Naples chips, which launched in 2017. Phoronix framed the exercise as a decade-long accounting of AMD's server performance trajectory rather than a head-to-head review of a single product generation.</p>
<p><strong>Cumulative progress, not one generational leap</strong></p>
<p>The most important caveat is embedded in the framing itself: the 12.1x geometric-mean figure represents cumulative progress across five generational steps — Naples, Rome, Milan, Genoa and Turin, and now Venice — not a single generational step. Treating it as anything else would misrepresent how server roadmaps actually compound. Readers weighing a hardware refresh should read the number as the total value of a ten-year upgrade cycle, not what they would gain by moving from a Zen 5 Turin chip to a Zen 6 Venice chip.</p>
<p>That distinction matters because AMD's more recent generational gains are far more modest — Phoronix describes the Zen 6 EPYC 9686F and EPYC 9996 as showing "excellent generational gains" over Zen 5 Turin, a much smaller delta than the decade-spanning comparison suggests.</p>
<p><strong>The AI number tells a different story</strong></p>
<p>The 133x peak uplift in AI workloads is the more striking figure, and it underscores an important asymmetry: performance gains over the last decade have not been distributed evenly across workload types. General compute has grown roughly twelvefold; AI workloads by a factor of 133. Organizations benchmarking only generic CPU throughput will systematically understate the value of a refresh if any part of their roadmap touches inference or AI-adjacent workloads, while teams with heavy AI requirements may find a far stronger case for upgrading sooner rather than later.</p>
<p><strong>Competitive positioning</strong></p>
<p>Phoronix also reported that the Zen 6 Venice parts deliver a sharp performance lead over competing offerings from Intel and NVIDIA in tested workloads. The review benchmarks the new chips against current server parts from both vendors; the specific model-by-model breakdowns are detailed in Phoronix's full test suite. Readers evaluating a purchase should note that NVIDIA does not ship a general-purpose server CPU competing directly with EPYC — its relevance in this context is primarily around accelerator and platform comparisons rather than an apples-to-apples socket-level CPU race.</p>
<p>Independent benchmark coverage of this kind carries more weight than vendor-supplied performance claims precisely because the test methodology and workload selection sit outside AMD's control. For infrastructure teams planning multi-year server refreshes, a decade-long, workload-diverse geometric mean is a more defensible planning input than any single marketing slide.</p>
<p>Whether the Zen 6 chips represent compelling value will depend on the workload mix — but the baseline for "how far server CPUs have come" now sits at 12.1x for general compute and triple digits for AI.</p>

<hr>
<p>根據 Phoronix 公布的基準測試結果，AMD 最新的 EPYC 9006「Venice」伺服器處理器，相較該公司第一代 EPYC「Naples」產品，幾何平均效能提升達 12.1 倍，其中 AI 工作負載的增幅更高達 133 倍。</p>
<p>這項比較在 AMD 於本月推出基於 Zen 6 的 EPYC 9006 SP7 系列之際發布，將旗艦型號 EPYC 9996 及 EPYC 9686F，與 2017 年推出的 Zen 1 Naples 晶片作對比。Phoronix 將此舉定位為對 AMD 伺服器效能發展軌跡的十年總結盤點，而非單一產品世代之間的正面比較。</p>
<p><strong>累積進步，而非單一世代跨越</strong></p>
<p>最重要的注意事項已蘊含於這個框架本身：12.1 倍的幾何平均數字，代表的是橫跨五個世代步進——Naples、Rome、Milan、Genoa、Turin，以至現時的 Venice——的累積進步，而非單一世代的跨越。若將其解讀為其他含義，便會誤導伺服器路線圖的實際複利累積方式。正在考慮硬件更新的讀者，應將此數字理解為十年升級周期的總價值，而非由 Zen 5 Turin 晶片升級至 Zen 6 Venice 晶片所能獲得的增幅。</p>
<p>這點區別十分重要，因為 AMD 較近期的世代增幅其實溫和得多——Phoronix 形容 Zen 6 EPYC 9686F 及 EPYC 9996 相較 Zen 5 Turin 有「優異的世代進步」，但此差距遠較跨越十年的比較所暗示的為小。</p>
<p><strong>AI 數字揭示另一個故事</strong></p>
<p>AI 工作負載高達 133 倍的峰值提升，是更為矚目的數字，並突顯一項重要的不對稱性：過去十年的效能增長，並未在各類工作負載之間平均分佈。一般運算效能約增長十二倍；AI 工作負載則增長 133 倍。若機構只以通用 CPU 吞吐量作基準測試，只要其路線圖任何部分涉及 inference 或與 AI 相關的工作負載，便會系統性低估更新換代的價值；反之，對 AI 有沉重需求的團隊，可能發現提早升級的理由遠比預期強烈。</p>
<p><strong>競爭定位</strong></p>
<p>Phoronix 另外報道，Zen 6 Venice 產品在受測工作負載中，較 Intel 及 NVIDIA 的同類產品有明顯的效能領先。是次評測將新晶片與兩家廠商現役的伺服器產品作基準比較；逐一型號的詳細數據載於 Phoronix 的完整測試套件。有意選購的讀者應注意，NVIDIA 並未推出與 EPYC 直接競爭的通用伺服器 CPU——其在此語境下的關聯性，主要在於 accelerator 及平台層面的比較，而非同級 socket 層面的 CPU 直接較量。</p>
<p>此類獨立基準測試報導之所以較供應商自行提供的效能宣稱更有份量，正因為測試方法學及工作負載的選擇並不受 AMD 控制。對規劃多年度伺服器更新的基礎設施團隊而言，跨越十年、涵蓋多元工作負載的幾何平均值，比任何單張市場推廣 slide 更站得住腳。</p>
<p>Zen 6 晶片是否具備極具吸引力的性價比，仍取決於工作負載組合——但「伺服器 CPU 究竟進步了多少」的基準線，如今已定於一般運算 12.1 倍、AI 則達到三位數。</p>
