Title: Framework's 192GB Gorgon Halo Desktop Pre-Orders Open — But HK Buyers Should Verify the Fine Print First
Content:
<p>Framework Computer has opened pre-orders for a new Framework Desktop built around AMD's Ryzen AI Max+ "Gorgon Halo" system-on-chip, with a configuration featuring 192GB of LPDDR5x-8533 memory starting at USD $6,799, according to a report published by Phoronix. The machine targets buyers who need very large amounts of memory on-device — AI researchers, media studios and developers working with local models — rather than the typical home user.</p>
<p>Details in the source remain unconfirmed in places, and buyers should treat the following as reportage to be verified rather than settled fact. The Phoronix headline cites the AMD Ryzen AI Max+ PRO 495, while the article's own summary refers to a model numbered 496. "Gorgon Halo" appears to be AMD's marketing name for the SoC family, but Framework has not published independent confirmation in the material we have seen. None of this changes the headline story — but if you are spending nearly USD $7,000, you should confirm the exact part number on Framework's live product page yourself.</p>
<p>There is one specification that deserves more attention than any other. The 192GB of unified memory is soldered to the SoC package. It cannot be upgraded after purchase. That puts the desktop at odds with Framework's core selling proposition: a modular, repairable, long-lived machine that owners can expand over time. For a company whose brand identity is built on upgradeability, the decision to lock down the single most expensive component is a meaningful shift — and for a buyer at this price point, it is the one thing that cannot be corrected later.</p>
<h4>What Hong Kong buyers should verify before ordering</h4>
<p>Framework has published no confirmed Hong Kong pricing, shipping arrangements, tax treatment or warranty terms in the material available to us. The USD $6,799 figure is a US starting price, not a Hong Kong landed cost — duties, freight, import handling and currency conversion all sit in between, and the store's shipping calculator may not accurately reflect what a HK purchaser will actually pay. Before committing, run the following checks yourself:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Is the desktop actually shipped to Hong Kong, and by whom?</strong> Confirm whether Framework's US online store will deliver to a HK address, and whether a third-party reseller or forwarder is the only route.</li>
<li><strong>What does the landed cost look like?</strong> The USD $6,799 starting price excludes local duties, import handling and shipping — all of which are currently unconfirmed. Convert at a live rate, not a headline figure.</li>
<li><strong>What warranty coverage applies outside the US?</strong> Framework's modularity programme promises parts and repairability, but repairability depends on parts actually being available and shipped to your region. Confirm warranty terms, service logistics and parts supply for a HK purchaser in writing.</li>
<li><strong>Which SKU are you actually buying?</strong> Resolve the 495/496 discrepancy on the official product page.</li>
<li><strong>What is the experience of other non-US buyers?</strong> Before relying on the official store's shipping calculator, research Framework's track record with deliveries, customs and warranty handling outside the United States independently — community forums such as r/framework, reseller networks and owner groups are where gaps between marketing and delivery show up first.</li>
</ul>
<h4>Where a local-AI purchase case fits</h4>
<p>Some buyers considering a large-memory workstation are doing so to run AI workloads on-device, in part because of concerns about where data ends up when it is sent to a third-party cloud service. In Hong Kong, organisations handling personal data are subject to the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance, and data-residency expectations can shape procurement decisions. That is a plausible purchase trigger for this class of machine — but it is a reason to verify your own compliance posture, not a claim that this product satisfies any particular requirement. Organisations should consult their own advisers on storage and transfer obligations.</p>
<h4>Cheaper alternatives worth considering</h4>
<p>The USD $6,799 entry point buys a genuinely unusual amount of unified memory, but buyers should compare against conventional workstation configurations — a discrete GPU workstation or multi-GPU server — where memory is upgradeable and the platform is already proven in the market. If the workload tolerates cloud inference, those options may cost substantially less per usable token of throughput. If it does not, Framework's desktop remains one of very few single-socket options with this much unified memory on a desktop.</p>
<h4>Repairability, honestly</h4>
<p>Framework has a genuine track record on right-to-repair, and its modularity claims are best attributed to the company's own marketing. The 192GB soldered memory configuration, however, cuts against that argument. Buyers should weigh modularity rhetoric against what is actually user-serviceable in this specific SKU.</p>
<p>Framework has not yet published confirmed Hong Kong pricing, warranty terms or ship dates in the material available to us. Once those details appear on the live pre-order page, we will follow up with a Hong Kong-specific landed-cost breakdown.</p>

<h4>香港買家落訂前應查證的事項</h4>
<p>就我們目前掌握的資料，Framework 並未公布任何已確認的香港定價、寄貨安排、稅務處理或保養條款。USD $6,799 是美國市場的起始價，並非香港的落地成本——當中牽涉關稅、運費、進口清關費用及貨幣兌換，而商店的運費計算器亦未必準確反映香港買家實際需要支付的金額。落訂前，應自行進行以下核實：</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>該桌面電腦是否真的會寄到香港，由誰負責寄出？</strong> 確認 Framework 的美國網上商店是否會送貨至香港地址，以及是否只能透過第三方經銷商或轉運服務購買。</li>
<li><strong>落地成本（landed cost）是多少？</strong> USD $6,799 的起始價不包括本地關稅、進口清關費用及運費——這些目前全部未獲確認。應以實時匯率換算，而非只看標價。</li>
<li><strong>美國以外的買家享有什麼保養保障？</strong> Framework 的模組化計劃承諾零件供應及可維修性，但可維修性取決於零件是否真的有貨及寄送到你所在地區。應以書面確認香港買家的保養條款、維修物流及零件供應。</li>
<li><strong>你實際購買的是哪一個 SKU？</strong> 請在官方產品頁面解決 495/496 的差異問題。</li>
<li><strong>其他非美國買家的實際體驗如何？</strong> 在依賴官方商店的運費計算器之前，應自行查證 Framework 在美國以外地區的寄貨、清關及保養處理往績——r/framework 等網上社群論壇、經銷網絡及用家群組往往是市場宣傳與實際交付之間出現落差的首要地方。</li>
</ul>
<h4>本地 AI 購買動機的適用性</h4>
<p>部分考慮購買大記憶體工作站的人士，是為了在機上執行 AI 工作負載，部分原因是憂慮資料傳送至第三方雲端服務後的去向。在香港，處理個人資料的機構受《個人資料（私隱）條例》規管，而資料存放地（data residency）的期望亦會影響採購決定。這確實可能是這類機器的一個合理購買動機——但這只代表你應自行查證合規狀況，而非本產品必定符合某項特定要求的聲明。各機構應就資料儲存及轉移的責任諮詢自身法律顧問。</p>
<h4>其他值得考慮的平價替代方案</h4>
<p>USD $6,799 的起始價確實買到異常大量的 unified memory，但買家應與傳統工作站配置作比較——例如配備獨立 GPU（discrete GPU）的工作站或多 GPU 伺服器，這類方案的記憶體可升級，平台亦已歷經市場考驗。如果工作負載可以接受雲端推理（cloud inference），這些方案每個可用 token 吞吐量的成本可能低得多。如果不能接受，Framework 這台桌面電腦仍是極少數在桌面上配備如此大量 unified memory 的單 socket 選擇之一。</p>
<h4>維修性，坦白說</h4>
<p>Framework 在維修權（right-to-repair）方面確實有實際往績，其模組化宣傳則宜視作公司自身的市場推廣。然而，192GB 焊接記憶體的配置與此論述背道而馳。買家應權衡模組化的宣傳口號，與這個特定 SKU 實際上有哪些部件可由用家自行更換。</p>
