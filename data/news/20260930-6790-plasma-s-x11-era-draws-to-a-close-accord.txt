<h3>Plasma's X11 Era Draws to a Close, According to Akademy 2026 Talk</h3>
<p>KDE Plasma appears set to retire its X11 session, if the picture painted at this year's Akademy is any guide. Marco Martin, a KDE contributor who spends most of his time on Plasma, delivered the year-in-review talk at the conference in Graz, Austria, with LWN.net reporting on the session.</p>
<p>The most consequential item in the preview was the plan to drop X11 support — something LWN.net's account describes as having been signalled for some time rather than announced abruptly. What remains unknown is when. KDE has published no firm removal date, and Martin's presentation should be read as a statement of intent about where the project is heading, not a shipping schedule.</p>
<p>The direction is consistent with the rest of the 6.x series, where the Wayland session has been the default and developer attention has shifted accordingly. For organisations running Plasma in public-sector, education, enterprise and kiosk environments, the practical concerns are the familiar ones: screen capture, remote-desktop access, automation and accessibility tooling built on X11-era interfaces. XWayland mitigates parts of that gap without replacing it.</p>
<p>Anyone planning around the change should assume a multi-release horizon and audit X11-dependent tooling now, rather than when a date lands. Confirmed milestones belong to KDE's own communications — the Plasma blog and the 6.x release notes — not to extrapolation from conference talks.</p>
<p>LWN.net's report of the session is available to subscribers at https://lwn.net/Articles/1096518/.</p>

<h3>Akademy 2026 演講指 Plasma 的 X11 時代即將落幕</h3>
<p>綜合今年 Akademy 大會呈現的圖景來看，KDE Plasma 似乎即將淘汰其 X11 session。大部分時間投入 Plasma 開發的 KDE 貢獻者 Marco Martin 在奧地利格拉茨（Graz）舉行的大會上發表了年度回顧演講，LWN.net 就此 session 作了報導。</p>
<p>預覽中影響最深遠的一項，是移除 X11 支援的計劃 — LWN.net 的記述指出，這訊息早有跡象，並非突然宣佈。目前未知的是確實日期。KDE 尚未公佈明確的移除時間表，Martin 的演講應被視為該專案未來方向的意向聲明，而非發佈日程。</p>
<p>這一方向與 6.x 系列的整體發展一致：Wayland session 已成為預設選項，開發者的注意力亦隨之轉移。對於在公共部門、教育、企業及 kiosk 環境中運行 Plasma 的機構而言，實際關注點仍是那些熟悉的問題：螢幕擷取、遙距桌面存取，以及建立在 X11 時代介面上的自動化與無障礙工具。XWayland 能減輕部分差距，但無法完全取代。</p>
<p>計劃因應這次改變的機構，應假設需要跨越多個 release 的時間線，並立即審視依賴 X11 的工具，而不是等到公佈日期後才行動。已確認的里程碑應以 KDE 自身的公佈為準 — 即 Plasma 網誌及 6.x release notes — 而非從會議演講中推測。</p>
