Title: Fortinet and Palo Alto Firewalls Hit in Campaign Exposing 70,000+ Enterprise Credentials
Content:
<p>A large-scale campaign aimed at internet-exposed network edge devices has yielded a credential haul in the tens of thousands — more than 70,000 enterprise secrets, according to cybersecurity researchers whose findings were reported by The Hacker News earlier this month.</p>
<p>The campaign zeroes in on firewalls and VPN gateways: the appliances that sit at the outermost boundary of corporate networks. Products named in the disclosure include Fortinet FortiGate firewalls and Palo Alto Networks GlobalProtect VPN gateways. Researchers said artifacts from their investigation indicate that campaign operators obtained access to numerous organizations worldwide and attempted to further compromise some victims.</p>
<p>The researchers' names, any campaign designation, specific CVE identifiers, and vendor responses could not be verified from the available material and are not asserted here; claims of a particular flaw number or attribution circulating elsewhere should be treated with caution until they appear in the original disclosure.</p>
<p>What makes this relevant beyond the individual products named is the mechanics of the exposure itself. Edge appliances of these families are routinely reachable from the public internet — by design, for remote access and branch connectivity, and by accident, when management interfaces and admin portals are left open. Any organisation running the same appliance classes, wherever it is located, faces the same reachability question.</p>
<p>For defenders, the story lands as a concrete audit task rather than an abstract warning.</p>
<p><strong>1. Inventory every edge device facing the internet.</strong> Start with firewalls, SSL VPN portals, and branch appliances — not just the core data centre. Shadow deployments registered by a regional office years ago and never documented are the most common blind spot.</p>
<p><strong>2. Check exposure before checking patch levels.</strong> An appliance cannot be exploited remotely if it is not remotely reachable. Verify that management interfaces, admin portals, and VPN login pages are not exposed directly to the internet, and prefer access restricted to named source networks or delivered through a zero-trust access layer.</p>
<p><strong>3. Rotate firewall and VPN credentials — including shared and service accounts.</strong> Credential dumps of this size tend to be replayed for months. Prioritise administrative accounts, accounts reused across sites, and any password that has never been rotated since deployment. Enforce multi-factor authentication on every remote-access path that supports it.</p>
<p><strong>4. Watch for the foothold, not just the initial hit.</strong> The researchers noted that operators attempted to move deeper into some victims. That is the pattern that turns an appliance breach into an enterprise-wide incident: a foothold on the gateway becomes lateral movement into identity systems and file shares. Hunt for new administrative accounts, unexpected outbound connections from the appliance, and configuration changes made outside change windows.</p>
<p>Edge-device hygiene — reachability, patching, credential rotation, and monitoring — is now a first-order security control, not routine housekeeping. The researchers' observation that operators tried to move beyond the initial foothold points to the practical test for any team defending these appliances: not whether the gateway can be breached, but how quickly a compromised one can be isolated once it is.</p>

<p>一場針對互聯網上暴露的網絡邊界設備的大規模攻擊行動，已竊取數以萬計的憑證——超過 7 萬組企業機密資料。網絡安全研究人員的調查結果本月早些時候經 The Hacker News 報道。</p>
<p>該攻擊行動鎖定防火牆及 VPN gateway：即位於企業網絡最外層邊界的設備。披露中點名的產品包括 Fortinet FortiGate 防火牆及 Palo Alto Networks GlobalProtect VPN gateway。研究人員表示，調查所得的跡象顯示，攻擊者已成功入侵全球多個組織，並嘗試進一步攻陷部分受害機構。</p>
<p>研究人員身份、行動代號、特定 CVE 編號及廠商回應，均未能從現有資料核實，本文亦不作此等聲稱；外界流傳的特定漏洞編號或攻擊者歸屬，應審慎看待，待其在原始披露中出現方宜採信。</p>
<p>此事件的意義不限於被點名的個別產品，而在於暴露問題本身的機制。這類邊界設備通常可從公眾互聯網直接連達——部分屬刻意設計，用作遠端接入及分支機構連線；部分則屬意外，源於管理介面及 admin portal 未有妥善關閉。任何採用同類設備的機構，不論位於何地，都面對同樣的可連達性問題。</p>
<p>對防禦方而言，這則新聞的啟示是一項具體的審計任務，而非抽象警告。</p>
<p><strong>1. 盤點所有面向互聯網的邊界設備。</strong> 由防火牆、SSL VPN portal 及分支機構設備開始——而不僅是核心數據中心。多年前由地區辦公室自行部署、從未記錄在案的「影子部署」，是最常見的盲點。</p>
<p><strong>2. 先檢查暴露情況，再檢查補丁版本。</strong> 設備若無法從遠端連達，便無法被遠端利用。須確認管理介面、admin portal 及 VPN 登入頁面沒有直接暴露於互聯網，並優先將存取限制於指定來源網絡，或透過 zero-trust access 層提供。</p>
<p><strong>3. 輪換防火牆及 VPN 憑證——包括共用帳戶及服務帳戶。</strong> 規模如此龐大的憑證外洩通常會被持續重用數月。應優先處理管理員帳戶、跨站點重用的帳戶，以及自部署以來從未更換的密碼。凡支援多因素驗證的遠端接入途徑，均應強制啟用。</p>
<p><strong>4. 留意立足點，而不只是首次入侵。</strong> 研究人員指出，攻擊者曾嘗試深入部分受害機構。正是這一模式，使單一設備遭入侵演變成全企業事故：gateway 上的立足點可延伸至身份系統及文件共享的橫向移動。應搜尋新增的管理員帳戶、設備發出的異常 outbound 連線，以及在變更窗口之外作出的配置改動。</p>
<p>邊界設備保養——可連達性、補丁、憑證輪換及監控——如今是一級安全控制措施，而非例行雜務。研究人員觀察到攻擊者嘗試超越初始立足點繼續推進，這正點出了防禦此類設備的團隊所要面對的實質考驗：不在於 gateway 是否會被攻破，而在於一旦被攻破，能多快將其隔離。</p>
