Title: Four Years On, MSI's Coreboot-Powered Boards Get a Fresh Dasharo Release
Content:
<p>Firmware consultancy 3mdeb has published updated Dasharo builds for two retail MSI desktop motherboards it ported to Coreboot roughly four years ago — the MSI PRO Z690-A and the MSI PRO Z790-P. The release targets owners of Intel Alder Lake and Raptor Lake era hardware, with downloads and a changelog posted to 3mdeb's Dasharo release pages.</p>
<h4>A port that stayed alive</h4>
<p>When 3mdeb first brought Coreboot to the Z690-A, and later to the Z790-P, the achievement stood out because these are ordinary, purchasable desktop motherboards rather than reference platforms or server gear. Porting Coreboot to modern consumer motherboards demands substantial work around memory training, integrated graphics and vendor-specific silicon initialization — work that motherboard makers themselves rarely fund.</p>
<p>Dasharo is 3mdeb's Coreboot-based firmware distribution, adding a boot interface, configuration tooling and a maintenance model on top of Coreboot itself. It is aimed at users and organisations that want a reviewable firmware stack on hardware they actually own.</p>
<p>The story here is not the feature list. It is that a maintenance cycle happened at all, four years after the port landed. Open-source firmware efforts frequently stall once initial bring-up is done; microcode updates and security patches quietly lapse, and users drift back to the stock vendor BIOS. A follow-up release on retail boards, years later, is comparatively rare.</p>
<h4>Free firmware, paid assurance</h4>
<p>The two halves of the support picture need to be kept apart. The builds themselves are downloadable at no cost, and the upstream project is open to inspection and community contribution. Sustained, guaranteed maintenance — the kind an organisation would want before deploying the firmware at scale — sits on 3mdeb's commercial side as a paid offering. In practice, that consulting model is one of the few viable ways to keep Coreboot ports alive on hardware whose vendors are not paying for them.</p>
<h4>Flash with care</h4>
<p>Prospective flashers should note the boundaries of the claim to openness. Projects in this space typically retain some reliance on vendor-supplied proprietary components, and Dasharo is no exception: it is open source in the sense that its code is reviewable and modifiable, not in the sense that it is entirely blob-free.</p>
<p>Flashing also carries real risk. Installing a Coreboot-based image on an unsupported board revision, or interrupting the write process, can leave a motherboard unbootable without external recovery hardware. Owners should check their exact board revision against the compatibility notes and take a firmware backup before proceeding — and ideally have an external programmer on hand as a recovery path.</p>
<h4>Why it matters</h4>
<p>MSI sells its PRO-series boards in Hong Kong through normal retail channels, so the hardware covered by this release is readily available locally. MSI did not commission or endorse the ports; the work originated with 3mdeb.</p>
<p>For anyone here specifying x86 desktops and weighing a vendor's long-term BIOS support against an independent firmware project, the more useful signal is whether updates keep arriving years after launch. On that count, this release is a modest but genuine positive.</p>
<p>The original report was published by Phoronix; builds and changelog are available from 3mdeb's Dasharo release pages.</p>

<p>韌體顧問公司 3mdeb 已為兩款零售版 MSI 桌面主機板發佈新版 Dasharo 韌體——這兩款主機板正是其約四年前移植至 Coreboot 的 MSI PRO Z690-A 及 MSI PRO Z790-P。是次發佈對象為 Intel Alder Lake 及 Raptor Lake 時代硬體的用戶，下載與變更記錄已上載至 3mdeb 的 Dasharo 發佈頁面。</p>
<h4>一次持續運作的移植</h4>
<p>當 3mdeb 最初將 Coreboot 移植至 Z690-A、其後再移植至 Z790-P 之際，此成就之所以矚目，在於這些是市面有售的普通桌面主機板，而非參考平台或伺服器級裝置。將 Coreboot 移植至現代消費級主機板，牽涉大量圍繞記憶體訓練（memory training）、整合圖形（integrated graphics）及廠商專屬晶片初始化的工作——而主機板製造商本身鮮少出資支援此類工作。</p>
<p>Dasharo 是 3mdeb 基於 Coreboot 的韌體發行版本，在 Coreboot 之上疊加開機介面、配置工具及維護機制，對象是希望在自己實際擁有的硬體上使用可供審查的韌體堆疊的用戶與機構。</p>
<p>是次報導的重點並非功能清單，而在於移植完成四年之後，竟然仍有一次維護週期出現。開放原始碼韌體項目往往在初步啟動完成後便停滯不前：微碼更新與保安修補悄然失效，用戶最終退回原廠 BIOS。零售主機板多年後仍獲推出後續版本，相對罕見。</p>
<h4>免費韌體，付費保證</h4>
<p>支援機制的兩個層面須加以區分。編譯版本本身可免費下載，上游項目亦開放予公眾審查及社群貢獻。持續而有保證的維護——即機構在大規模部署韌體前所期望的那種保障——則屬 3mdeb 商業服務範疇，需付費購買。就實務而言，這套顧問模式是讓 Coreboot 移植在廠商未有出資的硬體上持續運作的少數可行途徑之一。</p>
<h4>刷寫需謹慎</h4>
<p>有意刷寫的用戶應留意其開放性聲明的界限。此領域的項目通常仍依賴部分廠商提供的專有元件，Dasharo 亦不例外：它的開放原始碼是指其代碼可供審查及修改，而非指它完全不含 blob（二進制韌體塊）。</p>
<p>刷寫亦存在實際風險。在不受支援的主機板版本上安裝基於 Coreboot 的映像，或中斷寫入過程，均可能令主機板無法開機，且沒有外部復原硬體便無法補救。用戶應查閱相容性說明，核對自己主機板的確切版本，並在進行前備份韌體——理想情況下更應備有外部程式燒錄器（external programmer）作為復原途徑。</p>
<h4>重要性所在</h4>
<p>MSI 的 PRO 系列主機板透過香港一般零售渠道發售，因此是次發佈所涵蓋的硬體在本地容易購得。MSI 並未委託或背書這些移植工作；相關工作源自 3mdeb。</p>
<p>對本港任何正在規劃 x86 桌機、並在廠商長期 BIOS 支援與獨立韌體項目之間作出取捨的人士而言，更有參考價值的指標是：產品推出多年後更新是否仍持續到來。就此而言，是次發佈是一個適度但確實的正面訊號。</p>
