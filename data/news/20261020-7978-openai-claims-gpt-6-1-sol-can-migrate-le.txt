Title: OpenAI claims GPT-6.1 Sol can migrate legacy code — but the benchmarks aren't public yet
Content:
<p>OpenAI has said its newer GPT-6.1 Sol models are, in the company's own assessment, "dramatically better" at writing and migrating software written in older programming languages. The claim was relayed by BleepingComputer, which reported the vendor's announcement without presenting any independent evaluation of the capability.</p>
<p>The headline figure is OpenAI's, not a measured third-party result, and the announcement as covered does not include the benchmark specifics behind it: which legacy languages were tested, which repositories were used, or what counts as a successful migration. No pricing or availability detail was included in the coverage either, leaving would-be adopters without a documented path to procurement until OpenAI publishes further detail.</p>
<p>That distinction matters more than usual for this class of claim. Legacy migration is one of the areas where language models have historically struggled most — the code being moved is often undocumented, tightly coupled to business rules that exist only in production, and written in languages with thin training data. If OpenAI's self-assessment holds up under published methodology, it would be a meaningful data point. Until then, the burden of proof sits with the vendor.</p>
<p><strong>Why the use case outlasts the announcement</strong></p>
<p>Even if the specific GPT-6.1 Sol claim does not survive scrutiny, the underlying problem is not in dispute. Organisations running systems written in COBOL, Fortran, Visual Basic 6, or older Java and PHP versions face a shrinking pool of engineers who can safely modify them, and hand-rewrites remain one of the riskier projects an IT department can attempt — with regressions frequently surfacing long after the original authors have left.</p>
<p>The value of the announcement, then, is less the model than the signal about where OpenAI sees enterprise demand. A vendor staking a release narrative on migration work is a reasonable proxy for how much budget the market is spending to get off ageing stacks.</p>
<p>The story is broadly relevant to Hong Kong IT professionals, whose teams routinely maintain long-lived enterprise systems and compete for a comparatively thin talent pool in retired languages. The practical question for those teams is unchanged by any press release: does a model actually reduce the cost and risk of moving a specific component, measured against the alternative?</p>
<p><strong>What a credible pilot looks like</strong></p>
<p>The sensible response is a bounded pilot, not a platform commitment. Start with a self-contained component that already has meaningful test coverage, so that "the migration works" is a fact the tests can establish rather than an opinion. Where a human-written migration of comparable scope exists, compare against it directly. Track review time separately from generation time — the bottleneck in legacy work is usually understanding intent, not producing syntax. And treat generated code as a compliance artifact: licence provenance and data-handling obligations apply exactly as they would to anything else entering the codebase.</p>
<p>If those numbers hold, scale. If they do not, the pilot cost is the lesson.</p>
<p>OpenAI has not, in the reporting available at time of writing, published the underlying methodology. If and when those benchmarks appear in full, this story warrants an update — and any reader considering a purchase should be reading OpenAI's technical documentation alongside the BleepingComputer coverage, not instead of it.</p>
<hr>
<p>OpenAI 表示，其較新的 GPT-6.1 Sol 模型，在公司自行評估下，撰寫及遷移以較舊編程語言寫成的軟件方面「大幅優勝」。有關說法由 BleepingComputer 轉述，該報道在引述廠商公告的同時，並未就這項能力提供任何獨立評估。</p>
<p>核心數據來自 OpenAI 本身，而非第三方實測結果；而有關報道亦未載明支撐該說法的基準測試細節：測試了哪些舊有編程語言、使用了哪些 repositories，以及怎樣才算成功遷移。報道同樣沒有提及任何定價或供應詳情，令潛在採購者在 OpenAI 公布進一步詳情之前，沒有可供遵循的採購路徑。</p>
<p>對於這類說法而言，此一區別比平時更為重要。舊有系統遷移一直是語言模型歷來最難應付的領域之一——被搬移的代碼往往缺乏文件、與只存在於生產環境的業務規則緊密耦合，而且所用的訓練數據十分薄弱。如果 OpenAI 的自我評估在公開方法論下依然成立，那將是一個有意義的數據點。在此之前，舉證責任仍在廠商一方。</p>
<p><strong>為何相關用例的生命力超越公告本身</strong></p>
<p>即使 GPT-6.1 Sol 的具體說法經不起檢視，底層問題本身並無爭議。運行以 COBOL、Fortran、Visual Basic 6 或較舊版本 Java、PHP 編寫的系統的機構，正面對可安全修改這些系統的工程師日益萎縮的隊伍；而人工重寫至今仍是 IT 部門風險最高的項目之一——回歸問題往往在原作者離職很久之後才浮現。</p>
<p>因此，這則公告的價值與其說在於模型本身，不如說在於它透露了 OpenAI 眼中企業需求所在。一家廠商把產品發布的敘事押在遷移工作上，足以合理地反映市場正投放多少預算，以擺脫日漸老化的技術堆疊。</p>
<p>這則報道對香港 IT 從業人員整體而言具有參考價值：他們的團隊日常需要維護壽命很長的企業系統，並在已退役語言的範疇內爭奪相對稀薄的人才庫。對這些團隊而言，實質問題不會因任何新聞稿而改變：相比其他替代方案，某個模型是否真的能降低遷移特定元件的成本與風險？</p>
<p><strong>可信的試點計劃應是怎樣的</strong></p>
<p>合理的做法是進行有邊界的試點計劃（pilot），而非對整個平台作出承諾。由一個已具備有意義測試覆蓋率的獨立元件開始，令「遷移成功」成為測試可以確認的事實，而非主觀意見。如有範圍相若的人工遷移版本可作對照，應直接比較。審查時間應與生成時間分開追蹤——舊有系統工作的瓶頸通常在於理解意圖，而非產生語法。同時，生成的代碼應視為合規產物：程式碼授權來源及數據處理責任，與任何進入 codebase 的其他內容完全相同。</p>
<p>如果這些數字成立，就擴大試點規模；如果不成立，試點計劃的成本本身就是教訓。</p>
<p>截至撰稿時，在可得的報道中，OpenAI 尚未公布底層方法論。當相關基準測試完整公開時，本報導值得更新——而任何考慮購買的讀者，應該將 OpenAI 的技術文件與 BleepingComputer 的報道一併閱讀，而非以後者取而代之。</p>
