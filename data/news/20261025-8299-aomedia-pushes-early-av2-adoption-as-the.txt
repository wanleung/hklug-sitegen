Title: AOMedia Pushes Early AV2 Adoption as the SVT-AV2 Encoder Takes Shape

<h3>AOMedia Pushes Early AV2 Adoption as the SVT-AV2 Encoder Takes Shape</h3>
<p>The Alliance for Open Media (AOMedia) has issued a press release highlighting early adoption momentum for AV2, its next-generation royalty-free video codec, coming a few months after the AV2 v1.0 specification was finalized in May. The push, covered by Phoronix, signals the consortium's intent to move AV2 from specification to deployment — even as a key high-performance encoder for the format is still under active development.</p>
<p>AV2 is positioned as the successor to AV1, continuing AOMedia's royalty-free licensing model. That model is the central commercial argument for the codec: unlike licensed alternatives, AV2 is designed to be implemented without per-decoder patent royalties, a structure that materially changes the cost equation for organisations deploying video at scale — from streaming platforms to enterprise communications infrastructure.</p>
<p>According to Phoronix's reporting, AOMedia's release framed adoption activity around AV2 as early-stage. The consortium is making the case that interest from implementers is real, while the specification itself remains freshly minted. The distinction matters: a v1.0 spec establishes what the codec does, but it does not by itself deliver shipping encoders, decoders, hardware acceleration, or content pipelines. Deployability follows software and silicon, not press releases.</p>
<h4>Encoder progress: SVT-AV2</h4>
<p>On the tooling side, attention is focused on SVT-AV2, the next-generation encoder in the Scalable Video Technology family. Its predecessor, SVT-AV1, has become one of the reference-quality software encoders for AV1 workloads, and SVT-AV2 is intended to carry that lineage forward for the new format.</p>
<p>Per the source coverage, SVT-AV2 is being developed — a status that should be read strictly. It is not yet a shipped, production-ready encoder in the manner of SVT-AV1 today, and the reporting does not establish feature parity, performance benchmarks, or a release timeline. For teams evaluating codec roadmaps, that places SVT-AV2 in the "watch" category rather than the "adopt" category.</p>
<h4>Why the royalty-free angle carries weight</h4>
<p>As background, AV1's uptake has been widely attributed in part to its licensing profile: hyperscalers, browser vendors, and hardware makers could integrate it without negotiating patent pools. AV2 inherits that DNA, and AOMedia's early-adoption messaging is an effort to convince the ecosystem that the transition will repeat the pattern — broad support, no royalty friction.</p>
<p>For enterprise video buyers — those provisioning conferencing, streaming, archival, or high-CDN-traffic workloads — a royalty-free successor to AV1 is strategically relevant. Codec licensing has historically been one of the less visible but persistent cost lines in video deployments, and a clean AV2 path would reduce long-term exposure as AV1-capable hardware matures and AV2 support lands in client and server stacks.</p>
<h4>What to watch</h4>
<p>Three questions will determine whether AOMedia's optimism holds. First, when do production-grade encoders ship — including SVT-AV2 and hardware implementations? Second, which implementers move from evaluation to deployment, and in what products? Third, whether browser and operating-system support arrives quickly enough to give AV2 a viable playback base.</p>
<p>For now, the story is one of positioning: a finished specification, a consortium arguing for early momentum, and an encoder still under construction. The gap between those three facts is where the next six to eighteen months of AV2's trajectory will be decided.</p>

<h3>AOMedia 加速推動 AV2 早期採用，SVT-AV2 編碼器逐步成形</h3>
<p>開放媒體聯盟（Alliance for Open Media，AOMedia）發表新聞稿，強調其新一代免專利費影片編解碼器 AV2 的早期採用勢頭，距離 AV2 v1.0 規範於五月完成僅數個月。Phoronix 報導指，此舉顯示聯盟決意推動 AV2 由規範階段走向實際部署——儘管該格式一項關鍵高效能編碼器仍在積極開發之中。</p>
<p>AV2 被定位為 AV1 的接班者，延續 AOMedia 的免專利費授權模式。此模式正是該編解碼器最核心的商業賣點：與須付費授權的方案不同，AV2 設計上可實現無需按解碼器繳付專利費的部署，這一結構對大規模部署影片的機構——從串流平台到企業通訊基礎設施——的成本計算方式帶來實質改變。</p>
<p>根據 Phoronix 報導，AOMedia 的新聞稿將圍繞 AV2 的採用活動定位為早期階段。聯盟力證實現方的興趣確實存在，與此同時規範本身仍屬新鮮出爐。此區別十分重要：v1.0 規範確立了編解碼器的功能，但本身並不會帶來可出貨的編碼器、解碼器、硬件加速或內容 pipeline。能否部署取決於軟件與晶片，而非新聞稿。</p>
<h4>編碼器進展：SVT-AV2</h4>
<p>在工具方面，焦點集中在 SVT-AV2——Scalable Video Technology 家族的下一代編碼器。其前身 SVT-AV1 已成為 AV1 工作負載的參考級軟件編碼器之一，SVT-AV2 則旨在為新格式延續這一血統。</p>
<p>據原始報道，SVT-AV2 正在開發中——此狀態須嚴格理解。它尚未如今日的 SVT-AV1 般成為可出貨、可投入生產的編碼器，報道亦未確立功能對等、性能基準或發布時間表。對正在評估編解碼器路線圖的團隊而言，SVT-AV2 應歸入「觀察」而非「採用」類別。</p>
<h4>為何免專利費的定位舉足輕重</h4>
<p>作為背景，AV1 的普及被廣泛歸因於其授權模式：大型雲服務商、瀏覽器供應商及硬件製造商均可直接整合，無須與專利池談判。AV2 繼承了這套 DNA，AOMedia 的早期採用訊息正是要說服生態系統相信過渡將重現同一模式——廣泛支持，沒有專利費摩擦。</p>
<p>對企業級影片買家——即配置會議、串流、存檔或 CDN 流量龐大的工作負載者——而言，AV1 的免專利費接班者具有戰略意義。編解碼器授權一直是影片部署中較不顯眼但持續存在的成本項目之一，一條乾淨的 AV2 路徑將在支持 AV1 的硬件日趨成熟、AV2 支持進入客戶端及伺服器堆疊之際，降低長期曝險。</p>
<h4>值得留意之處</h4>
<p>三個問題將決定 AOMedia 的樂觀是否成立。第一，生產級編碼器何時出貨——包括 SVT-AV2 及硬件實現？第二，哪些實現方會由評估轉向部署，用於哪些產品？第三，瀏覽器及操作系統的支持是否來得足夠快，為 AV2 提供可行的播放基礎。</p>
<p>目前而言，這是一個定位的故事：一份已完成的規範、一個力陳早期勢頭的聯盟，以及一個仍在施工中的編碼器。這三者之間的落差，正是 AV2 未來六至十八個月走勢的決定所在。</p>
