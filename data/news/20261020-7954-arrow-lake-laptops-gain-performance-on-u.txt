<h3>Arrow Lake Laptops Gain Performance on Ubuntu 26.10, But Only Nine Months of Support</h3>
<p>Phoronix published a comparison in October covering the Intel Core Ultra 9 285H (Arrow Lake-H) on Ubuntu, and its results show Ubuntu 26.10 delivering broad, consistent performance gains over Ubuntu 26.04 LTS across dozens of benchmarks. For anyone running a Star Labs StarFighter Ultra or a comparable Arrow Lake laptop, that makes the upgrade a practical consideration rather than just another round of benchmark scores.</p>
<h4>The Test Categories Developers Will Care About</h4>
<p>The report spans dozens of benchmarks, of which the categories most relevant to development work include large-scale source compilation, compression and decompression, video transcoding, cryptographic operations, and GPU-accelerated 3D rendering. The overall trend is uniformly positive. Judging from the consistency of the results across categories, the improvements are more likely to stem from updates to the kernel, the Mesa graphics drivers, and the compiler toolchain than from chance variation in any single application.</p>
<h4>The Core Trade-Off: Long-Term Support vs. Latest Performance</h4>
<p>The real question here is not how large the benchmark wins are, but the trade-off between an LTS release and an interim one. Ubuntu 26.10 has roughly nine months of support (until around mid-2027), after which users must jump to the next interim release; Ubuntu 26.04 LTS carries five years of standard support. In other words, the performance dividend has to be paid for with a higher upgrade cadence.</p>
<p><strong>Worth upgrading if:</strong> the machine spends most of its time on sustained workloads such as compilation, transcoding, or GPU rendering — in those cases, the gains shown in the test are genuinely noticeable.</p>
<p><strong>Fine to stay on LTS if:</strong> you are doing general desktop productivity and web browsing, where the difference will mostly not be apparent.</p>
<p><strong>Hold off if:</strong> you operate a managed fleet or must comply with an LTS policy. It is hard to justify breaking a support policy to chase performance from a release with a nine-month lifespan. The upgrade path itself is straightforward — Ubuntu's standard release upgrade tool handles the in-place transition — but on any route, take a full backup first.</p>
<h4>Limitations You Should Know About</h4>
<p>This is a <strong>single-system configuration</strong> test — one Star Labs StarFighter Ultra — so its thermal and power settings may differ from your own machine, and extrapolating the results to other Arrow Lake models warrants caution. Whether both systems were re-run in the same period also directly affects how fair the comparison is; only a contemporaneous re-test makes the gap reliable, otherwise the figures should be treated as directional at best. In addition, the improvements vary considerably by workload and should be read as a directional indicator rather than a uniform multiplier. Shoppers and evaluators of newer Intel laptops should note the broader pattern: the full performance of new hardware often only materialises on a more recent distribution.</p>

<h3>Arrow Lake 手提電腦升級 Ubuntu 26.10：性能有得著，支持期僅九個月</h3>
<p>Phoronix 於 10 月刊出一份針對 Intel Core Ultra 9 285H（Arrow Lake-H）在 Ubuntu 平台上的對比測試報告，結果顯示 Ubuntu 26.10 相對 Ubuntu 26.04 LTS 在數十項基準測試中取得廣泛而一致的性能提升。對使用 Star Labs StarFighter Ultra 或同級 Arrow Lake 手提電腦的用家而言，這使升級成為實際考量，而不單純是另一輪跑分成績。</p>
<h4>開發者會關注的測試類別</h4>
<p>報告涵蓋數十項基準測試，當中對開發工作最具參考價值的類別包括：大規模原始碼編譯、壓縮與解壓、影片轉碼（transcoding）、加密運算，以及 GPU 加速的 3D 渲染。整體趨勢一致向好。從各類別測試結果的一致性來看，提升更可能源自核心（kernel）、Mesa 圖形驅動與編譯工具鏈的更新，而非任何單一應用程式的偶然波動。</p>
<h4>核心取捨：長期支持對最新性能</h4>
<p>這則新聞真正的問題不在跑分成績多寡，而在 LTS 版本與 interim 版本之間的取捨。Ubuntu 26.10 只有約九個月的支持期（至 2027 年年中前後），其後用家便須轉往下一個 interim 版本；Ubuntu 26.04 LTS 則提供五年標準支持。換言之，性能紅利須以更高的升級頻率換取。</p>
<p><strong>值得升級的情況：</strong> 電腦大部分時間用於編譯、轉碼、GPU 渲染等持續性 workload——在這些情況下，測試所示的提升確實明顯可感。</p>
<p><strong>可留在 LTS 的情況：</strong> 從事一般桌面文書處理及網頁瀏覽，差距多數不會顯現。</p>
<p><strong>應暫緩升級的情況：</strong> 管理機隊或須遵守 LTS 政策的環境。為追求一個壽命僅九個月的 release 的性能而中斷支持政策，難以合理化。升級途徑本身簡單——Ubuntu 標準的 release upgrade 工具可處理就地升級——但無論採用哪一條路徑，執行前都應先作完整備份。</p>
<h4>必須留意的限制</h4>
<p>今次屬<strong>單一系統配置</strong>的測試——僅一部 Star Labs StarFighter Ultra——因此其散熱與供電設定可能與閣下電腦不同，將結果推廣至其他 Arrow Lake 機型須審慎。兩套系統是否在同一時期重新測試，亦直接影響對比是否公平；只有同期重測才可使差距可靠，否則相關數字最多只宜作方向性參考。此外，各項 workload 的提升幅度差異頗大，只應視為方向性指標，而非統一的倍數。選購或評估較新 Intel 手提電腦的人士應留意這個更廣泛的模式：新硬件的完整性能，往往要等待較新的發行版才得以充分發揮。</p>
