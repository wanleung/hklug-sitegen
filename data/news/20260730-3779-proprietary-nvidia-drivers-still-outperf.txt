Title: Proprietary NVIDIA Drivers Still Outperform Open-Source Stack on RTX 5090 Linux Laptop
Content:
<p>A performance benchmark on a high-end Linux laptop underscores the persistent gap between NVIDIA's proprietary drivers and the open-source Nouveau+NVK stack. Testing by Phoronix on a Razer Blade 18, the first such model Razer has certified for Ubuntu Linux, reveals that the open-source drivers still trail significantly on this flagship GeForce RTX 5090 Laptop GPU.</p>
<p>The evaluation compared the official NVIDIA R610 proprietary driver stack against the upstream Nouveau+NVK drivers, running under the CachyOS distribution. While the bulk of the testing used the proprietary stack, additional graphics benchmarks were run on the open-source drivers before the review unit was returned.</p>
<p>Across the suite of tests, the proprietary drivers delivered substantially higher frame rates and smoother operation. This performance delta means longer iteration times for developers engaged in GPU-accelerated tasks like rendering or machine learning, where direct hardware utilization is critical.</p>
<p>The choice, however, isn't solely about raw speed. The open-source stack offers integration benefits: it avoids proprietary kernel modules, simplifies distribution packaging, and eases kernel upgrades. For developers using their machine as a daily driver for coding and general productivity, these advantages in maintainability and ecosystem harmony can be decisive. The proprietary drivers remain the pragmatic choice for workloads demanding peak GPU performance or full feature parity, such as advanced ray tracing or specific CUDA workloads.</p>
<p>Razer's official certification of the Blade 18 for Linux marks a notable shift, signaling growing vendor confidence that premium hardware can meet professional users' expectations. This trend, alongside hardware like the Steam Deck, continues to apply pressure for robust open-source GPU drivers.</p>
<p>For developers weighing their options, the benchmarks provide a clear framework. Workloads centered on compilation, containerized services, or lighter creative tasks may find Nouveau+NVK "good enough," offering simpler long-term maintenance. Conversely, environments heavily reliant on GPU compute or real-time graphics will likely require the proprietary driver stack for the foreseeable future. Dual-boot or driver-switching setups can offer a flexible evaluation path.</p>
<p>While the open-source community steadily works to close the gap, the Phoronix data confirms it remains material on current-generation flagship mobile GPUs. Developers planning hardware purchases or driver strategies would do well to consult these measured results against their specific performance and maintenance priorities, rather than relying on vendor assurances. As more manufacturers embrace Linux certification, ongoing independent benchmarking will remain vital for the community.</p>
<hr>
<p>一項針對高端 Linux 筆記本電腦的性能基準測試，突顯了 NVIDIA 專有驅動程式與開源 Nouveau+NVK 方案之間持續存在的差距。Phoronix 在 Razer Blade 18（首款獲得 Ubuntu Linux 認證的 Razer 機型）上進行的測試顯示，對於這款旗艦級 GeForce RTX 5090 筆記本電腦 GPU，開源驅動程式的性能仍大幅落後。</p>
<p>該評估比較了官方 NVIDIA R610 專有驅動程式方案與上游 Nouveau+NVK 驅動程式，後者在 CachyOS 發行版上運行。雖然大部分測試使用專有方案，但在歸還測試機器前，也在開源驅動程式上運行了額外的圖形基準測試。</p>
<p>在整套測試中，專有驅動程式提供了明顯更高的幀率和更流暢的操作。這種性能差距意味著，對於從事渲染或機器學習等 GPU 加速任務的開發者而言，其迭代時間將會更長，因為這些任務直接依賴硬件性能。</p>
<p>然而，選擇並非僅關乎原始速度。開源方案提供了整合優勢：它避免了專有的核心模組，簡化了發行版打包，並簡化了核心升級。對於將機器用於日常編程和一般生產力的開發者來說，這些在可維護性和生態系統整合性方面的優勢可能是決定性的。而對於需要峰值 GPU 性能或完整功能對等的工作負載（例如進階光線追蹤或特定的 CUDA 工作負載），專有驅動程式仍是務實的選擇。</p>
<p>Razer 官方對 Blade 18 進行 Linux 認證是一個顯著轉變，表明硬件供應商日益相信高端硬件能夠滿足專業用戶的期望。這一趨勢，加上像 Steam Deck 這樣的硬件，持續對堅實的開源 GPU 驅動程式施加壓力。</p>
<p>對於權衡選項的開發者，基準測試提供了一個清晰的框架。以編譯、容器化服務或較輕量的創意任務為中心的工作負載，可能會發現 Nouveau+NVK「足夠好」，並提供更簡單的長期維護。相反，在可預見的未來，嚴重依賴 GPU 計算或即時圖形的環境，很可能仍需採用專有驅動程式方案。雙重開機或驅動程式切換設定則可提供靈活的評估途徑。</p>
<p>雖然開源社群正持續努力縮小差距，但 Phoronix 的數據證實，在當代旗艦級移動 GPU 上，這種差距仍然顯著。計劃購置硬件或制定驅動程式策略的開發者，最好參考這些實測結果，並結合其特定的性能與維護優先級來考量，而非僅依賴硬件廠商的宣稱。隨著更多製造商擁抱 Linux 認證，持續的獨立基準測試對社群而言仍將至關重要。</p>
