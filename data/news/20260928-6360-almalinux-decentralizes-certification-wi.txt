Title: AlmaLinux Decentralizes Certification with New Self-Service Portal and App
Content:
<p>AlmaLinux has launched a new public certification portal and a self-service application, decentralizing its compatibility validation program to empower hardware and software vendors to initiate their own certification.</p>
<p>The core of the initiative is a downloadable app that allows vendors to run a standardized test suite locally on an AlmaLinux system. It generates a compatibility report, which can then be submitted for listing on AlmaLinux's official public portal. This marks a significant expansion from the project's original hardware-focused certification program, which began two years under its Certification Special Interest Group (SIG), to now include third-party software.</p>
<p>This model presents a stark contrast to more centralized certification programs like Red Hat's Hardware Compatibility List (HCL). Instead of vendors relying entirely on a central distributor's approval process, AlmaLinux is providing the tooling for the initial validation step. The approach is designed to significantly lower the barriers to participation, particularly for small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) and independent software vendors (ISVs) that may lack the resources for expensive, formal certification engagements.</p>
<p>For Hong Kong-based vendors, this creates a pragmatic, no-cost path to demonstrate platform support proactively. The self-service app acts as an initial validator, helping ensure a baseline of quality before a product is publicly listed. The resulting portal serves as a central repository for system administrators to find pre-validated combinations, reducing deployment risks.</p>
<p>By democratizing the certification workflow, AlmaLinux aims to foster a more collaborative ecosystem. Wider vendor participation is expected to build a more comprehensive catalog, which in turn strengthens AlmaLinux's position as a dependable, enterprise-grade distribution built through community-driven processes.</p>
<hr>
<p>AlmaLinux 推出了一個新的公開認證入口網站及自助服務應用程式，將其兼容性驗證計劃去中心化，賦予硬件及軟件供應商自主啟動認證的能力。</p>
<p>該計劃的核心是一個可下載的應用程式，允許供應商在 AlmaLinux 系統上本地運行一套標準化測試組合。它會生成一份兼容性報告，隨後可提交至 AlmaLinux 的官方公開入口網站進行登記。這標誌著該項目原始的硬件認證計劃（始於兩年前的認證特別興趣小組）的重大擴展，如今已涵蓋第三方軟件。</p>
<p>此模式與更為中心化的認證計劃（如 Red Hat 的硬件兼容性列表）形成鮮明對比。 AlmaLinux 無需供應商完全依賴中央發行商的審批流程，而是提供工具來進行初步驗證步驟。這種方法旨在顯著降低參與門檻，特別是對於資源有限、難以負擔昂貴正式認證流程的中小型企業及獨立軟件供應商。</p>
<p>對於香港的供應商而言，這提供了一條務實、無成本的途徑，以主動展示其平台支持能力。該自助服務應用程式充當初步驗證工具，有助於確保產品在公開登記前達到基本的質量標準。隨後形成的入口網站成為系統管理員查找經預先驗證組合的中央資料庫，從而降低部署風險。</p>
<p>通過普及認證工作流程，AlmaLinux 致力於促進一個更具協作性的生態系統。更廣泛的供應商參與預期將建立一個更全面的目錄，進而鞏固 AlmaLinux 作為一個由社區驅動流程構建、可靠且達至企業級標準的發行版的地位。</p>
