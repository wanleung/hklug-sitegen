<h3>Always On VPN Outage From the September Windows 11 Patches Finally Has a Fix</h3>
<p>Administrators who put Always On VPN remediation tickets on hold can close them: Microsoft has shipped a fix for the remote-access failures that began hitting Windows 11 clients as soon as the September 2026 security updates went out, BleepingComputer reports.</p>
<h4>What happened</h4>
<p>The breakage was simple and severe — Always On VPN stopped connecting on Windows 11 machines after the September 2026 security updates were installed, and the update release itself was the trigger. That puts the fault squarely in the monthly patch cycle rather than in any local configuration, which is why it escalated from a nuisance to a standing incident for enterprises that depend on Always On VPN for remote access.</p>
<p>The confirmed picture rests on four points: the symptom is Always On VPN connections failing after the September 2026 Windows 11 security updates; the cause is that update release; Microsoft has issued a fix; and the affected scope is Windows 11 clients running Always On VPN. The specific KB references and build numbers are set out in Microsoft's release notes.</p>
<p>Several operational questions remain open until those release notes are read in full: whether the fix arrives through the normal Windows Update channel or as an out-of-band release, whether affected machines must reinstall the September updates before connectivity is restored, and whether Windows Server and Windows 10 are in scope at all. Teams should treat any procedural detail beyond the four confirmed points as unconfirmed until it is cross-checked against Microsoft's own documentation.</p>
<h4>The rollback is the real work</h4>
<p>For most organisations, the fix does not end the incident — it starts the reconciliation.</p>
<p><strong>Confirm the fix is present.</strong> Validate against the fix release Microsoft has published, and test a live Always On VPN connection from a representative client rather than trusting update history.</p>
<p><strong>Rebuild the deferred-update backlog.</strong> Teams that uninstalled or deferred the September updates to keep remote access alive have been running machines without the September security fixes for weeks. Plan a controlled reinstall of those updates now, and re-test VPN connectivity immediately afterwards.</p>
<p><strong>Re-test through a pilot group.</strong> Always On VPN failures tend to emerge at scale rather than on a single test machine. Confirm both initial connection and reconnection behaviour on a pilot ring before widening deployment.</p>
<p><strong>Retire overlapping workarounds.</strong> Configuration changes, profile tweaks, and firewall rules introduced to keep the VPN alive during the outage should be identified and removed as the fix lands, so the production configuration reflects reality instead of a temporary patch.</p>
<h4>Why it matters</h4>
<p>Always On VPN is the Microsoft-recommended successor to the legacy DirectAccess remote-access setup, and it remains a core dependency for Windows-first enterprises that need device-level connectivity outside the office. When a routine monthly update silently breaks it, administrators face a direct conflict: patch for security, or preserve connectivity. That tension is why this class of bug draws outsized attention in enterprise operations channels relative to its technical complexity.</p>
<p>For Hong Kong IT teams running hybrid workloads and remote-access infrastructure on Windows 11, the takeaway is practical rather than theoretical. The stalemate between patching and remote access has been broken, and the deferred-update backlog now needs a remediation plan with a deadline — not an indefinite hold.</p>

<h3>九月 Windows 11 更新導致 Always On VPN 中斷 終於推出修復</h3>
<p>將 Always On VPN 修補工單暫時擱置的管理員現在可以關閉它們了：據 BleepingComputer 報道，微軟已發布修復程式，解決自 2026 年 9 月安全更新推出當日起便開始影響 Windows 11 用戶端的遠端存取故障問題。</p>
<h4>事件經過</h4>
<p>故障情況簡單卻嚴重：2026 年 9 月安全更新安裝後，Windows 11 電腦上的 Always On VPN 無法連線，而更新發布本身正是觸發原因。這將責任明確歸於每月例行的 patch cycle，而非任何本機配置問題，亦是事件由滋擾升級為企業持續性事故的原因——這些企業依賴 Always On VPN 進行遠端存取。</p>
<p>已確認的四點事實如下：症狀為 2026 年 9 月 Windows 11 安裝安全更新後 Always On VPN 連線失敗；成因為該次更新發布；微軟已發布修復程式；受影響範圍為執行 Always On VPN 的 Windows 11 用戶端。具體的 KB 編號及 build number 詳列於微軟的 release notes。</p>
<p>在完整閱讀 release notes 之前，若干操作問題仍有待釐清：修復程式是透過正常 Windows Update 渠道發布還是以 out-of-band 方式推出、受影響電腦是否須重新安裝 9 月更新才能恢復連線，以及 Windows Server 與 Windows 10 是否在受影響範圍之內。除上述四點已確認事項外，團隊應視任何其他程序細節為未經證實，直至與微軟官方文件交叉核對為止。</p>
<h4>回復工作才是真正的挑戰</h4>
<p>對大多數機構而言，修復程式並非事件的終點——而是數據核對工作的開始。</p>
<p><strong>確認修復程式已安裝。</strong>對照微軟已公布的修復版本進行驗證，並從具代表性的用戶端實測一次 Always On VPN 連線，而非僅依賴更新記錄。</p>
<p><strong>重建延後更新的積壓清單。</strong>為維持遠端存取運作而卸載或延後安裝 9 月更新的團隊，其電腦數週以來一直缺少 9 月的安全修正。現在應計劃受控地重新安裝這些更新，隨後立即重新測試 VPN 連線。</p>
<p><strong>透過試點群組重新測試。</strong>Always On VPN 故障往往在大規模部署時才浮現，而非在單一測試電腦上出現。在擴大部署前，應先於 pilot ring 確認初始連線及重新連線的表現。</p>
<p><strong>撤除重疊的臨時對策。</strong>為在中斷期間維持 VPN 運作而引入的配置更改、profile 調整及防火牆規則，應在修復程式落地時加以識別並移除，令 production 配置反映實際狀況，而非臨時的權宜措施。</p>
<h4>問題為何重要</h4>
<p>Always On VPN 是微軟推薦取代舊有 DirectAccess 遠端存取方案的產品，對於需要在辦公室外維持裝置層級連線的 Windows 為本企業而言，仍是核心依賴。當例行每月更新悄然令其失效，管理員便面臨直接矛盾：是為了安全而打 patch，還是維持連線？正因這種張力，此類 bug 在企業營運渠道所獲得的關注程度，遠超其技術複雜度本身。</p>
<p>對於在香港以 Windows 11 營運混合工作負載及遠端存取基礎設施的 IT 圢隊而言，啟示是務實而非理論性的。打 patch 與遠端存取之間的僵局已被打破，延後更新的積壓清單現在需要一份設有期限的補救計劃——而非無限期擱置。</p>
