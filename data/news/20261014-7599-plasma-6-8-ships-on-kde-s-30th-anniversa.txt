Title: Plasma 6.8 Ships on KDE's 30th Anniversary, Three Decades After Ettrich's Founding Email
Content:
<p>The KDE project has released version 6.8 of its Plasma desktop, and it has deliberately paired the launch with a piece of its own history: the 30th anniversary of the 1996 email in which Matthias Ettrich first proposed what would become KDE.</p>
<p>According to LWN.net's report on the release, the timing was intentional — the announcement of Plasma 6.8 coincides with the milestone marking Ettrich's original call for a coherent, visually consistent free desktop for Unix systems at a time when Linux was growing rapidly.</p>
<p>Ettrich's founding message, preserved in the project's historical archive, remains strikingly blunt about the gap he set out to fill: "Unix popularity grows thanks to the free variants, mostly Linux. But still a consistent, nice looking free desktop-" is how the appeal opens, arguing that free operating systems deserved software with the polish and integration of their commercial rivals.</p>
<p>Three decades on, that argument has largely been won. Plasma ships as an official desktop environment in major Linux distributions, underpins several commercial workstation offerings, and forms the core of a wider ecosystem that includes the KDE Frameworks libraries, the KWin compositor, and the KDE Applications suite.</p>
<p>Plasma 6.8 is the latest in the 6.x line, the series that began with Wayland as the default display server rather than the legacy X11 stack. The new release brings a set of practical changes for desktop users: accessibility improvements across the shell, the inclusion of the Kup backup tool, and an upgrade to the Spectacle screen-recording tool that now supports both system audio and microphone input capture. Most notably, Plasma 6.8 drops the X11 session — a decision KDE announced in November 2025 — meaning X11 applications remain usable through XWayland, but the Plasma desktop itself now runs exclusively on Wayland. KDE's official release announcement carries the full changelog, and LWN.net's item enumerates these highlights alongside its anniversary coverage.</p>
<p>Distributions will pick up Plasma 6.8 on their own cadence, with rolling-release offerings typically landing first and fixed-release distributions following according to vendor backporting policy.</p>
<p>The anniversary framing is worth pausing on for the wider open-source community. KDE is one of the few free-software projects of its scale that can point to an unbroken thirty-year lineage — an original vision document, a continuous series of desktop releases, and a governance and contributor culture that survived the turbulent early years of the Linux desktop wars. That longevity is increasingly rare in a field where projects fork, stall, or are superseded within a decade.</p>
<p>Whether Plasma 6.8 itself marks a generational leap or a steady incremental step, KDE's decision to launch it alongside the anniversary is a reminder of how much of today's free desktop still traces back to one programmer's frustration in 1996 — and a community's thirty-year answer to it.</p>
<p>The full release notes are available directly on <a href="https://kde.org/announcements/plasma/6/6.8.0/">KDE's announcement page</a>, as well as via LWN.net's coverage of the release.</p>
<hr>
<p>KDE 專案已正式發布 Plasma 桌面 6.8 版本，並刻意將此次發布與專案自身的一段歷史結合：即 1996 年 Matthias Ettrich 首次提出日後成為 KDE 的構想之郵件發表三十周年。</p>
<p>根據 LWN.net 就此次發布的報道，時機的安排並非偶然——Plasma 6.8 的公告與 Ettrich 當年呼籲建立一個連貫、視覺上統一的自由 Unix 系統桌面之里程碑正好重合，當時 Linux 正迅速發展。</p>
<p>Ettrich 那封保存於專案歷史檔案中的創辦郵件，對他當初決意填補的空白依然直言不諱：「Unix 的普及有賴自由版本，主要是 Linux。但一個連貫、美觀的自由桌面——」正是這樣開篇，論證自由操作系統理應享有與商業對手同等水平的軟件完成度及整合度。</p>
<p>三十年過去，這番論點已大體上獲得勝利。如今 Plasma 以官方桌面環境之姿出現在各大 Linux 發行版之中，支撐多款商用工作站產品，並構成一個更廣泛生態系統的核心，當中包括 KDE Frameworks 函數庫、KWin 合成器（compositor）以及 KDE Applications 套件。</p>
<p>Plasma 6.8 是 6.x 系列的最新版本；該系列自開始起即以 Wayland 作為預設顯示伺服器，取代舊有的 X11 技術堆疊。此次新版本為桌面用戶帶來一系列實用改動：shell 整體的無障礙功能改進、備份工具 Kup 的納入，以及螢幕錄影工具 Spectacle 的升級——現已同時支援擷取系統音頻及麥克風輸入。最值得注意的是，Plasma 6.8 移除了 X11 會話（session）——此決定 KDE 已於 2025 年 11 月公布——意味著 X11 應用程式仍可透過 XWayland 使用，但 Plasma 桌面本身現已完全運行於 Wayland 之上。KDE 官方發布公告載有完整的更新日誌（changelog），而 LWN.net 的報道則在周年紀念報道之餘，逐一列舉了這些重點。</p>
<p>整體而言，各發行版會按自身節奏納入 Plasma 6.8；一般而言，rolling-release 發行版通常會率先跟進，固定版本發行版則會隨後視乎軟件供應商的 backport 政策推出更新。</p>
<p>這次以周年紀念為框架的處理，值得整個開源社群細味。KDE 是少數具此規模的自由軟件專案，能指向一段從未中斷的三十年血脈——一份最初的願景文件、一系列從未間斷的桌面版本發布，以及一套熬過 Linux 桌面爭霸戰動盪早期的治理與貢獻者文化。在一個專案動輒在十年之內分叉、停滯或被取代的領域裡，如此長壽已愈來愈罕見。</p>
<p>不論 Plasma 6.8 本身代表的是一次跨代飛躍，還是穩步的漸進改良，KDE 選擇與周年紀念同步發布，提醒了我們：今日自由桌面的種種，很大程度上仍可追溯至 1996 年一位程序員的不滿——以及一個社群對此作出的三十年回應。</p>
<p>完整的發布說明可直接於 <a href="https://kde.org/announcements/plasma/6/6.8.0/">KDE 官方公告頁面</a> 查閱，亦可透過 LWN.net 對此次發布的報道取得。</p>
