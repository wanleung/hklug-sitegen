Title: AlmaLinux Introduces Self-Certification App, Lowering Barriers for Ecosystem Validation
Content:
<p>The AlmaLinux project has taken a significant step toward building a self-sustaining enterprise ecosystem with the launch of a new certification portal and a self-service application that lets vendors validate their own hardware and software directly on the platform.</p>
<p>The "AlmaLinux Certify" app is the centerpiece of the expanded program. Vendors and users install the application to run automated compatibility tests on systems running AlmaLinux, replacing a process that traditionally required formal engagements with a central certifying body — a barrier that often priced out smaller players.</p>
<p>The release broadens an existing effort. Two years ago, AlmaLinux's Certification Special Interest Group (SIG) introduced a hardware certification program. That program has now been expanded to cover third-party software as well, and all certified products are listed in a new public directory that serves as a discoverable resource for users evaluating compatible hardware and software combinations.</p>
<p>The approach marks a deliberate philosophical break from the certification model used by Red Hat, whose partner-driven, centrally managed program is widely trusted but operates within a curated framework that limits who can participate and at what cost. AlmaLinux's self-service model flips that dynamic, opening validation to any vendor willing to run the tests.</p>
<p>For small and medium-sized enterprises and independent developers — particularly in cost-conscious markets like Hong Kong — the practical impact is significant. Gaining formal compatibility validation for an enterprise-grade Linux platform no longer requires navigating a lengthy, expensive pipeline. Vendors can demonstrate reliability on AlmaLinux quickly and affordably, which is especially valuable for those looking to enter the enterprise market without committing resources to complex certification programs.</p>
<p>The move also signals something broader about AlmaLinux's trajectory. What began as a community-driven rebuild of Red Hat Enterprise Linux is now investing in the infrastructure needed to function as an independent enterprise platform. By providing open tools for validation and pairing them with a public directory that doubles as a discovery mechanism, AlmaLinux is building the collaborative foundation on which a diverse hardware and software ecosystem can grow — one where vendors and users, not a central authority, drive the pace of adoption.</p>
<hr>
<p>AlmaLinux 計劃在建立可持續發展的企業生態系統方面邁出了重要一步，推出了新的認證入口網站及自助應用程式，讓供應商能直接在平台上驗證自家硬件與軟件。</p>
<p>「AlmaLinux Certify」應用程式是擴展計劃的核心。供應商和用戶安裝該應用程式後，可在運行 AlmaLinux 的系統上執行自動化兼容性測試，取代了傳統上需與中央認證機構進行正式接洽的流程——此門檻常將規模較小的參與者排除在外。</p>
<p>此次發佈擴展了既有工作。兩年前，AlmaLinux 認證特別興趣小組（SIG）推出了硬件認證計劃，如今該計劃已擴展至涵蓋第三方軟件。所有已認證產品均收錄於新的公開目錄中，方便用戶查找並評估硬件與軟件的兼容組合。</p>
<p>此舉有意識地偏離了 Red Hat 所採用的認證模式。Red Hat 基於合作夥伴、中央管理的計劃廣受信賴，但其嚴格的管控框架限制了參與者範圍及准入成本。AlmaLinux 的自助模式則顛覆了這一動態，向任何願意執行測試的供應商開放驗證流程。</p>
<p>對於中小企業和獨立開發者——尤其在香港等注重成本的市場——這帶來了實質影響。獲得企業級 Linux 平台的正式兼容性認證，不再需要經歷冗長且昂貴的流程。供應商能快速且低成本地展示其在 AlmaLinux 上的可靠性，這對希望進軍企業市場卻不願投入複雜認證計劃的企業尤具價值。</p>
<p>此舉亦反映 AlmaLinux 發展路線的更深層意義。這個起源於社區驅動、重建 Red Hat Enterprise Linux 的項目，現正投資於基礎設施，以獨立企業平台之姿運作。透過提供開源驗證工具，並搭配兼具發現機制的公開目錄，AlmaLinux 正在構建協作基礎，讓多元化的硬件與軟件生態得以成長——在這個生態中，驅動採用節奏的不是中央權威，而是供應商與用戶。</p>
