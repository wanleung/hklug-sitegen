Title: Flipper Devices Crowdfunds $399 Flipper One, a Linux Multi-Tool Succeeding the Flipper Zero
Content:
<p>Flipper Devices has opened a crowdfunding campaign for the Flipper One, a portable Linux-based computer and networking multi-tool priced at US$399, BleepingComputer reported. The new device is positioned as the successor to the hardware that made the company a familiar name in the infosec community — the Flipper Zero, a pocket-sized wireless testing tool with a large following among researchers, hobbyists and penetration testers.</p>
<p>The headline change is under the hood: where the Flipper Zero shipped with a locked-down, purpose-built firmware environment, the Flipper One runs a general-purpose Linux operating system. For anyone who has wanted to treat the device less like a single-purpose gadget and more like a small, portable workstation for network and radio security work, that shift from closed firmware to an open operating system is the most consequential part of the announcement. A Linux base means users can expect a familiar toolchain, the ability to install standard packages, and the flexibility to adapt the device to tasks the manufacturer has not anticipated — subject, of course, to what the shipped software image and hardware actually permit.</p>
<p><strong>Crowdfunding caveat</strong></p>
<p>It is worth stressing what the campaign is: a pledge, not a retail transaction. The US$399 figure, the technical specifications, the software image and the delivery schedule are all subject to change until units ship. Detailed hardware specifications have not yet been published, which is itself a reason to verify before backing. Prospective backers in Hong Kong should independently verify shipping options, regional availability, import duties and any radio-regulatory constraints on the device before committing funds. Flipper-class devices have historically been subject to different treatment across markets depending on their radio capabilities, so confirm that the hardware and its wireless functions are permissible for their intended use where you live and work. Crowdfunding launches in this category have a mixed record on delivery dates and final specifications, and the gap between announced capability and shipped capability is where expectations are most often disappointed.</p>
<p><strong>Why it matters</strong></p>
<p>The Flipper Zero carved out a niche precisely because it bundled many radio and interface-testing functions into an inexpensive, approachable package. Its popularity also drew scrutiny: the device became shorthand in some circles for cheap, off-the-shelf hardware that could be repurposed for questionable ends, a dual-use tension that has followed the product line throughout its life. The Flipper One's move to a general-purpose OS arguably sharpens that tension, since a Linux multi-tool is inherently more general — and more capable — than a fixed-function device. For defenders, the same properties that make it useful for auditing one's own systems, prototyping radio experiments or teaching security fundamentals are what make it a tool worth understanding regardless of one's position on its misuse.</p>
<p>For the open-source and hardware community, the practical questions come down to execution: how open the bootloader and kernel stack really are, what documentation ships with the device, and whether the crowdfunding timeline holds. For Hong Kong's IT and open-source audience, the announcement lands in a familiar space — affordable, general-purpose security hardware that blurs the line between learning tool and professional instrument. How it is received will depend less on the campaign video than on the documentation, the openness of the platform, and the fine print on availability and compliance.</p>
<hr>
<p>據 BleepingComputer 報道，Flipper Devices 已為 Flipper One 開啟眾籌項目，這是一部售價 399 美元、以 Linux 為基礎的流動電腦及網絡多功能工具。新設備定位為該公司在信息安全（infosec）社群中聞名的硬件產品——Flipper Zero 的後繼機型；Flipper Zero 是一款口袋大小的無線測試工具，在研究人員、業餘愛好者及滲透測試人員之間擁有大量擁躉。</p>
<p>最主要的改動在於核心：Flipper Zero 出廠時搭載的是封閉、專為特定用途而設的 firmware 環境，而 Flipper One 則運行通用 Linux 操作系統。對於一直希望把該設備視作一部小型流動工作站、用於網絡及無線電保安工作，而非單一用途裝置的人來說，這次由封閉 firmware 轉向開放操作系統的變更，是整項公佈中影響最深遠的部分。以 Linux 為基礎，意味用戶可使用熟悉的 toolchain、安裝標準軟件套件，並有靈活度把設備改裝作廠商未曾預見的用途——當然，一切仍取決於出廠軟件鏡像及硬件實際容許的範圍。</p>
<p><strong>眾籌注意事項</strong></p>
<p>有必要強調該項目的性質：這是一項 pledge，而非零售交易。399 美元的價錢、技術規格、軟件鏡像及交付時間表，在產品正式發貨前均可能更改。詳細硬件規格尚未公布，這本身已是支持前應加以查證的理由。香港的潛在支持者應在投入款項前，自行核實運輸選項、地區發售情況、進口關稅，以及設備在無線電法規方面的任何限制。Flipper 類設備過去在不同市場因無線電功能差異而受到不同對待，因此須確認該硬件及其無線功能在你居住和工作的地方，就預期用途而言是否合法合規。此類別的眾籌項目在交付日期及最終規格方面往績好壞參半，而公佈規格與實際出貨規格之間的差距，正是期望最常落空之處。</p>
<p><strong>為何重要</strong></p>
<p>Flipper Zero 之所以開創獨特市場定位，正是因為它把眾多無線電及介面測試功能整合於一個價格相宜、門檻不高的套裝之中。其受歡迎程度亦引來審視：在某些圈子裏，該設備已成為廉價現成硬件可被改裝作可疑用途的代名詞，這種 dual-use 張力一直伴隨該產品線整個生命周期。Flipper One 轉用通用操作系統，某程度上令這種張力更為尖銳，因為 Linux 多功能工具本質上比固定功能設備更為通用、功能更強。對於防禦方而言，那些令它適合用於審視自身系統、進行無線電實驗原型設計或教授保安基礎知識的特性，同樣令它成為一款無論你對其濫用問題持何種立場都值得了解的工具。</p>
<p>對開源及硬件社群而言，實際問題歸結為執行層面：bootloader 及 kernel stack 究竟有多開放、設備隨附什麼文件，以及眾籌時間表能否兌現。對香港的 IT 及開源受眾而言，這項公佈落在一個熟悉的領域——價格相宜、用途廣泛的保安硬件，模糊了學習工具與專業儀器之間的界線。它會否受歡迎，與其說取決於眾籌宣傳片，不如說取決於文件、平台的開放程度，以及有關發售及合規的細則條款。</p>
