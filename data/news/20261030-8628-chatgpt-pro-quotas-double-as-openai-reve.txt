Title: ChatGPT Pro Quotas Double as OpenAI Reverses Planned Usage Cut
Content:
<p>OpenAI has abandoned a planned 50% reduction in usage limits across its paid ChatGPT tiers and instead doubled the allowances on ChatGPT Pro, according to BleepingComputer. The reversal covers the $100, $200 and $500 ChatGPT plans as well as Business Premium, with the company also committing to keeping usage of "Dots" on a meter separate from the core conversation quota.</p>
<p>The practical effect is that no subscriber will see a reduction under the revised plan — and high-volume users of the top tier will have roughly twice the headroom they had before the episode began. For teams that treat a ChatGPT subscription as a fixed line item in a coding or support budget, that is the number that matters: the flat monthly price stays constant, while the volume of work it buys moves in both directions at the vendor's discretion.</p>
<h4>From cut to restoration to doubling</h4>
<p>The episode unfolded in three steps. OpenAI first signalled that it would halve usage allowances on its paid plans — a change that would have effectively raised the per-task cost of every workflow built on the service without touching the sticker price. After that proposal drew objections, the company restored the existing limits. It then went further, raising the ChatGPT Pro ceiling to roughly double its previous level.</p>
<p>For developers and enterprise buyers, the sequence is the story as much as the final numbers. A tier that could be halved and then doubled inside a short window is, by demonstrated behaviour, a tunable parameter rather than a fixed entitlement. Procurement discussions should therefore focus less on today's quota and more on how limits are measured, what happens at the ceiling — throttling, hard stop, or metered overage — and whether the provider commits to notice periods before changes take effect.</p>
<h4>Dots billed on a separate meter</h4>
<p>One detail with direct budgeting consequences: OpenAI says usage of Dots will be tracked independently rather than drawn from the main conversation allowance. In practice, that prevents a single feature from silently consuming the quota a team relies on for day-to-day model work, which makes month-end spend forecasting more dependable. Teams that had modelled Dots usage against their existing conversation limits should revisit those assumptions — the arithmetic now works in their favour.</p>
<p>The pricing question also sits against a backdrop of alternatives. Developers weighing a flat-rate subscription against per-token API billing can compare the plan against metered access to OpenAI's own APIs and against competing coding assistants more generally. The break-even point depends heavily on how close a given team's usage sits to its quota: a plan that is generous at 60% utilisation can become poor value at the ceiling, and unlike an API bill, the overage behaviour is a policy decision rather than a predictable per-token cost.</p>
<h4>A general concern for AI-coding budgets</h4>
<p>The story is broadly relevant to IT professionals in Hong Kong and elsewhere who are building AI-assisted coding and support workflows on subscription tools: it is a reminder that flat-priced AI services carry discretionary usage caps, and that those caps belong in risk registers and fallback planning alongside vendor lock-in and model-availability concerns.</p>
<p>OpenAI has not said whether the expanded quotas are permanent or how long they will hold through future model releases and capacity planning cycles. That unanswered question is the one worth monitoring — and the most likely trigger for the next revision.</p>
<hr>
<p>據 BleepingComputer 報導，OpenAI 已放棄原定將各項付費 ChatGPT 方案用量上限削減 50% 的計劃，反而將 ChatGPT Pro 的配額提高一倍。是次逆轉涵蓋 100 美元、200 美元及 500 美元的 ChatGPT 方案，以及 Business Premium；公司同時承諾將「Dots」的用量獨立計量，不佔用核心對話配額。</p>
<p>實際效果是，修訂後的計劃下不會有任何訂戶遭到削減——而最高層級方案的重度用戶，其可用空間約為事件發生前的兩倍。對於將 ChatGPT 訂閱視為編碼或支援預算中固定開支項目的團隊而言，這才是關鍵：每月固定收費不變，但換取的工作量多寡則由供應商自行決定。</p>
<h4>由削減、恢復到倍增</h4>
<p>是次事件分三個階段演變。OpenAI 最初表示會將付費方案的用量配額削減一半——此舉實際上會令所有基於該服務建立的工作流程每項任務成本上升，而標價不變。該建議惹來反對後，公司恢復原有上限。其後更進一步，將 ChatGPT Pro 的上限提升至約為先前水平的兩倍。</p>
<p>對開發者及企業買家而言，整個時序本身就跟最終數字一樣重要。一個可在短時間內先減半再倍增的方案層級，以其實際表現而言，屬於可調校的參數，而非固定的權益。因此，採購談判應少著眼於今日的配額，多關注用量上限如何計算、達到上限時會如何處理——是限流（throttling）、硬性停止，還是按量計費的超額使用——以及供應商是否承諾在變更生效前提供通知期。</p>
<h4>Dots 以獨立計量收費</h4>
<p>一個直接影響預算編列的細節：OpenAI 表示 Dots 的用量將獨立追蹤，不會從主要對話配額中扣減。實際上，這可防止單一功能暗中消耗團隊日常工作模型任務所依賴的配額，令月底支出預測更為可靠。曾經以現有對話上限來估算 Dots 用量的團隊，應重新檢視相關假設——如今運算結果對其有利。</p>
<p>定價問題亦存在其他替代方案作對比。開發者在固定月費訂閱與按 token 計費的 API 之間取捨時，可將該方案與 OpenAI 自家 API 的計量存取方式作比較，亦可更廣泛地比較其他編程助理。收支平衡點很大程度取決於特定團隊的用量與配額的接近程度：在 60% 使用率下寬裕的方案，到了上限可能變得不划算；而且與 API 帳單不同，超額使用時的處理方式屬政策決定，而非可預測的每 token 成本。</p>
<h4>對 AI 編碼預算的普遍憂慮</h4>
<p>是次事件對香港及其他地區的 IT 專業人士廣泛具參考價值，尤其是正在以訂閱式工具建立 AI 輔助編碼及支援工作流程者：它提醒人們，固定價格的 AI 服務附帶由供應商自行決定的用量上限，而這些上限應與供應商鎖定（vendor lock-in）及模型可用性等風險一併納入風險登記冊及後備計劃之中。</p>
<p>OpenAI 未有說明擴大的配額是否屬永久安排，亦未交代在未來模型發布及容量規劃周期中這些配額可維持多久。這個懸而未決的問題正是值得持續監察之處——也最可能成為下一次修訂的觸發點。</p>
