Title: Yunsilicon Submits First Mainline Linux Network Driver Patches
Content:
<p>Chinese semiconductor startup Yunsilicon has begun posting patches to the Linux kernel mailing list (LKML), marking its first attempt to get networking hardware supported by the mainline Linux kernel, according to Phoronix.</p>
<p>Founded in 2021, Yunsilicon develops high-performance network adapters and data processing units (DPUs) — the smart-NIC-class accelerators that increasingly handle storage, security and virtualisation offload inside data-centre servers. Per Phoronix, the past month has been the company's first foray into kernel patch submission, with the aim of landing support for its networking silicon upstream.</p>
<p>Anyone assessing where the submission currently stands should check the LKML archives and the relevant subsystem trees directly, as review status on new-driver submissions can shift quickly between revision cycles. The vendor's "high performance" positioning should likewise be treated as a marketing claim pending third-party benchmarks.</p>
<p><strong>Why a driver submission matters</strong></p>
<p>For network engineers and systems administrators, a mainline driver is the practical difference between hardware that works out of the box with the distributions already running in production and hardware that depends on vendor-supplied out-of-tree modules. Out-of-tree drivers lag kernel releases, complicate security patching, and can break silently across upgrades — a maintenance liability in fleets where uptime and reproducible builds are non-negotiable.</p>
<p>For Hong Kong engineers sizing up new networking silicon, the same questions apply: multi-year support lifecycles and supply-chain continuity weigh as heavily as throughput figures. A new silicon contender seeking adoption in such environments generally needs a credible path to upstream support, not just competitive benchmark numbers.</p>
<p><strong>A crowded field</strong></p>
<p>Yunsilicon would be entering an established competitive landscape. NVIDIA (through its Mellanox acquisition) has long dominated high-performance NICs and DPUs with deep upstream kernel support. Intel, AMD (via Pensando) and Broadcom all ship adaptive and programmable networking silicon with varying degrees of mainline driver coverage. For a 2021-founded entrant, upstreaming early is arguably less about immediate market share and more about establishing legitimacy with the kernel community and the cloud and telco operators that follow its conventions.</p>
<p>Getting code merged into the kernel also signals interoperability commitment to buyers worldwide — a durable asset where hardware specs alone may not differentiate.</p>
<p><strong>What to watch</strong></p>
<p>Whether Yunsilicon's patches survive review is the open question. New network driver submissions routinely go through multiple revision cycles as maintainers scrutinise code quality, ABI stability and licensing. A first submission is a starting point rather than an acceptance.</p>
<p>For the open-source community, the story is a familiar one: another vendor discovering that mainline support is earned through sustained review participation, not announced at a launch event. Whether Yunsilicon commits to that long tail of maintenance — fixing regressions, rebasing across releases, responding to review feedback — will determine if its networking hardware becomes a routine option in Linux environments or a footnote.</p>
<p>According to Phoronix, the work is ongoing.</p>

<hr>
<p>據 Phoronix 報道，中國半導體初創企業雲矽智源（Yunsilicon）已開始向 Linux 內核郵件列表（LKML）發佈補丁，這是該公司首次嘗試讓其網絡硬件獲得 Linux 主線內核支援。</p>
<p>雲矽智源成立於 2021 年，開發高性能網絡適配器及數據處理單元（DPU）——即智能 NIC 級別的加速器，此類設備日益承擔數據中心服務器內的存儲、安全及 virtualisation 卸載工作。據 Phoronix 指出，過去一個月是該公司首次涉足 kernel patch 提交，目標是令其網絡芯片支援得以進入上游（upstream）。</p>
<p>任何人士如欲評估此項提交目前的進展，應直接查閱 LKML 存檔及相關 subsystem 的源碼樹，因為新驅動程式提交的審核狀態往往在多個 revision cycle 之間迅速轉變。該廠商「高性能」的定位同樣應視為市場宣傳說法，尚待第三方 benchmark 驗證。</p>
<p><strong>為何驅動程式提交如此重要</strong></p>
<p>對網絡工程師及系統管理員而言，主線驅動程式帶來的是實際差別：硬件能否在生產環境中現有的 Linux 發行版上即插即用，抑或必須依賴廠商提供的 out-of-tree 模組。Out-of-tree 驅動程式往往滯後於 kernel 發版，令安全修補工作更為複雜，並可能在升級過程中無聲失效——對於 uptime 及可重複構建（reproducible builds）不容妥協的機隊而言，這是一項維護負擔。</p>
<p>對香港工程師評估新網絡芯片而言，同樣的問題適用：多年支援週期及供應鏈延續性，與吞吐量數字同等重要。尋求在此類環境採用的新芯片競爭者，一般需要一條可信的上游支援路徑，而不僅是具競爭力的 benchmark 數字。</p>
<p><strong>群雄逐鹿</strong></p>
<p>雲矽智源將進入一個已然成熟的競爭格局。NVIDIA（透過收購 Mellanox）長期主導高性能 NIC 及 DPU 市場，具備深厚的上游 kernel 支援。Intel、AMD（透過 Pensando）及 Broadcom 均出貨可適應及可編程的網絡芯片，主線驅動程式覆蓋程度不一。對於一家 2021 年才成立的後進者而言，提早上游化與其說是為了即時市場份額，不如說是在 kernel 社群以及遵循其慣例的雲端和電訊營運商之間確立正當性。</p>
<p>代碼成功合併入 kernel，亦向全球買家釋出互操作性承諾——當硬件規格本身未必能構成差異化時，這是一項持久資產。</p>
<p><strong>值得留意之處</strong></p>
<p>雲矽智源的補丁能否通過審核，仍是懸而未決的問題。新的網絡驅動程式提交通常要經歷多個 revision cycle，由維護者仔細檢視代碼質量、ABI 穩定性及授權條款。首次提交只是一個起點，而非獲接納。</p>
<p>對開源社群而言，這是屢見不鮮的故事：又一家廠商發現，主線支援是透過持續參與審核換取的，而非在發布會上宣布便能取得。雲矽智源是否願意投入這條漫長的維護長尾——修復回歸問題（regression）、跨版本 rebase、回應審核意見——將決定其網絡硬件會否成為 Linux 環境中的常規選項，抑或只屬一則註腳。</p>
<p>據 Phoronix 報道，相關工作仍在進行中。</p>
