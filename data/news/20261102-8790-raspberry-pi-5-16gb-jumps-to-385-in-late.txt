Title: Raspberry Pi 5 16GB Jumps To $385 In Latest Round Of Memory-Driven Price Hikes
Content:
<p>The 16GB Raspberry Pi 5 now carries a $385 price tag, according to Phoronix, which ties the increase to the RAM shortage and elevated memory pricing that continue to work their way through the hardware industry. It is the latest in a series of price increases to hit the single-board computer maker as memory costs climb.</p>
<p>One caveat matters here: the report does not break out whether the $385 figure reflects an official list-price revision or a distributor-side adjustment. Buyers weighing a purchase should confirm the current number against Raspberry Pi's own price list before treating it as the manufacturer's recommended figure.</p>
<p><strong>Why the top-end model is most exposed</strong></p>
<p>Memory costs have climbed sharply, with demand from AI datacentre build-outs widely cited as a major driver of global DRAM tightness. For a board like the Pi 5, which carries its memory soldered onto the PCB rather than in a user-replaceable slot, there is little room to absorb that cost elsewhere in the bill of materials. The 16GB configuration, holding the largest memory footprint in the lineup, is correspondingly the most exposed to per-gigabyte price movement.</p>
<p>Vendors across the industry have been passing memory costs downstream, and the Pi pricing move appears to be a continuation of that pattern rather than an isolated adjustment.</p>
<p><strong>Why it matters beyond the sticker price</strong></p>
<p>For hobbyists, the hike is an annoyance. For others, it is a budgeting problem. Prototyping fleets that previously specified a dozen boards for bench testing now face a materially different cost curve. Educational institutions and nonprofit programmes, historically among the Pi's most price-sensitive buyers, plan around per-unit costs that have traditionally sat well under $100 for lower configurations.</p>
<p>Embedded vendors that model the Pi as a cheap development and deployment platform will also need to revisit assumptions in their bills of materials. And at $385, the gap between a top-tier Pi and a refurbished x86 mini-PC narrows to the point where the classic "cheapest capable Linux machine" argument starts to shift.</p>
<p><strong>What is not yet known</strong></p>
<p>The report as published covers the 16GB model only. There is no word on whether the price movement extends to lower Pi 5 tiers, the Pi 4 family, or the Pico line. Nor is it clear whether the $385 figure is a temporary response to spot-market memory pricing or a new baseline that will hold once supply and demand normalise. Both questions are worth revisiting if Raspberry Pi issues a broader statement or if Phoronix publishes a follow-up.</p>
<p><strong>What buyers should weigh</strong></p>
<p>There is no definitive answer while the direction of DRAM pricing remains uncertain, and readers should treat any specific conversion or street price cautiously — retail totals vary by region, tax treatment, and sales channel. Checking current listings before committing to a purchase is advisable, and official pricing can be confirmed directly through Raspberry Pi's own site.</p>
<p>What is no longer abstract is the trajectory. The memory shortage that has dominated DRAM market reporting for months is now visible on hobbyist price tags — a signal that even the most cost-disciplined corner of the hardware world is not insulated from the AI build-out's demand for chips.</p>

<hr>
<p>據 Phoronix 報導，16GB 版 Raspberry Pi 5 現售價為 $385，報道將此次加價歸因於持續影響硬件行業的 RAM 短缺及高企的記憶體價格。這是這家單板電腦製造商在記憶體成本攀升背景下，最新一次遭遇的加價。</p>
<p>有一點值得注意：原報告並未說明 $385 究竟屬於官方建議售價的修訂，還是分銷商層面的價格調整。有意購買的消費者，應先對照 Raspberry Pi 官方價目表核實現時數字，再將其視為廠商建議售價。</p>
<p><strong>為何頂配型號最受影響</strong></p>
<p>記憶體成本急劇上升，人工智能數據中心擴建所帶來的需求被廣泛視為全球 DRAM 供應緊張的主要推手。對於 Raspberry Pi 5 這類將記憶體直接焊接在 PCB 上、而非採用用戶可更換插槽的主機板而言，成本壓力幾乎無法在物料清單的其他項目中消化。16GB 配置是整個系列中記憶體容量最大的型號，因此對每 GB 價格波動也最為敏感。</p>
<p>業界各供應商一直將記憶體成本向下游傳導，Raspberry Pi 的此次加價看來是這一趨勢的延續，而非孤立的個別調整。</p>
<p><strong>為何影響不止於標價</strong></p>
<p>對業餘愛好者而言，加價只是麻煩；對其他用戶而言，則是預算問題。以往在實驗台測試中選用十二塊主機板的原型開發團隊，如今面對截然不同的成本曲線。教育機構及非牟利項目歷來是 Raspberry Pi 對價格最敏感的買家群體，其規劃一向以單位成本為基礎，而較低配置的售價長期以來遠低於 $100。</p>
<p>將 Raspberry Pi 作為低成本開發及部署平台的嵌入式系統供應商，同樣需要重新審視其物料清單中的假設。而在 $385 的價位上，頂級 Pi 與一部翻新 x86 mini-PC 之間的差距已大幅收窄，以至「最便宜的可用 Linux 機器」這一經典論點開始動搖。</p>
<p><strong>尚不明朗之處</strong></p>
<p>已刊出的報告只涵蓋 16GB 型號。至於此次價格調整是否延伸至 Pi 5 較低配置、Pi 4 系列或 Pico 系列，則未有消息。同樣不明確的是，$385 究竟是因應現貨市場記憶體價格的暫時反應，還是在供求恢復正常後仍會維持的新基準。若 Raspberry Pi 發表更全面的聲明，或 Phoronix 刊出後續報導，這兩個問題都值得再作跟進。</p>
<p><strong>買家應權衡什麼</strong></p>
<p>在 DRAM 價格走勢仍不明朗之時，並無定論，讀者對任何具體換算價或市面零售價應審慎看待——零售總價會因地區、稅務處理及銷售渠道而異。建議在落實購買前查閱最新報價，官方定價則可直接透過 Raspberry Pi 官方網站核實。</p>
<p>不再抽象的是這條走勢。數月來主導 DRAM 市場報導的記憶體短缺，如今已反映在業餘愛好者產品的價牌上——這顯示，即使硬件世界中成本控制最嚴謹的角落，也無法完全避開人工智能擴建浪潮對芯片的需求。</p>
