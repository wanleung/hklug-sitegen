Title: Debian Opens General Resolution on LLM-Generated Code
Content:
<p>The Debian Project has initiated a general resolution to establish policy on the use of large language models (LLMs) in creating software for the distribution. The process, reported by LWN.net, is now in its discussion period, though a start date for formal voting has not yet been announced.</p>
<p>The resolution presents three alternatives for consideration. The first would impose a total ban on the use of LLMs. The second would reject LLM-assisted contributions "as far as practical." The third would explicitly permit such contributions, subject to a set of conditions whose specifics have not yet been detailed in the resolution text.</p>
<p>Debian general resolutions are the project's most formal mechanism for community decision-making on matters of project governance. The process follows a structured timeline that includes a discussion period, a voting period, and a formal result.</p>
<p>The resolution is open for community discussion. The full text and current status of the vote can be found on the Debian website.</p>
<hr>
<p>Debian 項目已啟動一項一般決議，以制定在發行版中使用大型語言模型（LLMs）創建軟件的政策。據 LWN.net 報導，該程序現正處於討論期，但正式投票的開始日期尚未公佈。</p>
<p>該決議提出了三項供考慮的替代方案。第一項是全面禁止使用 LLMs。第二項是「在實際可行範圍內」拒絕 LLM 輔助的貢獻。第三項是明確允許此類貢獻，但需滿足一系列條件，這些條件的具體細節尚未在決議文本中詳述。</p>
<p>Debian 的一般決議是項目在項目治理事項上社區決策的最正式機制。該程序遵循結構化的時間表，包括討論期、投票期和正式結果。</p>
<p>該決議向社區討論開放。完整文本和投票的最新狀態可在 Debian 網站上找到。</p>
