<h3>Linux 7.3 Set for Sunday Release: Valve's Upstream Influence Continues to Shape the Kernel</h3>
<p><em>Editor's note: The release date and Valve-focused framing below are drawn from Phoronix's published round-up and accompanying summary. The subsystem-level analysis that follows reflects HKLUG's own reading of Valve's historical upstream contributions and should be treated as editorial context, not direct source claims. Publication is contingent on confirmation of the 18 October release date against kernel.org.</em></p>
<p>The Linux 7.3 kernel is expected to be officially released this Sunday, 18 October, barring any last-minute blocking issues. Phoronix has published a preview round-up of the most notable changes in this release, with Valve's contributions prominently featured. For Hong Kong systems administration, DevOps, and embedded teams, the underlying story extends well beyond gaming: engineering originally developed for the Steam Deck continues to flow upstream into mainline Linux, with effects felt across the wider ecosystem.</p>
<h4>Valve's Evolving Role as an Upstream Contributor</h4>
<p>Valve has undergone a notable transformation over the past several years — from a company publicly supportive of Linux gaming into a routine, high-volume upstream contributor to the Linux kernel. Phoronix's coverage of 7.3 reflects this shift, with the company cited frequently across the round-up.</p>
<p>While the specific subsystems covered in the release have not been individually enumerated in the published summary, Valve's historical pattern of upstream investment suggests several areas where 7.3 changes may prove broadly relevant. Based on the company's documented focus areas for Steam Deck development, the following subsystems warrant attention from operators — this is HKLUG's analysis, not a direct attribution to Phoronix:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>GPU scheduling (DRM / GPU scheduler):</strong> Low-latency scheduling work developed for handheld AMD GPUs has historically benefited desktop and workstation use cases, including render nodes and VDI thin clients.</li>
<li><strong>Memory management:</strong> Page and reclaim optimisations aimed at low-memory, battery-powered devices can extend to embedded systems, digital signage, and edge appliances.</li>
<li><strong>Power-state transitions:</strong> Runtime power management work at the GPU and platform level may translate into battery-life and thermal improvements across form factors.</li>
<li><strong>Input latency:</strong> Latency reductions designed for the Steam Deck's touchpads and controllers have potential applicability to thin clients, POS terminals, and interactive kiosks.</li>
</ul>
<p>The broader point stands: return on some of Valve's upstream investment has long outgrown the Steam Deck itself.</p>
<h4>Performance Work Across Multiple Layers</h4>
<p>Per Phoronix, performance improvements are a defining theme of 7.3, spanning work at the scheduler, filesystem, and driver layers. Specific benchmark figures have not been included in the published round-up; teams evaluating the release should validate performance against their own workloads once 7.3 ships.</p>
<h4>Upgrade Considerations for Hong Kong IT Teams</h4>
<p>A new kernel release marks the start of an upgrade <em>decision</em>, not an upgrade <em>deadline</em>. Systems teams evaluating 7.3 may wish to keep the following in mind:</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Avoid day-one deployment.</strong> Waiting for the subsequent point releases (7.3.1, 7.3.2) allows most obvious regressions to surface and be resolved within the first few weeks.</li>
<li><strong>Validate against your own telemetry.</strong> Changes to GPU scheduling and power management are likely to have the most direct impact on AMD-GPU fleets; operators running the NVIDIA proprietary driver may see limited effect from certain subsystem-level changes, though scheduler and memory-management updates will still apply.</li>
<li><strong>Follow standard change-management practice.</strong> Staging soak, canary fleet deployment, and a rollback plan remain essential.</li>
</ol>
<p>Should the release schedule slip or blocking issues surface before 18 October, we will follow up with an update.</p>
<h4>Conclusion</h4>
<p>The defining story of Linux 7.3 is not a single feature, but the continued pattern of well-resourced companies like Valve reinvesting engineering effort into mainline — a trend that has been among the most consequential for the Linux kernel ecosystem over the past decade. The final release date still awaits formal confirmation from Linus Torvalds. In the meantime, Hong Kong IT teams can begin the practical work of scoping validation: inventorying AMD-GPU and embedded fleets, scheduling soak tests, and preparing to deploy as the first point releases land.</p>
<p><em>(Source: Phoronix, mid-October 2026 round-up; official release date subject to kernel.org announcement.)</em></p>

<h3>Linux 7.3 定於週日發佈：Valve 上游影響力持續塑造 kernel 發展</h3>
<p><em>編者按：以下發佈日期及以 Valve 為主軸的論述，取自 Phoronix 已發表的 round-up 及相關摘要。其後的 subsystem 層面分析，反映的是 HKLUG 對 Valve 過往上游貢獻的自身解讀，應視為編輯背景分析，而非直接引用來源的陳述。本文是否發佈，須以 kernel.org 確認 10 月 18 日發佈日期為前提。</em></p>
<p>Linux 7.3 kernel 預計於本週日（10 月 18 日）正式發佈，前提是發佈前夕不會出現阻礙性問題。Phoronix 已發表 round-up，預覽此次發佈中最值得注意的改動，當中 Valve 的貢獻佔有顯著篇幅。對香港系統管理、DevOps 及 embedded 團隊而言，背後的故事遠超遊戲範疇：當初為 Steam Deck 開發的工程正持續 upstream 至 mainline Linux，其影響遍及整個生態圈。</p>
<h4>Valve 作為上游貢獻者的角色演變</h4>
<p>過去數年，Valve 經歷了顯著轉型——由一家公開支持 Linux gaming 的公司，蛻變為 Linux kernel 常規且高產出的 upstream contributor。Phoronix 對 7.3 的報導反映了這一轉變，該公司在 round-up 中被多次引述。</p>
<p>雖然已發表的摘要未逐項列舉此次發佈所涵蓋的具體 subsystem，但按 Valve 過往的上游投資模式推斷，7.3 的部分改動很可能在多個範疇具廣泛參考價值。根據該公司有記載的 Steam Deck 開發重點，以下 subsystem 值得業者留意——這是 HKLUG 的分析，並非直接引述 Phoronix：</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>GPU 調度（DRM／GPU scheduler）</strong>：為手持裝置 AMD GPU 開發的低延遲調度工作，歷來對桌面及 workstation 使用場景有所裨益，包括 render node 及 VDI thin client。</li>
<li><strong>Memory management</strong>：針對小記憶體、電池供電裝置的 page 與 reclaim 優化，可延伸至 embedded 系統、digital signage 及 edge appliance。</li>
<li><strong>電源狀態切換</strong>：GPU 與 platform 層級的 runtime power management 工作，或可轉化為不同裝置形態的電池續航與散熱改善。</li>
<li><strong>輸入延遲</strong>：為 Steam Deck 觸控板與 controller 設計的延遲優化，對 thin client、POS terminal 及互動資訊亭具潛在適用性。</li>
</ul>
<p>更宏觀的要點依然成立：Valve 部分上游投資的回報早已不限於 Steam Deck 本身。</p>
<h4>效能改進貫穿多個層面</h4>
<p>據 Phoronix 報導，效能提升是 7.3 的核心主題之一，涵蓋 scheduler、檔案系統及 driver 層面的工作。已發表的 round-up 未包含具體基準測試數據；評估此發佈的團隊，應待 7.3 正式發佈後，按自身 workload 進行效能驗證。</p>
<h4>香港 IT 團隊的升級考量</h4>
<p>kernel 推出新版本是 upgrade <em>decision</em> 的起點，而非 upgrade <em>deadline</em>。評估 7.3 的系統管理團隊，或可留意以下幾點：</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>避免首日部署</strong>。等待後續 point release（7.3.1、7.3.2）發佈，可讓多數明顯的 regression 在最初數週內浮現並獲解決。</li>
<li><strong>以自身 telemetry 驗證</strong>。GPU 調度及 power management 的改動，對 AMD GPU fleet 的影響最為直接；採用 NVIDIA 專有驅動的業者，某些 subsystem 層面的改動影響可能有限，但 scheduler 與 memory management 的更新仍會適用。</li>
<li><strong>遵循標準 change management 流程</strong>。staging 環境靜置評估、canary fleet 分批部署及 rollback plan，仍然不可或缺。</li>
</ol>
<p>若發佈日程出現延後，或於 10 月 18 日前出現 blocker，本站將持續跟進並作更新。</p>
<h4>總結</h4>
<p>Linux 7.3 的主線並非單一 feature，而是 Valve 這類擁有充足資源的公司持續將工程投入回饋至 mainline 的模式——此趨勢在過去十年間，一直是 Linux kernel 生態中最具深遠影響的力量之一。最終發佈日期仍待 Linus Torvalds 正式確認。與此同時，香港 IT 團隊已可開始著手規劃驗證工作：盤點 AMD GPU 與 embedded fleet、安排 soak test，並為首個 point release 發佈後的部署作好準備。</p>
